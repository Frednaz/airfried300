There is no virtuous reason to write a hot dog recipe. The recipe is:
open the package, heat the hot dog. But the air fryer does something
the microwave doesn't and the boiling pot definitely doesn't — it
splits the skin lengthwise, gives you those slightly burnt edges that
the rest of the cooking world calls "good char," and toasts the bun on
top of itself in the same five-minute window.

Which is to say: yes, this is just hot dogs. But it's better hot dogs,
in less time, with one less pan. The photo is what you're going to make:
ketchup and mustard in zigzag, relish underneath, the bun slightly
toasted, side of plain potato chips for crunch. (The chips are not
optional. They are the recipe.)

A note on the dog itself: this works for everything — beef, pork, all-
beef kosher, the cheap ones, the fancy ones, the chicken sausages
pretending to be hot dogs, the plant-based ones. Different brands cook
in slightly different times — start checking at four minutes. You want
the skin just split, not burst entirely.

## Ingredients

- 4 hot dogs (any kind)
- 4 hot dog buns
- Yellow mustard
- Ketchup
- Sweet pickle relish (or chopped pickles)
- 4 oz potato chips, to serve

## Method

### 01. Score and load.
Optional but worth it: score each hot dog with three or four shallow
diagonal cuts. Place in the air fryer basket in a single layer.

### 02. First fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Cook the hot dogs for 4–5 minutes,
until the skins have split slightly along the cuts and the edges are
just starting to char.

### 03. Toast the buns.
Open the buns and place them cut-side-up on top of the hot dogs (or off
to the side if your basket is big enough). Cook 1–2 minutes more, until
the buns are toasted golden inside.

### 04. Assemble.
Slide each dog into a bun. Lay down a stripe of relish, then zigzag
mustard over the top, then ketchup over that. Serve with a pile of
chips on the side.

## Notes

For Chicago-style: skip the ketchup (offensive to Chicagoans), add a
dill pickle spear, chopped onion, sport peppers, tomato, and celery
salt. For a chili dog: spoon ¼ cup of warmed chili over the top
before the cheese (sharp cheddar, in this case). For the bacon-wrapped
version: wrap each dog in a strip of bacon before air-frying, add 3
minutes to the time, and brush with maple syrup in the last minute if
you want the lacquered-glaze look.
