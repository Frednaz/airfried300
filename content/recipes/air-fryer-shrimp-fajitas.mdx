This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Shrimp, peppers, and onions with smoky fajita seasoning, cooked fast enough that dinner feels like a trick.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Tortillas and lime, for serving

## Method

### 01. Start vegetables.
Toss peppers and onion with oil and spices. Air fry at 390°F for 7 minutes.

### 02. Add shrimp.
Add shrimp and cook 5–6 minutes more, shaking once, until pink.

### 03. Warm tortillas.
Warm tortillas separately or briefly in the basket.

### 04. Serve.
Squeeze lime over everything and pile into tortillas.

## Notes

Shrimp cook quickly; add them late or they will tighten up.
