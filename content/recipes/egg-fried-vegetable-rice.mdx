Egg-fried rice is the dish where leftover rice goes to prove
itself. Fresh rice steams; day-old rice fries. The starch on the
outside of each grain dries out overnight, and that dried surface
is what allows the rice to take on the lacquered finish and
slight chew that turns a side into a meal.

The rule is therefore: never make this with rice you just cooked.
Cook it the night before, spread it on a tray, refrigerate
uncovered. Or buy a pouch of microwave rice and tip it into the
fridge for an hour before using — same effect, faster. Hot
freshly-steamed rice will steam in the pan and turn to mush.

The technique is wok-style stir-frying: very high heat, fast
movement, ingredients added in stages. Eggs first (scrambled
and pushed aside), vegetables next (carrots, red cabbage,
mangetout — anything crunchy), rice last with the soy sauce.
Total cook time is 8 minutes once the pan is hot.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups (500g) cooked day-old jasmine rice, broken up
- 3 large eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (sunflower or rapeseed)
- 1 large carrot, peeled into thin ribbons with a vegetable
  peeler
- 1½ cups (130g) shredded red cabbage
- 1 cup (130g) mangetout, halved on the bias (or sugar snap peas)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, finely grated
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 4 spring onions, finely sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 1 fresh red chilli, finely sliced (deseeded for less heat)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method

### 01. Prep everything first.
Stir-frying moves fast — get the rice broken up, the vegetables
chopped, the soy sauces measured before you turn on the heat.

### 02. Scramble the eggs.
Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high.
Pour in the beaten eggs, scramble gently with a spatula for 90
seconds until just set but still glossy. Tip out onto a plate.

### 03. Stir-fry the aromatics.
Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to the same pan over high heat.
Add garlic, ginger, and spring onion whites. Stir-fry 30 seconds
until fragrant.

### 04. Add the vegetables.
Add carrot ribbons, red cabbage, and mangetout. Stir-fry hard
for 2 minutes — vegetables should be crisp-tender, not soft.

### 05. Add the rice.
Tip in the day-old rice and break up any clumps with the spatula.
Stir-fry 2–3 minutes, pressing the rice against the hot pan
between stirs, until the grains separate and start to crackle.

### 06. Sauce.
Drizzle in both soy sauces, sesame oil, white pepper, and rice
vinegar. Toss for 60 seconds until evenly coated.

### 07. Fold in the egg.
Return the scrambled egg to the pan, breaking it into smaller
pieces with the spatula. Toss for 30 seconds to combine.

### 08. Plate.
Divide between two bowls. Top with spring onion greens, sliced
chilli, sesame seeds. Serve lime wedges on the side.

## Notes

For a complete meal, top with leftover roast chicken (shredded)
or pan-fried prawns. For the truly classic Chinese-takeaway
version, add ½ cup of frozen peas (defrosted) with the
vegetables. The white pepper is non-optional — it's what gives
takeaway fried rice its distinctive savoury kick. For
gluten-free, swap soy for tamari. Don't skip the rice-pressing
step — it's what gives every grain that lacquered, slightly
crisp surface.
