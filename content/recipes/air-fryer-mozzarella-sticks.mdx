The mozzarella stick is a hard appetizer to make well at home
because of one specific failure mode: cheese leakage. If the
breading isn't thick enough, or the cheese isn't cold enough, or
the cooking time is even slightly too long, the mozzarella melts
faster than the crust can hold it, and you end up with a basket of
empty breadcrumb tubes and a layer of welded cheese on the floor of
your air fryer. Years of frustration.

The fix is two-part. First, the **double dredge**: each stick gets
flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, then egg AGAIN, then breadcrumbs
AGAIN. This builds a thick, rugged crust that holds the cheese in
during cooking. Second, the **freezer step**: the breaded sticks
must be frozen solid (at least 45 minutes, ideally 2+ hours) before
they hit the air fryer. The cold core gives the crust time to
crisp and brown before the cheese melts.

The photo shows the right finish: sticks lined up in a red-checked-
paper-lined basket with a small black bowl of marinara dipping
sauce in the middle, parsley scattered, one stick broken open at
the bottom-right showing the molten cheese pulling apart. The
breading is craggy-golden, not pale; the cheese is melted but
contained.

Use string cheese (the snack-pack kind) — it's the right shape and
size and crucially uses low-moisture mozzarella which holds
together better than fresh.

## Ingredients

- 12 string cheese sticks (low-moisture mozzarella), unwrapped
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1½ cups (150g) panko breadcrumbs
- ⅓ cup (35g) grated parmesan
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Olive oil spray
- Fresh parsley, chopped (to garnish)

**For the marinara dip:**
- 1 cup good marinara (jarred is fine)
- 2 tbsp fresh basil, torn (optional)
- A pinch of red pepper flakes

## Method

### 01. Set up dredging stations.
Three bowls. Bowl 1: flour. Bowl 2: beaten eggs. Bowl 3: panko +
parmesan + Italian seasoning + garlic powder + salt + red pepper
flakes, stirred together.

### 02. Double-dredge.
Working one cheese stick at a time: flour, egg, breadcrumbs, then
egg AGAIN, then breadcrumbs AGAIN. Press the breading firmly to
ensure full coverage. Set on a plate as you go.

### 03. Freeze.
Place the plate of breaded sticks in the freezer for 45 minutes
minimum, 2 hours preferred. They should feel solid to the touch.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray the frozen sticks with
olive oil. Place in the basket in a single layer, not touching.
Cook 5–7 minutes, flipping at the 3-minute mark — pull as soon as
the crust is deep golden. **Do not over-cook** or you'll get
cheese leaks.

### 05. Warm the marinara.
While the sticks cook, warm the marinara in a small saucepan over
medium-low. Stir in fresh basil and red pepper flakes if using.

### 06. Plate and serve.
Move sticks to a basket or platter. Scatter parsley. Serve
immediately with warm marinara on the side. Eat within 5 minutes
— the cheese sets up as they cool.

## Notes

**Freeze ahead**: the breaded sticks freeze beautifully for a
month. Make a big batch, freeze on a sheet pan until solid, then
bag. Cook from frozen, no need to thaw, same cooking time. **Why
the basket-bottom welded-cheese disaster happens**: insufficient
freezing OR overcooking. Both are easily prevented. For a spicier
version, swap red pepper flakes for cayenne in the breading. For
gluten-free, use gluten-free panko (same technique). Watch the
sticks closely the first time — your air fryer's actual temperature
may run hot or cool, so calibrate based on the colour of the crust.
