The chicken kiev — pounded chicken breast wrapped around herb butter,
breaded and fried, butter erupting when sliced — is a Russian-by-way-
of-1970s-dinner-party classic. This version applies the same logic to
mashed potato: thick mash wrapped around a frozen disc of herb-garlic
butter, rolled into a ball, breaded, air-fried.

The technique requires freezing the butter discs first (they need
to stay solid until the inside of the potato heats up). Make herb
butter (parsley, chives, garlic, lemon zest), shape into 4 discs,
freeze 30 minutes. Wrap each disc in seasoned mashed potato — like
arancini construction. Bread, air-fry at 200°C for 12 minutes.

The photo shows three balls on white plates: panko-golden round
dumplings, one split open showing molten green-flecked butter
pouring out, white potato around the edges. The kind of dish that
makes everyone pause when you cut into it.

## Ingredients

**For the herb butter:**
- ½ cup (115g) salted butter, softened
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For the potato:**
- 2 lb (900g) Maris Piper or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp double cream
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp white pepper

**For breading:**
- ½ cup plain flour
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1½ cups (130g) panko breadcrumbs
- Olive oil spray

## Method

### 01. Make the herb butter.
Mash butter with garlic, parsley, chives, lemon zest, salt, and
pepper. Divide into 4 portions, shape each into a thick disc on a
piece of parchment. Freeze at least 30 minutes (until rock-hard).

### 02. Cook and mash.
Boil potatoes in salted water until very tender (15 minutes). Drain
well. Mash with butter, cream, egg yolk, salt, and white pepper
until smooth and very thick. Cool to room temperature.

### 03. Shape.
Divide mash into 4 portions. Working with one at a time: flatten the
mash into a 5-inch round, place a frozen butter disc in the centre,
fold mash around it and seal completely. Roll into a smooth ball.

### 04. Chill.
Refrigerate balls 15 minutes — they need to be firm before breading.

### 05. Bread.
Set up flour, beaten egg, and panko. Coat each ball in flour, egg,
then panko, pressing firmly.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray balls generously with
oil. Cook 12–14 minutes, turning gently halfway, until deeply
golden.

### 07. Serve.
Plate each on a small plate. Cut into the centre — butter should
erupt. Scatter extra chives. Serve immediately.

## Notes

The butter must be frozen solid before wrapping, or it melts into
the potato during cooking and you lose the eruption effect. Best
served with steamed greens (tenderstem broccoli or beans) — the
butter is rich on its own. Make ahead through step 5: refrigerate
up to 24 hours, cook from chilled (add 2 minutes to cook time).
