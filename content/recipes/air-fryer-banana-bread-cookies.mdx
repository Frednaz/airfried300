Two bananas you forgot about, turning black on the counter. A half-eaten
bag of rolled oats from when you were going to make granola in January
and didn't. Some chocolate chips that have started to bloom white at
the edges of the bag. This is a recipe in the strict sense — it has
ingredients and instructions and a temperature — but it's really just a
permission slip to clear your pantry without feeling like you've thrown
food away.

The cookie itself is the texture of a soft granola bar that fell into a
hot pan: dense, slightly cakey, oat-flecked, with little molten chocolate
spots from the chips. It's not crunchy. It's not supposed to be. You eat
it warm from the air fryer, ideally standing up, with a cup of coffee
that's slightly too hot. There's enough banana that you don't need to
add sugar; there's enough peanut butter that you don't need to add fat.
There's just enough chocolate that you don't accidentally feel virtuous.

You can make these in a sheet-pan version in the oven — same recipe,
350°F for 12 minutes — but the air fryer is faster, and the dry heat
gives the edges a slightly chewier crust. They're the size of a large
muffin top, four to a batch. They don't last long.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium ripe bananas, mashed (about ¾ cup)
- 1 cup rolled oats (not instant, not steel-cut)
- 2 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt
- ⅓ cup mini chocolate chips (semi-sweet)

## Method

### 01. Mash.
Mash the bananas in a medium bowl until mostly smooth — a few lumps
are fine. The riper the banana, the sweeter the cookie.

### 02. Mix.
Stir in the peanut butter, maple syrup, and vanilla until combined.
Add the oats, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt and stir until you
have a thick, lumpy batter. Fold in the chocolate chips.

### 03. Portion.
Cut a square of parchment to fit your air fryer basket. Scoop the
batter into four equal portions onto the parchment, leaving space
between (about ⅓ cup each). Flatten gently with the back of a spoon
to about ¾-inch thick — they don't spread much.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 325°F (165°C). Slide the parchment into the
basket and cook for 9–11 minutes, until the tops are set and the
edges are turning golden.

### 05. Cool briefly.
Lift the parchment out and let the cookies rest on it for 5 minutes
before peeling them off — they firm up as they cool.

## Notes

These keep in an airtight container at room temp for 2 days, or 4 days
in the fridge. They freeze well — wrap individually, microwave for 30
seconds to reheat. Swap the chocolate chips for chopped walnuts or
raisins if you want the breakfast version. Swap the maple syrup for
honey if that's what you have.
