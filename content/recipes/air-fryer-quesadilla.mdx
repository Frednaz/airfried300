This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. A crisp flour tortilla with molten cheese inside, made flat and tidy in the air fryer.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 2 large flour tortillas
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Monterey Jack or cheddar
- 1/2 cup cooked chicken or beans, optional
- 2 tbsp salsa
- Cooking spray
- Guacamole and sour cream, for serving

## Method

### 01. Fill.
Layer cheese and optional chicken or beans over half of each tortilla. Add a little salsa and fold.

### 02. Pin.
Secure with toothpicks if the tortilla wants to fly open.

### 03. Air fry.
Mist lightly and cook at 375°F for 5–7 minutes, flipping once.

### 04. Slice.
Rest 1 minute, remove toothpicks, and cut into wedges.

## Notes

Do not overfill. A quesadilla should be generous, not structurally doomed.
