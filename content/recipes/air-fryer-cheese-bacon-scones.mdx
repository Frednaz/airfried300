The savoury scone is the underloved cousin of the sweet jam-and-
cream scone. Same fundamental technique (cold butter rubbed into
self-raising flour, milk to bring together), different filling —
crisp bacon, sharp cheddar, chives, and a pinch of mustard powder
for warmth. The result is a tender breakfast biscuit halfway between
a scone and a pull-apart cheese bread.

The air fryer makes these straightforward. The bacon crisps in the
basket first (8 minutes), gets crumbled into the dough. The dough
mixes in five minutes, pat into rounds, more bacon on top, air-fry
at 200°C for 12 minutes.

twelve minutes hands-on.

## Ingredients

**For the bacon:**
- 6 slices streaky bacon

**For the scones:**
- 2½ cups (375g) self-raising flour
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp mustard powder (optional but worth it)
- ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)
- ½ cup (115g) cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 cup (110g) grated cheddar
- 3 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- 1 cup (250ml) buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

**For finishing:**
- 1 tbsp milk (for brushing)
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- Extra crumbled bacon (reserved from above)

## Method

### 01. Cook the bacon.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Air-fry bacon strips 7–8
minutes until crisp. Drain on paper towels, crumble. Reserve 2 tbsp
for the tops; the rest goes in the dough.

### 02. Rub in butter.
In a bowl, whisk flour, salt, mustard powder, and cayenne. Add
butter, rub in with fingertips until coarse-breadcrumb consistency.
Stir in cheddar, chives, and most of the crumbled bacon.

### 03. Add wet.
Whisk buttermilk, egg, and Dijon. Pour into dry, stir with a knife
just until a shaggy dough forms.

### 04. Shape.
Tip onto a floured surface. Pat into a 1-inch-thick rectangle. Cut
8 squares (or use a 2½-inch round cutter).

### 05. Top.
Place on parchment. Brush tops with milk. Sprinkle parmesan and
reserved bacon crumbles over each.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook in a single layer 12–14
minutes, until deeply golden.

### 07. Serve warm.
Move to a wire rack, rest 3 minutes, serve warm with butter.

## Notes

For a ham version: swap bacon for finely diced honey ham (no
pre-cooking needed). For a herbier version: add 2 tbsp chopped
parsley + 1 tbsp thyme leaves. Don't overwork the dough — gluten
development makes tough scones. Best within 4 hours of baking;
reheat in the air fryer for 3 minutes at 350°F.
