This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Mozzarella and pepperoni tucked into dough strips, air-fried until golden, then dragged through warm marinara.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 tube refrigerated pizza dough
- 24 pepperoni slices
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- Warm marinara, for dipping

## Method

### 01. Cut dough.
Roll dough into a rectangle and cut into 12 strips.

### 02. Fill.
Add pepperoni and mozzarella to one end of each strip, then fold and pinch closed.

### 03. Season.
Brush lightly with oil and sprinkle with garlic powder and Italian seasoning.

### 04. Air fry.
Cook at 375°F for 7–9 minutes, turning once, until puffed and golden. Serve with marinara.

## Notes

Pinch the seams harder than seems necessary; melted cheese is always looking for an exit.
