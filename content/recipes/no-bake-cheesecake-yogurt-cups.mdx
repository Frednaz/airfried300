The no-bake cheesecake is a small culinary loophole. The full
cheesecake — the one that requires a water bath, an hour at 325°F, and
an overnight rest — is a project. The no-bake version is twenty
minutes and tastes about 85% as good, which is a remarkable percentage
for a recipe that requires no oven. The trick is that whipped cream
cheese + Greek yogurt + a little sugar approximates the texture of
cheesecake closely enough to fool most people, and the graham cracker
crumb base does the rest of the heavy lifting.

These mason-jar versions are the meal-prep adaptation. Make four on
Sunday, eat one a day through Thursday. The structure is: graham
cracker crumb pressed into the bottom (the crust), a generous layer of
sweetened cream-cheese-and-yogurt mixture (the filling), fresh berries
on top (the garnish that makes everything look intentional).

The photo shows the cross-section perfectly: golden crust layer at the
bottom, fluffy white filling in the middle, a generous handful of mixed
berries (raspberries, blackberries, blueberries, halved strawberries)
piled on top. The mason jar is the right size — about ½ cup
capacity — which is a satisfying portion that doesn't get into "I ate
a whole cheesecake" territory.

## Ingredients

**For the crust:**
- 6 graham cracker squares, finely crushed (about ½ cup crumbs)
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar
- Pinch of cinnamon

**For the cheesecake filling:**
- 8 oz cream cheese (1 block), softened to room temperature
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt (full-fat for richness)
- ¼ cup granulated sugar (or 3 tbsp honey)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp lemon juice

**For topping (per jar):**
- ¼ cup mixed berries (raspberries, blackberries, blueberries,
  quartered strawberries)

## Method

### 01. Make the crust.
In a bowl, mix the crushed graham crackers with melted butter, sugar,
and cinnamon until the crumbs hold together when pinched.

### 02. Press into jars.
Divide the crumb mixture evenly among 4 small mason jars (½-cup
capacity). Press down firmly with the back of a spoon to form a
compact crust about ¼-inch thick.

### 03. Make the filling.
In a bowl, beat the softened cream cheese with a hand mixer (or by
hand with a strong arm) until smooth and lump-free. Add the Greek
yogurt, sugar, vanilla, and lemon juice. Beat until uniform and
fluffy — about 1 minute.

### 04. Layer the filling.
Spoon the filling over the crust, dividing evenly among the four jars.
Smooth the tops with the back of a spoon. Tap each jar gently on the
counter to settle.

### 05. Top with berries.
Pile mixed berries on top of each jar, mounded slightly.

### 06. Chill.
Cover with lids and refrigerate at least 1 hour (preferably 4) to let
the filling set and the crust soften slightly. Keeps 5 days.

## Notes

The crust will soften over the week from absorbing moisture — for
maximum crunch, keep the crust separate and add it just before
eating (one of those "advanced meal-prep" tips that's actually worth
the trouble). Variations: swap berries for sliced peaches, drizzle
with honey, top with toasted coconut, add lemon zest to the filling.
For a smaller serving, use 8 oz jars and make 6 instead of 4. For a
holiday version, layer pumpkin puree (with cinnamon and nutmeg) into
the filling instead of berries on top.
