This is the salad that proves "salad" doesn't have to mean
"sad lunchtime concession." It's structured around two
air-fried elements (maple-glazed squash and crisp bacon) that
do real flavour work, then bulked out with baby spinach and
finished with a creamy yogurt-tahini drizzle. The pecans —
candied separately with maple syrup — add the third texture.
It's filling enough to be dinner.

The technique is staged. Squash first (12 minutes at 200°C
with maple syrup brushed on at the 9-minute mark — sugars
caramelise without burning). Bacon next in the same basket (8
minutes). Pecans last (4 minutes with maple — they go from
nuts to candied in that short window).

The dressing is yogurt + tahini + lemon + garlic + salt —
creamy without being heavy, slightly nutty from the tahini,
sharp from the lemon. Squeezed in a zigzag pattern across the
finished salad.

## Ingredients

**For the squash:**
- 1 medium butternut squash (about 2 lb / 900g), peeled,
  deseeded, sliced into ½-inch half-moons
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne (optional)

**For the bacon:**
- 6 slices smoky bacon (streaky)

**For the candied pecans:**
- ½ cup (60g) pecan halves
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- Pinch of salt

**For the yogurt-tahini drizzle:**
- ½ cup (120g) Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp warm water (to loosen)
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- ½ tsp kosher salt

**To assemble:**
- 5 oz (140g) baby spinach
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves (optional)
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar (for the spinach)
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

### 01. Cook the squash.
In a bowl, toss the squash half-moons with olive oil, salt,
cinnamon, pepper, and cayenne. Heat the air fryer to 400°F
(200°C). Cook 9 minutes, then brush with maple syrup and
cook 3–4 more minutes until caramelised at the edges.

### 02. Crisp the bacon.
Remove the squash. Add bacon to the basket in a single layer,
cook at 400°F for 7–9 minutes until crisp. Drain on paper
towels, then crumble into pieces.

### 03. Candy the pecans.
Toss pecans with 1 tbsp maple syrup and a pinch of salt. Air
fry at 350°F for 4 minutes, shaking once. Tip onto parchment
paper to cool — they'll firm up to crunchy as they cool.

### 04. Make the dressing.
Whisk yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, water, garlic, and salt
until smooth. Add more water if needed to drizzle
consistency.

### 05. Dress the spinach.
Toss baby spinach lightly with red wine vinegar and olive oil
— just enough to coat.

### 06. Assemble.
Pile dressed spinach onto a large platter. Arrange squash
slices across. Scatter bacon and candied pecans evenly.
Drizzle yogurt-tahini sauce in zigzags. Sprinkle thyme over
the top.

### 07. Serve.
Eat immediately while the squash is still warm against the
cool greens.

## Notes

For a vegan version, skip the bacon (add ½ cup smoked
almonds for the same salt-smoke note) and use a coconut yogurt
in the dressing. For more substance, add ½ cup of cooked
quinoa or pearl barley folded through. The squash can be
cooked ahead and reheated briefly. The candied pecans keep in
a jar for 2 weeks. For a Thanksgiving plated salad, add
pomegranate seeds and a crumble of goat's cheese.
