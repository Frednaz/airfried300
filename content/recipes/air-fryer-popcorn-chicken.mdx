Popcorn chicken is really a permission slip: dinner can be small, crunchy, and dipped in three sauces. The air fryer handles little pieces beautifully because every edge gets a blast of heat.

A quick buttermilk soak keeps the chicken tender, and seasoned panko keeps it from tasting like plain nuggets wearing a fancy coat.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast or thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- Cooking spray
- Honey mustard, ranch, or barbecue sauce

## Method

### 01. Soak the chicken.
Toss chicken with buttermilk and let stand 10 minutes while the air fryer heats.

### 02. Coat.
Mix panko, flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt. Lift chicken from buttermilk and press into crumbs.

### 03. Air fry.
Mist well with spray and cook at 390°F for 10–12 minutes, shaking once, until crisp and 165°F inside.

### 04. Serve by the handful.
Pile into a bowl with dipping sauces. A squeeze of lemon is shockingly good here.

## Notes

Chicken thighs stay juicier, chicken breast looks neater. Either works. Keep pieces similar in size so they finish together.
