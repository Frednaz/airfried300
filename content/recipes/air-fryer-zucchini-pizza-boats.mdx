This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Zucchini halves filled with marinara, mozzarella, and pepperoni for a lighter pizza-adjacent dinner.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 4 medium zucchini
- 1/2 cup marinara
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1/3 cup mini pepperoni
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

## Method

### 01. Hollow zucchini.
Halve zucchini lengthwise and scrape out a shallow channel. Brush with oil and season.

### 02. Start boats.
Air fry cut-side up at 375°F for 6 minutes.

### 03. Fill.
Add marinara, mozzarella, pepperoni, and Italian seasoning.

### 04. Melt.
Air fry 4–6 minutes more, until cheese bubbles and zucchini is tender.

## Notes

Do not hollow too deeply. You want a boat, not a canoe with structural problems.
