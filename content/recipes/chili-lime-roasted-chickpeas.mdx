Crispy roasted chickpeas are the snack you make when you want to
feel slightly more virtuous than eating chips but still want
something crunchy and salty to put in your mouth while you stand
near the kitchen island reading the news on your phone. They're
high-protein, full of fiber, naturally gluten-free, and they take
about 16 minutes from opening a can to standing at the counter
eating them.

The air fryer is the right tool because the alternative — roasting
chickpeas in the oven — takes about 35 minutes at 425°F, with
shaking and turning every 10 minutes. The air fryer does it in
half the time, with one shake halfway through, and you don't have
to heat the whole oven.

The technique is straightforward but has one critical step:
**drying the chickpeas before cooking**. After draining the can,
spread them on a clean kitchen towel and roll them around — many
of the loose skins come off (you don't have to peel each one;
just remove the loose ones). Then pat them dry. Wet chickpeas
won't crisp; they'll just dehydrate and stay chewy. Properly dried
chickpeas crackle when you bite them.

The chili-lime seasoning is what makes these specifically
craveable rather than just generic-crispy. Chili powder, smoked
paprika, lime zest, garlic, salt — sprinkled on at two points:
half before cooking, half after (the residual oil from the basket
helps the second dose stick).

## Ingredients

- 2 (15 oz / 425g) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp lime zest
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 tbsp lime juice (to finish)
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped (to finish)

## Method

### 01. Drain and dry.
Drain the chickpeas. Tip onto a clean kitchen towel, spread out
in a single layer. Roll gently — many of the loose skins will
come off (discard them). Pat the chickpeas very dry. Let air-dry
3 minutes.

### 02. Mix the seasoning.
In a small bowl, combine chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, lime
zest, garlic powder, cumin, and cayenne. Set aside.

### 03. Toss with oil and half the seasoning.
In a larger bowl, toss the dried chickpeas with olive oil. Add
HALF of the seasoning blend, toss until coated.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Tip the chickpeas into the
basket in a roughly even layer. Cook 14–18 minutes, shaking the
basket every 5 minutes, until deeply golden and crackling.

### 05. Toss with remaining seasoning.
While the chickpeas are still hot, tip into a bowl. Sprinkle the
remaining seasoning over them and toss — the residual oil makes
it stick. Add lime juice and chopped cilantro.

### 06. Serve.
Best within an hour of cooking, while still warm and crisp. They
soften as they cool — if you want to preserve crispness, store in
a jar with a paper towel inside (the towel absorbs moisture).

## Notes

For a sweet-savoury variation, swap the chili-lime blend for
cinnamon + smoked paprika + brown sugar (warm and slightly
dessert-leaning). For a ranch-style, use 1 tablespoon of dried
ranch seasoning mix instead. The chickpeas can be served on top
of salads, in grain bowls, or as a crouton substitute. Don't be
tempted to skip the drying step — wet chickpeas are the
single most common failure mode of this recipe.
