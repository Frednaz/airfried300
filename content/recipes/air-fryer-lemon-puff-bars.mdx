This is the kind of recipe that proves a frozen sheet of puff pastry
is one of the great cheat-code ingredients of home cooking. Two sheets
of puff (top and bottom), a generous layer of lemon curd (jarred is
fine, homemade is better), sealed and cut into squares, air-fried at
200°C until the pastry puffs golden and the curd starts to bubble
through the edges.

Twelve minutes of work, twenty-five minutes total, and you have
something that looks like a bakery hand-pie and tastes like the lemon
tart you'd serve at a dinner party.

The trick is sealing the edges properly. Brush the bottom sheet with
beaten egg around the perimeter where the curd ISN'T. Press the top
sheet down firmly. Crimp with a fork. The egg acts as glue when it
bakes — the seal holds and the curd stays inside (mostly; some
oozes through, which is the point and looks beautiful).

The photo shows the finished bars on parchment: deeply golden pillowy
squares dusted with powdered sugar, one broken open to show the
bright yellow curd oozing out, lemon zest curls scattered around.
The kind of dessert that looks more difficult than it is.

## Ingredients

- 2 sheets ready-rolled frozen puff pastry, thawed
- ⅔ cup (200g) lemon curd (jarred or homemade)
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 2 tbsp powdered sugar (icing sugar), for dusting
- Zest of 1 lemon, in long curls

**To serve (optional):**
- Whipped cream
- Fresh berries

## Method

### 01. Prep the pastry.
Place one sheet of pastry on a square of parchment paper that fits
your air fryer.

### 02. Mark and fill.
Using a knife, lightly score the pastry into 6 equal squares (don't
cut through). Spoon a generous tablespoon of lemon curd into the
centre of each square.

### 03. Egg wash.
Brush around each curd pile (the un-curd-filled pastry) with beaten
egg.

### 04. Top and seal.
Lay the second pastry sheet on top. Press down firmly around each
curd pile to seal the edges. Use a fork to crimp around each square.

### 05. Cut and brush.
Cut along the score lines into 6 separate parcels. Brush the tops
with more egg wash.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Carefully transfer the parcels
(with parchment underneath) to the basket. Cook 10–12 minutes, until
the pastry is deeply golden and puffed.

### 07. Finish.
Let cool 5 minutes — the curd is molten. Dust generously with powdered
sugar, scatter lemon zest curls over the top.

### 08. Serve.
Plate with whipped cream and berries if using. Eat warm or at room
temperature.

## Notes

For passionfruit puffs: swap lemon curd for passionfruit curd. For a
chocolate-orange version: substitute jarred chocolate spread + 1 tsp
orange zest. Don't skip the egg-wash sealing step — un-sealed bars
ooze all their curd into the basket. They keep at room temperature
for 24 hours but soften; re-crisp briefly in the air fryer (3 minutes
at 350°F). For a sweeter glaze: skip the powdered sugar and brush
with warmed honey while still hot.
