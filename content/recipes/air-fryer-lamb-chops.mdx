Lamb chops are one of those dishes where the air fryer makes a really
substantive difference to the home cook's experience. Pan-frying lamb
chops splatters fat across the hob and produces a kitchen that smells
like lamb for the next day. The air fryer contains the splatter
inside the basket, cooks the chops evenly on all sides at once
(meaning you don't need to flip every 30 seconds), and produces a
finish that's actually closer to a proper restaurant sear than what
most home cooks achieve on a stovetop.

Loin chops are the cut here — small, mostly tender, with the perfect
ratio of meat to fat and bone. They cook in 6–8 minutes at 200°C
for medium-rare (54°C internal). Get them out at 50°C and let them
rest — carryover heat finishes them. The herb butter (made in 30
seconds while the chops cook) melts on contact and is the entire
finishing sauce.

## Ingredients

**For the chops:**
- 4 lamb loin chops (about 1-inch thick, 4 oz each)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp coarse black pepper

**For the herb butter:**
- 3 tbsp salted butter, softened
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- ½ tsp lemon zest
- Pinch of flaky salt

**To serve:**
- Lemon wedges
- Steamed greens (broccolini, beans, or asparagus)

## Method

### 01. Bring to room temperature.
Take the chops out 20 minutes before cooking. Pat very dry with
paper towels.

### 02. Season.
Rub each chop with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
Press the seasoning into the meat.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the chops in a single
layer, standing upright if your basket is small (it's fine if they
lean). Cook 6–8 minutes for medium-rare (54°C / 130°F internal),
8–10 for medium (60°C / 140°F).

### 04. Make the herb butter.
While chops cook, mash softened butter with garlic, parsley, thyme,
lemon zest, and salt until uniform.

### 05. Rest.
Move chops to a plate. Top each with a generous dollop of herb butter
and rest 4 minutes — the butter will melt and the juices will
redistribute.

### 06. Serve.
Plate with lemon wedges and a side of steamed greens. Spoon the
melted butter pan juices over the chops.

## Notes

For thicker chops (1¼-inch), add 2 minutes to the cook time. For
thinner cutlets (½-inch), reduce to 4 minutes. The herb butter
keeps 1 week in the fridge — make a double batch and use on steak,
fish, or roasted vegetables. For a Greek-leaning version: skip
rosemary, use oregano + lemon zest in the marinade and crumble feta
over at the end. Lamb chops should rest — pulling apart a hot chop
loses all the juices to the plate.
