This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Sweet corn with browned kernels and butter in the grooves, no pot of boiling water required.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 4 ears corn, husked
- 1 tbsp olive oil or softened butter
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp butter, for serving
- Black pepper

## Method

### 01. Prep corn.
Rub corn lightly with oil or butter and season with salt.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway, until spots begin to brown.

### 03. Butter.
Rub hot corn with butter and black pepper.

### 04. Serve.
Eat immediately, preferably over the plate.

## Notes

If your basket is small, cut the ears in half. Frozen corn works too; add 3–4 minutes.
