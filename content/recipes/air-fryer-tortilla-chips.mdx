This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Corn tortillas cut into wedges and air-fried into a warm chip bowl for salsa, queso, or lazy nachos.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 8 corn tortillas
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Lime zest or chili powder, optional

## Method

### 01. Cut.
Cut tortillas into wedges and toss with oil and salt.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 350°F for 6–8 minutes, shaking twice, until crisp and lightly browned.

### 03. Cool.
Let chips cool 2 minutes; they crisp more as they sit.

### 04. Serve.
Serve with salsa, guacamole, or queso.

## Notes

Older tortillas make better chips because they are drier. If using fresh tortillas, leave them uncovered for 10 minutes first.
