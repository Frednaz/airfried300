This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Cornmeal hush puppies with crisp nubbly edges and a tender middle, made without a pot of oil.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 2 tbsp minced onion
- Cooking spray

## Method

### 01. Mix batter.
Stir dry ingredients, then add egg, buttermilk, and onion. Rest 5 minutes.

### 02. Scoop.
Drop tablespoon mounds onto parchment cut to fit your basket.

### 03. Spray.
Mist generously with cooking spray.

### 04. Air fry.
Cook at 375°F for 9–11 minutes, turning once, until golden.

## Notes

The batter should be thick enough to mound. Add a spoonful more cornmeal if it spreads.
