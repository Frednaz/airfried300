The British sausage-and-bean casserole is one of those dishes that
gets cooked in every household in November, makes huge batches, and
freezes well. The traditional version is meat sausages, tomato sauce,
beans, slow-cooked for forty-five minutes. The vegetarian version
needs a slight rethink because veggie sausages don't develop the
same fond as meat ones — they need a dedicated browning step before
they go into the sauce, or they end up pale and sad.

This is where the air fryer earns its keep. While you cook the sauce
on the hob, the sausages crisp in the basket for ten minutes, then
you cut them into chunks and stir them through the sauce. They've
developed proper caramelisation on the outside, which gives the
casserole a savoury depth that "veggie sausage in tomato sauce" usually
lacks.

## Ingredients

**For the air fryer:**
- 6 fresh vegetarian sausages (Cumberland-style or Italian-style)
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp kosher salt

**For the sauce:**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 (14 oz / 400g) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 (14 oz / 400g) can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- ½ cup (120ml) vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**To finish:**
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Crusty bread, to serve

## Method

### 01. Prep the air-fryer batch.
In a bowl, toss the bell pepper and red onion with the olive oil
and salt. Place the sausages in the air fryer basket and scatter
the vegetables around and on top.

### 02. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook 12–14 minutes, shaking
the basket once at the 6-minute mark, until the sausages are
deeply browned and the vegetables are charred at the edges.

### 03. Build the sauce base.
While the air fryer runs, heat the olive oil in a large saucepan
over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until
fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste, paprika, oregano, thyme, and
red pepper flakes; cook 1 minute to bloom the spices.

### 04. Simmer the sauce.
Add the crushed tomatoes, beans, stock, balsamic vinegar, brown
sugar, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook 12 minutes,
stirring occasionally, until thickened to a stew-like consistency.

### 05. Combine.
When the sausages are done, slice each into 4 large chunks. Stir
the sausage chunks and the air-fried vegetables into the sauce.
Simmer together for 4 minutes more, to let the flavours meld.

### 06. Serve.
Ladle into bowls, scatter parsley over the top. Serve with thick
slices of crusty bread for mopping the sauce.

## Notes

Keeps 4 days refrigerated and freezes well for 3 months — it
actually improves overnight. For a meat version, swap the veggie
sausages for proper pork ones (same air-frying time). For a one-pot
version, skip the air fryer and brown the sausages in a hot pan
first — adds about 6 minutes total. Serve over mashed potatoes
instead of with bread for a heartier dinner. Add a bay leaf and a
splash of red wine to the sauce for a deeper, stew-like version.
