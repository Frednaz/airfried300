If pecans-and-maple gives you a Southern-American dessert flavour
profile, pistachios-and-cardamom takes you straight to the Eastern
Mediterranean — Turkish delight, Iranian pastries, the spiced
sweetness of Persian rosewater desserts. Same technique as the pecan
balls (dates + nuts + binder, blitzed, rolled), completely different
cultural territory.

The green is the visual hook. Ground pistachios have a vivid jade
colour that survives blitzing in the food processor, and the second
coat of ground pistachios on the outside is what gives these their
distinctive look. The photo shows the colour exactly: bright green
all over, with the dark date interior visible in the cut-open ball at
the front. They look more sophisticated than they have any right to.

A note on the rose water: it's optional but transformative. ½ teaspoon
is enough to add a floral note that you can't quite identify — it
smells like "fancy" without smelling like soap. Without it, you get a
straightforward pistachio-date-cardamom flavour, which is also good.
If you want to skip it because you don't have it, the recipe still
works.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups shelled pistachios, plus 3 tbsp finely ground (for coating)
- 12 Medjool dates, pitted (about 1 cup pitted)
- 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup for vegan)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp ground cardamom
- ½ tsp rose water (optional)
- Pinch of kosher salt

## Method

### 01. Prepare the pistachios.
If your pistachios are roasted-and-salted, that's fine — adjust salt
accordingly. If raw, toast in a dry skillet over medium heat for 4
minutes, until fragrant. Cool to room temperature. Reserve 3 tbsp,
grind those very finely (food processor on high for 20 seconds), and
set aside for coating.

### 02. Process the main batch.
In a food processor, pulse the 1¼ cups of pistachios 8 times until
coarsely chopped. Add the dates, honey, vanilla, cardamom, rose
water (if using), and salt.

### 03. Blend.
Process for 60–90 seconds, stopping to scrape down the sides once or
twice, until the mixture forms a cohesive, slightly sticky paste.
You want it textured — not a smooth puree.

### 04. Roll.
Scoop by the tablespoon and roll into balls between your palms (damp
hands prevent sticking). You should get 12 balls.

### 05. Coat in ground pistachios.
Roll each ball in the reserved ground pistachios, pressing gently so
the green coating clings. Some balls will be more thickly coated than
others — that's fine.

### 06. Chill and store.
Refrigerate at least 30 minutes to firm up. Keeps 2 weeks
refrigerated, 3 months frozen.

## Notes

For a more decadent version, dip half of each ball in melted dark
chocolate after coating in pistachios — they look like little geodes.
Swap the cardamom for ground cinnamon for a more familiar flavour, or
add ¼ tsp orange zest for a citrus twist. Without rose water, add 1
tsp lemon zest for brightness. Best within 5 days at room temp (after
the initial chill); they soften slightly but stay delicious.
