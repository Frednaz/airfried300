Fajitas are the Tex-Mex dinner that nobody complains about. Adults
get the freedom to build their own; kids get to assemble; the
table conversation moves slower because everyone's chopping their
own pepper. The traditional version is grilled over fire (the name
"fajita" comes from the skirt-steak cut, which historically went
on outdoor grills), but in a home kitchen, the air fryer gets you
most of the way there — high heat, fast cook, the charred-edge
effect that fajita meat needs.

The marinade is the difference between "boring weeknight chicken"
and "actual fajitas." Chili powder for colour and depth. Lime juice
for acid that doubles as a tenderiser. Garlic, cumin, smoked
paprika, brown sugar (a small amount, to balance the acid). The
chicken sits in this for 20 minutes — longer is better but 20 is
enough — and comes out with bright red-orange crust that screams
Tex-Mex from across the room.

The peppers and onions go in the same basket as the chicken at the
last 8 minutes. They char at the edges, soften in the middle, pick
up some of the chicken's seasoning. By the time you take everything
out, the basket looks like a small-scale charred-fajita-platter
that would arrive sizzling at a Chili's table.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken marinade:**
- 1½ lb (700g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into ½-inch
  strips
- 3 tbsp lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

**For the vegetables:**
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced into ½-inch strips
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced into ½-inch strips
- 1 yellow onion, sliced into half-moons
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp chili powder

**To serve:**
- 8 small flour or corn tortillas, warmed
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- ½ cup pico de gallo or salsa
- ½ cup sour cream
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes, cut into wedges

## Method

### 01. Marinate the chicken.
In a bowl, combine the chicken with lime juice, olive oil, chili
powder, cumin, paprika, brown sugar, salt, garlic, and cayenne.
Toss to coat. Marinate 20 minutes at room temperature (or up to
4 hours refrigerated).

### 02. Season the vegetables.
In a separate bowl, toss the peppers and onion with olive oil,
salt, and chili powder.

### 03. First cook — chicken alone.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Lift the chicken from the
marinade (let excess drip off) and arrange in a single layer in
the basket. Cook 8 minutes.

### 04. Add the vegetables.
Open the basket and scatter the seasoned peppers and onion over
and around the chicken. Shake gently to distribute. Cook 6 more
minutes, shaking at the 3-minute mark, until the chicken hits
165°F internal and the peppers are slightly charred.

### 05. Warm the tortillas.
While the air fryer finishes, warm the tortillas: stack on a
plate, cover with a damp paper towel, microwave 30 seconds. Or
warm briefly over a gas burner for a slight char.

### 06. Plate.
Tip the chicken and vegetables onto a serving platter. Squeeze a
lime wedge over the top, scatter cilantro. Serve family-style with
tortillas, avocado, salsa, and sour cream alongside — let people
build their own.

## Notes

For a beef version, swap chicken thighs for flank or skirt steak,
cut into strips against the grain — same marinade, same cooking.
Cook to medium-rare (130°F internal). For a vegetarian version,
replace the chicken with portobello mushroom strips and add an
extra 4 oz of mushrooms. The marinade keeps in the fridge for 3
days. Leftover fajita filling reheats well in the air fryer (3
minutes at 380°F) and is excellent over rice the next day.
