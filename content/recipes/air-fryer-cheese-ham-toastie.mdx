The toastie is one of the most heavily contested sandwiches in
British food. There are factions: the sealed-edges Breville-Snack-N-
Sandwich-Toaster school, the open-faced welsh-rarebit-adjacent school,
the panini-press school, the deep-frying-it school. All of them are
correct depending on context. The air-fryer toastie joins the canon as
the laziest possible high-quality version: no pressing, no flipping,
no specialist equipment.

The technique is the same as for a stovetop grilled cheese, with the
heat applied from all sides at once. You butter the outsides of two
slices of sourdough, fill with cheese and ham, slide into the basket,
flip once at the halfway mark. Six minutes later, the bread is mahogany
on both sides and the cheese has oozed enough to weld the bread
together. No fight with a pan, no babysitting.

The cheese here is where the recipe earns its keep. Sharp cheddar is
the structural component (the melt), grated parmesan adds salt and
depth, a teaspoon of Dijon mustard mixed in adds the slight tang that
keeps the whole thing from being one-note. Fresh chives stirred
through give green flecks and a faint allium note. Two slices of
quality ham (not the wafer-thin sliced stuff — proper ham, ideally
honey-roast or maple-glazed).

## Ingredients

**Per toastie (makes 2):**
- 4 thick slices of sourdough (or other sturdy bread)
- 3 tbsp salted butter, softened
- 1¼ cups (140g) sharp cheddar, grated
- ¼ cup (25g) parmesan, finely grated
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh chives
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 4 slices good-quality ham (about 4 oz / 110g)

## Method

### 01. Make the cheese mix.
In a bowl, combine the cheddar, parmesan, Dijon mustard, chives,
and black pepper. Mix with a fork until uniform.

### 02. Butter the outsides.
Butter ONE side of each slice of bread, going all the way to the
edges. Use a generous amount — about 1½ tsp per slice. This is the
side that touches the basket.

### 03. Assemble.
With the buttered sides facing OUT, lay two slices buttered-side-down
on a plate. Divide the cheese mix between them, mounded slightly.
Lay 2 slices of ham over each pile of cheese. Top with the remaining
two slices of bread (buttered side up).

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Place the toasties in the basket
with space between them (work in two batches if needed). Cook 6
minutes, then carefully flip with a spatula. Cook 3–4 minutes more,
until both sides are deeply golden and the cheese has oozed slightly
from the edges.

### 05. Rest and slice.
Move to a board and let rest 90 seconds — the molten cheese needs to
settle slightly so it doesn't pour out when you slice. Cut on the
diagonal for the photo-perfect cross-section.

## Notes

For a fancier version: spread the bread (interior side) with a thin
layer of caramelised onion chutney before adding the cheese. For a
Croque Madame direction: skip the ham, add a fried egg on top after
air-frying. The cheese mix can be made 3 days ahead and refrigerated;
bring to room temperature before assembling. Don't substitute pre-
sliced cheese for grated — pre-sliced melts unevenly and doesn't ooze
the same way. Sturdy sourdough or rustic white bread holds up best;
soft sandwich bread compresses too much.
