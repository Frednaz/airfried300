The Oreo cheesecake exists because someone realised that an Oreo's
cookie-and-cream architecture is essentially a tiny pre-made cheesecake
base. Build on that. Use one whole Oreo as the bottom of a muffin
cup, fold chopped Oreos into a sweet cream-cheese filling, pour over,
top with another whole Oreo. Bake gently. The result is a portion-
controlled dessert that's mostly cheesecake but visually mostly Oreo.

The air fryer is unexpectedly good for small cheesecakes. The low,
even heat (160°C) prevents the cracking that happens in a conventional
oven at higher temperatures. Fifteen minutes and they're set; the
texture firms up further as they chill.

These are a kids' birthday-party staple in Australia and Britain.
They're also surprisingly elegant served at the end of a dinner with
coffee — small, photogenic, slightly nostalgic.

## Ingredients

**For the cheesecake filling:**
- 8 oz (225g) cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup (50g) caster sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ cup (60g) sour cream
- 6 Oreos, finely chopped (for folding into filling)

**For the base and top:**
- 12 whole Oreos (6 for bases, 6 for tops)

**To finish:**
- 1 tbsp powdered sugar, for dusting

**Equipment:**
- 6 small silicone or paper muffin cases (the size that fit your
  air fryer basket)

## Method

### 01. Place the bases.
Set the muffin cases in a tray that fits your air fryer. Place one
whole Oreo at the bottom of each case.

### 02. Make the filling.
In a bowl, beat softened cream cheese with sugar until smooth. Add
the egg and beat until incorporated. Stir in vanilla and sour cream.
Fold in the chopped Oreos.

### 03. Fill.
Spoon the cheesecake mixture into each case, dividing evenly. Fill
to just below the rim.

### 04. Top.
Press one whole Oreo onto the top of each cheesecake — let it sink
in slightly.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 320°F (160°C). Place the muffin cases in the
basket. Cook 14–16 minutes, until the filling is just set with the
slightest wobble in the centre.

### 06. Cool and chill.
Let cool to room temperature in the cases (about 30 minutes), then
refrigerate at least 2 hours (overnight is better).

### 07. Serve.
Remove from cases. Dust lightly with powdered sugar. Serve cold,
straight from the fridge.

## Notes

For chocolate-cheesecake variation: melt 50g dark chocolate and fold
into the filling before adding chopped Oreos. For a different cookie:
swap Oreos for chocolate digestives or speculoos. The cheesecakes
keep 4 days refrigerated — best on day 2 (the flavour matures). For
a make-ahead party version: bake, chill overnight, serve the next
day. Don't overbake — they should still wobble slightly when pulled
from the air fryer; they firm up as they cool. The first dessert
disappears within 60 seconds at a kids' table.
