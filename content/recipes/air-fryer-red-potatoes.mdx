Red potatoes are structurally different from russet potatoes, and
the difference matters for how they cook. Russets are starchy and
floury — they bake into fluffy interiors and crisp exteriors
(perfect for baked potatoes and chips). Red potatoes (and other
waxy varieties: Yukon Gold, fingerlings) are denser and lower in
starch — they hold their shape when cut and stay creamy through
the centre even after cooking. They're the better choice when you
want potatoes that go with something, rather than potatoes that
ARE the dish.

The technique is universal — halve or quarter so each piece has a
cut face, toss with olive oil, salt, garlic, and rosemary, air-fry
at 400°F until the cut faces caramelise. About 18 minutes for
1½-inch baby reds. The skins crackle slightly; the cut sides go
deep amber; the interiors stay tender. They go with everything:
roast chicken, grilled fish, steak, eggs.

The photo shows them in their natural habitat: a black ceramic
bowl, piled high, with visible salt and rosemary needles. No
sauce, no garnish — these don't need anything. They're the
supporting side dish to a roast or a stew, not the headliner.

A note on the herbs: fresh rosemary is meaningfully better than
dried here. The needles release oil when they hit the hot air
fryer and that oil perfumes the potatoes throughout. Dried rosemary
works in a pinch but use about a third as much.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (700g) baby red potatoes, halved (quartered if large)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary leaves, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1¼ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper, freshly cracked
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (to finish)
- Lemon wedges, to serve (optional)

## Method

### 01. Cut.
Halve the potatoes through the equator. If any are particularly
large (over 1½ inches), quarter them. The aim is uniform-sized
pieces so they cook evenly.

### 02. Season.
In a bowl, toss the potatoes with olive oil, rosemary, garlic,
salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if using.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the potatoes
cut-side-down in a single layer in the basket. Cook 16–20 minutes,
shaking at the 10-minute mark, until the cut faces are deeply
golden and a knife slides easily through the centre.

### 04. Finish.
Tip into a serving bowl. Scatter parsley over the top, with a
small squeeze of lemon juice if you want brightness.

### 05. Serve.
Serve immediately while the skins still crackle slightly.

## Notes

For a fully vegetarian dinner, serve over a bowl of greens with a
fried egg on top — that's a complete meal. For Italian-leaning,
finish with grated parmesan instead of (or alongside) the parsley.
For breakfast-leaning, dice the potatoes smaller (½-inch instead
of halves), shorten the cook time to 14 minutes, and serve under
scrambled eggs. The recipe scales linearly — double for 8 servings,
just work in 2 batches in the basket. Don't substitute russet
potatoes — they go fluffy in the middle but lose the structural
integrity that makes the red potato dish work.
