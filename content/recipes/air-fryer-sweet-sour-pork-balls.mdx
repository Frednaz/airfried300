Sweet-and-sour pork is the Chinese-takeaway classic that took
British and Australian high streets by storm decades ago and never
left. The takeaway version is usually battered cubes of pork in a
neon-orange sauce that's mostly sugar. This homemade version replaces
batter with seasoned pork mince balls — still crisp on the outside
from air-frying, but more tender and savoury inside.

The sauce is balanced rather than candy-sweet: rice vinegar for the
sour, brown sugar and ketchup for the sweet, soy and sesame for
depth, cornflour to thicken. Made in a pan in 5 minutes while the
pork cooks.

## Ingredients

**For the pork balls:**
- 1 lb (450g) pork mince
- ½ cup (60g) panko breadcrumbs
- 1 small onion, finely grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp Chinese five-spice
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp white pepper
- Olive oil spray

**For the sweet-and-sour sauce:**
- ½ cup tomato ketchup
- ⅓ cup rice vinegar
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 cup pineapple juice (from a can of pineapple)
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water

**To finish:**
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

**To serve:**
- Steamed jasmine rice
- Pineapple chunks (optional)

## Method

### 01. Mix the pork balls.
Combine pork, panko, onion, garlic, egg, soy, ginger, five-spice,
salt, and pepper. Mix gently.

### 02. Shape.
Roll into 20 balls, golf-ball-sized.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray balls with oil. Cook in a
single layer 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until deeply browned
and internal temp reads 160°F (71°C).

### 04. Make the sauce.
While balls cook: combine ketchup, vinegar, sugar, soy, pineapple
juice, and garlic in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium
heat. Whisk in cornflour slurry, simmer 2 minutes until glossy and
thickened.

### 05. Toss.
Transfer cooked balls to a bowl. Pour over the sauce, toss to coat.

### 06. Plate.
Tip balls onto rice. Scatter spring onions, sesame seeds, and
pineapple chunks if using. Serve immediately.

## Notes

For a chicken version: same recipe, swap pork for chicken mince. For
prawn balls: swap pork for finely minced raw prawns + 2 tbsp
cornflour to help bind. The sauce can be doubled and frozen. For a
crispy-coating version: dust balls in cornflour before air-frying
for an extra crunch. Leftovers reheat in the air fryer for 4 minutes
at 380°F.
