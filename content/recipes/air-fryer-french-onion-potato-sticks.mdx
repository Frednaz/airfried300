French onion soup mix — the powdered packet of dehydrated onion,
beef stock, and seasoning that lives in every Australian and British
pantry — is one of the great cheat-code ingredients. It seasons mince,
flavours dips, and here, transforms grated potato into something that
tastes way more interesting than its component parts.

The technique is borrowed from latkes and rösti but simplified.
Grated potato (squeezed dry), grated cheese, beaten egg, French onion
soup mix, a small handful of flour to bind. Shape into thick rectangular
sticks about 3 inches long, air-fry until the outside crisps and the
cheese inside the dough melts down. The salt and umami of the soup
mix is what makes them taste like a $14 menu item rather than a
homemade snack.

The photo shows them stacked on a white plate: golden-brown sticks
with visible cheese pools at the cut faces, a small bowl of sour cream
and chives behind. The kind of side dish people pick at while waiting
for the rest of dinner.

## Ingredients

**For the sticks:**
- 1½ lb (700g) Maris Piper or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and
  coarsely grated
- 1 tsp salt (for sweating the potato)
- 1 packet (35g) French onion soup mix
- 1 cup (110g) grated cheddar
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 3 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, finely sliced
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Olive oil spray

**To serve:**
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- Cracked pepper

## Method

### 01. Squeeze the potato.
Grate the potatoes. Toss with 1 tsp salt and let sit 10 minutes —
the salt draws out moisture. Tip into a clean tea towel and squeeze
hard to remove as much liquid as possible.

### 02. Mix.
In a bowl, combine squeezed potato, French onion soup mix, grated
cheese, beaten egg, flour, chives, and pepper. Mix until uniform.

### 03. Shape.
Press the mixture into thick rectangular sticks about 3 inches long
by ¾-inch wide. You'll get 12–14 sticks.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray sticks with olive oil.
Cook in a single layer for 14–16 minutes, flipping halfway, until
deeply golden and crisp.

### 05. Make the dipping cream.
Stir sour cream with chives and pepper.

### 06. Serve.
Pile onto a plate with the sour cream bowl alongside. Eat while
the cheese is still molten inside.

## Notes

For more contrast: add 2 tbsp grated parmesan to the mix for sharper
flavour. For a smokier version: swap half the cheddar for smoked
gouda. The sticks reheat passably in the air fryer for 3 minutes at
380°F. Without French onion soup mix: use 2 tsp onion powder + 1 tsp
beef stock powder + ¼ tsp dried thyme + ½ tsp salt. Don't skip
squeezing the potato — moisture is the enemy of crisp.
