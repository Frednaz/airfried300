The French omelette is a beautiful, exacting thing — pale yellow,
folded, soft-set, made in a non-stick pan with your full attention for
ninety seconds. The air-fryer omelette is none of those things. It's
puffed, golden on top, more frittata-shaped than half-moon-shaped,
made while you do something else entirely. It is a completely
different dish wearing the name "omelette" because we don't have a
better word for it. Don't try to compare them. They're cousins, not
twins.

What the air-fryer omelette has going for it is the unattended cook
and the ability to use whatever you have. Whisk three eggs with a
splash of milk and salt, pour into a greased ramekin or small heat-
proof dish, scatter the toppings, slide into the basket, set the
timer. Eight minutes later you have breakfast that's protein-dense
and almost zero-effort.

The photo is the classic Mediterranean version: spinach (wilts into
the eggs as they cook), torn prosciutto (the pink flecks visible
through the surface), crumbled feta (the white pockets that stay
softer than the eggs around them), served with a side of mixed
greens dressed with olive oil and lemon. On a cream-coloured plate
with teal cutlery — for the joy of it.

## Ingredients

**For the omelette (single serving):**
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tbsp whole milk (or cream)
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- Pinch of black pepper
- 1 tsp butter or olive oil (for greasing)
- ½ cup baby spinach
- 2 slices prosciutto, torn into pieces
- 2 tbsp feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 tsp fresh dill or chives (optional)

**To serve:**
- 1 cup mixed salad leaves
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Squeeze of lemon

## Method

### 01. Prep the spinach.
If using fresh spinach, microwave for 30 seconds with a tablespoon of
water to wilt slightly (skip if you want it crunchier in the finished
omelette). Squeeze out excess moisture.

### 02. Whisk the eggs.
In a small bowl, whisk the eggs with the milk, salt, and pepper until
uniform and slightly frothy.

### 03. Prepare the dish.
Lightly grease a small heat-proof dish (5–6 inches wide, about 1½
cups capacity — a small cake tin or oven-safe bowl works). Pour the
egg mixture in.

### 04. Add the fillings.
Scatter the spinach, torn prosciutto, and crumbled feta over the
egg mixture — they'll sink slightly as they cook, which is what
you want. Sprinkle with dill or chives if using.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Place the dish in the basket.
Cook 7–9 minutes, until the eggs are set throughout and the top is
slightly golden. The middle should be just barely jiggly — it
finishes setting from carryover heat.

### 06. Rest briefly.
Let the omelette rest 1 minute in the dish, then turn out onto a
plate (or eat directly from the dish). Toss the mixed leaves with
olive oil and lemon, place beside the omelette.

## Notes

This scales well — make two at once in separate ramekins if your
basket fits them. For a low-carb breakfast, this is the move; for
a brunch spread, serve with toast and avocado. Other filling combos:
goat cheese + sundried tomatoes + basil (Italian-leaning), smoked
salmon + dill + cream cheese (Sunday-brunch-y), bacon + cheddar +
scallions (American-diner). The dish needs to be heatproof — Pyrex,
ceramic ramekins, mini cake tins all work. Don't use thin metal —
you'll get hot spots and uneven cooking.
