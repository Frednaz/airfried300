Poaching eggs is one of those techniques home cooks dread for no good
reason — the result is consistently excellent but the methods (vortex
of swirling water, vinegar, careful crack) feel finicky. The air
fryer poach is the cheat: crack an egg into a small ramekin filled
with hot water, air-fry for six minutes, lift out with a slotted
spoon. The whites set evenly because they're contained; the yolks
stay liquid; the texture is the same as a proper hob-poached egg.

The technique requires four small ramekins (silicon or ceramic, 6oz
each) and pre-boiled water poured in just before the eggs go in. The
hot water gets the cooking started before the air fryer takes over.

Six minutes at 180°C produces a perfectly runny yolk. Seven minutes
gets you a softer-set yolk. Eight minutes is fully cooked through —
useful for nervous eaters and Sunday-school morning teas.

## Ingredients

- 4 fresh, cold large eggs
- 2 slices good sourdough or country bread
- 2 tbsp salted butter
- Flaky sea salt
- Cracked black pepper

**To serve (optional):**
- Fresh chives or parsley, chopped
- Hot sauce
- Crumbled feta
- Smashed avocado

**Equipment:**
- 4 small ramekins (6oz each, silicone or ceramic)

## Method

### 01. Boil the water.
Bring a kettle of water to a rolling boil. You'll need about 1 cup.

### 02. Fill the ramekins.
Place 4 ramekins in the air fryer basket. Pour about ¼ cup of
boiling water into each.

### 03. Crack the eggs.
Crack one egg into each ramekin. The water should cover the egg by
about ¼ inch.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 360°F (180°C). Cook 6 minutes for runny
yolks, 7 minutes for softer-set, 8 for fully cooked.

### 05. Toast the bread.
While eggs cook, toast the bread (toaster, grill, or briefly in the
air fryer the minute before).

### 06. Butter the toast.
Butter the toast generously.

### 07. Lift the eggs.
Use a slotted spoon to lift each egg out of its ramekin, letting
water drain. Place 2 eggs on each slice of toast.

### 08. Season and serve.
Top with flaky salt, cracked pepper, and herbs. Add hot sauce or
crumbled feta if using.

## Notes

For the truly soft yolk: 5½ minutes — the whites are just-set. For
prawn-shaped slightly raw whites avoided: ensure your eggs are
fresh (the fresher the egg, the tighter the white). Don't skip the
boiling-water start — cold water means undercooked whites by the
time the yolk is right. For breakfast for four: use 8 ramekins or
cook in 2 batches. The water in the ramekins prevents the eggs
from sticking and provides the gentle steaming-poaching environment.
