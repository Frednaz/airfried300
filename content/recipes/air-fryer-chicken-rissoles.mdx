The rissole is the Australian patty — minced meat (chicken, beef, or
lamb) mixed with onion, breadcrumbs, herbs, an egg to bind, formed
into ovals about the size of small potatoes, pan-fried until crusty
on the outside, served with mashed potato and peas. It's a school-
canteen lunch, a Wednesday night dinner, a meal-prep staple. Every
Australian household has its own ratio.

This version adds grated zucchini to the chicken mince — the moisture
keeps the rissoles from drying out (chicken mince is notoriously
quick to go dry), and a young child probably won't notice. The air
fryer gives you the same browned crust as a pan without the splatter,
and you can cook 8 rissoles at once instead of 4.

The brush of sweet chilli sauce at the end is the optional flourish
— glossy, slightly hot, makes the rissoles look intentional rather
than utilitarian.

The photo shows them piled in a black ceramic bowl: golden brown,
visible flecks of green zucchini and herbs in the dough, sweet chilli
sauce glazing the tops. A weeknight dinner that doesn't apologise
for being a weeknight dinner.

## Ingredients

**For the rissoles:**
- 1 lb (500g) chicken mince
- 1 medium zucchini, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 small yellow onion, finely grated
- ½ cup (60g) panko breadcrumbs
- ¼ cup (25g) grated parmesan
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp paprika
- Olive oil spray

**For glazing (optional):**
- 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tsp soy sauce

**To serve:**
- Mashed potato
- Peas

## Method

### 01. Squeeze the zucchini.
Grate the zucchini, place in a clean tea towel, and squeeze hard to
remove as much liquid as possible.

### 02. Mix.
In a large bowl, combine chicken mince, squeezed zucchini, grated
onion, breadcrumbs, parmesan, egg, garlic, parsley, mustard, salt,
pepper, and paprika. Mix gently with your hands until just combined.

### 03. Shape.
Divide the mixture into 8 portions. Shape each into an oval rissole
about ¾-inch thick.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray the rissoles with olive
oil. Cook in a single layer for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway,
until deeply golden and internal temp reads 165°F (74°C).

### 05. Glaze (optional).
Mix sweet chilli and soy sauce. In the last 2 minutes of cooking,
brush each rissole with the glaze.

### 06. Serve.
Pile onto a serving dish. Spoon over any remaining glaze. Serve with
mashed potato and peas.

## Notes

For meal prep: cook, cool, refrigerate up to 4 days; reheat in the
air fryer for 4 minutes at 350°F. Freezes well — freeze uncooked
shaped rissoles on a tray, then bag. Cook from frozen at 400°F for
17 minutes. For beef rissoles: substitute beef mince, use Worcestershire
sauce instead of Dijon, skip the parmesan, add 1 tsp tomato paste.
The zucchini is the most important addition — chicken mince without
moisture is dust on a plate.
