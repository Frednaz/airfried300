The traybake is the great British weeknight invention — protein and
vegetables on the same sheet pan, slid into a hot oven, dinner ready
in under 45 minutes. The air-fryer version compresses the timeline
further: same one-pan logic, half the cooking time, faster preheat,
no oven warming the kitchen on a summer evening.

Rose harissa is the ingredient that transforms this from "tray of
roasted things" into "tray of roasted things with serious personality."
It's harissa paste with dried rose petals added — slightly floral,
deeply smoky-spicy, the colour of dried tomato. A spoonful tossed with
the sausages glazes them with reddish-orange lacquer; a smaller
spoonful in with the vegetables ties everything together. If you can't
find rose harissa, regular harissa works (just add a teaspoon of honey
to mellow it).

The photo shows the finished plate, plated rather than tray-served:
two harissa-glossed sausages laid across a bed of roasted butternut
squash chunks, red bell pepper, red onion wedges, and crinkled bits
of kale that have crisped at the edges. Coral-pink ceramic plate
underneath, which makes the colour palette of the dish even more
intense.

## Ingredients

- 6 fresh Italian sausages (sweet or hot)
- 1 lb (450g) butternut squash, peeled and cubed (¾-inch)
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 tbsp rose harissa paste, divided (regular harissa + 1 tsp honey works)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 cups (60g) kale, stems removed, torn into 2-inch pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for the kale)
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (to finish)
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Method

### 01. Season the vegetables.
In a large bowl, toss the butternut squash, red pepper, and red onion
with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp harissa, cumin, paprika, salt, and
pepper.

### 02. Glaze the sausages.
In a separate bowl, rub the sausages all over with the remaining 1
tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp harissa, coating evenly.

### 03. Layer in the basket.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Add the seasoned vegetables to
the basket in a roughly even layer. Place the glazed sausages on top.

### 04. Cook.
Cook 18–22 minutes, shaking the basket at 10 minutes (the vegetables;
leave the sausages on top), until the sausages are deeply browned
and the squash is tender with caramelised edges.

### 05. Crisp the kale.
While the cooking finishes, toss the kale with 1 tbsp olive oil and
a pinch of salt — massage gently for 30 seconds to soften. In the
last 4 minutes of cooking, lift the sausages out briefly, scatter
the kale over the vegetables in the basket, replace the sausages,
and continue cooking.

### 06. Plate.
Move everything to a serving platter (or 4 individual plates).
Sprinkle with lemon zest and fresh parsley. Serve with lemon wedges
for squeezing over.

## Notes

For a vegetarian version, swap the sausages for chickpeas — drain and
rinse a can, toss with 1 tbsp harissa + 1 tbsp olive oil, scatter
over the vegetables for the last 12 minutes. The squash can be swapped
for sweet potato (similar cook time) or carrots and parsnips (add 5
minutes). Without rose harissa, use regular harissa + 1 tsp honey +
½ tsp dried mint — close enough. Leftovers are excellent cold over
couscous the next day.
