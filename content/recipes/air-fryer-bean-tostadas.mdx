The tostada is the simplest possible Mexican-American dish — a
crispy flat tortilla loaded with toppings, eaten with the hands. It's
also one of the easiest dishes to ruin, because the tostada shell is
the load-bearing wall and if it goes soft, everything collapses. The
deep-fried version (the proper way) is excellent but messy. The
shop-bought tostada shells are fine but tend to be stale by the time
you buy them. The air-fryer method, which I genuinely didn't think
would work, produces tostada shells that are crisp enough to support
a full load of beans and toppings without bending.

The technique is laughably simple: corn tortillas brushed lightly
with oil, air-fried at 400°F for about five minutes. They puff
slightly in the basket, then settle into a flat, crisp disc with
slightly bubbled spots. Better than restaurant tostadas by a small
margin, better than bagged tostadas by a wide one.

The photo shows a single tostada in its natural arrangement: the
crisp tortilla as the base (you can see it through the toppings),
spread with refried beans, scattered with shredded cheese (the
Mexican blend — cheddar, Monterey Jack, asadero), shredded lettuce
or romaine, diced tomato. There are flecks of green lettuce on the
surrounding plate — the casual confetti of mealtime. Serve two per
person; they're more substantial than they look.

## Ingredients

**For the tostada shells:**
- 4 corn tortillas (6-inch, white or yellow)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (or avocado oil)
- Pinch of kosher salt

**For the toppings:**
- 1 (15 oz / 425g) can refried black beans (or pinto)
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp chili powder
- 1 cup shredded Mexican-blend cheese (cheddar + Monterey Jack)
- 1½ cups shredded romaine or iceberg lettuce
- 1 large Roma tomato, diced
- 2 tbsp red onion, finely diced (optional)
- ¼ cup sour cream (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
- Hot sauce, to serve
- Lime wedges, to serve

## Method

### 01. Warm the tortillas.
Stack the tortillas, cover with a damp paper towel, microwave 20
seconds to make them pliable.

### 02. Oil and salt.
Brush each tortilla on both sides with olive oil. Sprinkle a
pinch of salt on each.

### 03. Air fry the shells.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place 2 tortillas at a time
in the basket (try not to overlap). Cook 4–5 minutes, flipping at
the 2-minute mark, until deeply golden and crisp. Repeat with the
other 2 tortillas.

### 04. Warm the beans.
While the tortillas cook, warm the refried beans in a small
saucepan over medium-low heat with the cumin and chili powder.
Thin with a tablespoon of water if needed; they should be
spreadable.

### 05. Assemble.
Lay the crispy tostada shells on plates. Spread each with a
heaping ¼ cup of refried beans (don't go to the edge — leave a
small rim of crispy tortilla). Top with cheese, lettuce, diced
tomato, and red onion. Dollop with sour cream if using. Scatter
cilantro over the top.

### 06. Serve.
Eat with both hands, leaning forward over a plate. Hit with hot
sauce and a squeeze of lime before the first bite.

## Notes

**For meat-eaters**: add a layer of cooked, seasoned ground beef or
shredded rotisserie chicken between the beans and the cheese. For
a fancier version: top with sliced avocado, pickled red onions, and
a drizzle of crema. The tostada shells can be made ahead — cool to
room temp, store in an airtight container at room temperature for
up to 2 days. They'll soften slightly; re-crisp in the air fryer for
60 seconds at 380°F before using. Flour tortillas work too but
they're less authentic and slightly less crisp.
