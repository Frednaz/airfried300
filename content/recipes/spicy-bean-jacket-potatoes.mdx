The jacket potato with baked beans is the foundation stone of
British weeknight cooking. A potato in the oven, beans on the
hob, butter and grated cheese, salt and pepper, eat with a fork
on the sofa. The recipe doesn't need defending; it's a category
of food.

This version takes the same logic and pushes it just slightly —
the baked beans are not from-the-can-with-nothing-added, but
from-the-can-stirred-with-smoked-paprika-and-chipotle-and-bbq-
sauce. The transformation takes 5 minutes of work and turns the
beans from "soft sweet sauce" into "smoky, slightly hot, layered
flavour." The pickled jalapeños on top are the final pop.

The air fryer cuts the potato cook time roughly in half — about
45 minutes for a 12-oz baking potato at 400°F vs 75–90 in a
conventional oven. The skin gets blisteringly crisp (rub with
olive oil and sea salt before cooking, this is non-negotiable).
The inside stays fluffy.

## Ingredients

**For the potatoes:**
- 4 large baking potatoes (12 oz / 340g each)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**For the spiced beans:**
- 1 (14 oz / 400g) can baked beans (Heinz or any classic UK
  brand; for Americans, baked beans with the standard tomato-
  molasses sauce)
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chipotle paste (or 1 chipotle in adobo, finely chopped)
- 1 tbsp barbecue sauce
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- ¼ tsp salt (adjust — beans are pre-salted)

**To assemble:**
- 4 oz (110g) sharp cheddar, grated
- 8 pickled jalapeño slices (or fresh, sliced)
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp soured cream (optional)
- Fresh coriander, to garnish

## Method

### 01. Prep and bake the potatoes.
Scrub the potatoes, prick each several times with a fork, rub
with olive oil, sprinkle with salt and pepper. Heat the air
fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook 40–50 minutes, until a knife
slides easily into the centre.

### 02. Make the spiced beans.
While the potatoes cook, heat olive oil in a small saucepan
over medium heat. Add onion, cook 5 minutes until softened.
Add garlic, cook 30 seconds. Stir in paprika, chipotle paste,
barbecue sauce, Worcestershire, cumin, and brown sugar. Cook
1 minute.

### 03. Add the baked beans.
Tip in the can of baked beans (sauce and all). Simmer 5
minutes to thicken slightly and let flavours combine. Taste
and adjust salt.

### 04. Split the potatoes.
When the potatoes are done, cut a deep cross into the top of
each. Squeeze the sides to push the cross open and fluff the
interior with a fork.

### 05. Top.
Spoon the spiced beans generously over each potato. Pile
grated cheese on top — it'll melt from the residual heat.
Scatter pickled jalapeños and spring onions.

### 06. Optional cheese melt.
If the cheese hasn't fully melted, return the topped potatoes
to the air fryer at 380°F (193°C) for 3 minutes.

### 07. Serve.
Add a dollop of soured cream if using and a scatter of fresh
coriander. Eat with a fork, from a tray, while watching
something undemanding on television.

## Notes

For a meatier version, add 4 oz of chorizo (sliced) to the beans
while sautéing. For more heat, double the chipotle and add a
diced fresh chilli. The spiced beans keep 4 days in the fridge
and freeze well — make a double batch. For sweet-potato jackets
(slightly different proposition but excellent), substitute sweet
potatoes — they cook in 35 minutes at 400°F. Without baked beans,
use a can of black beans (drained) + ¼ cup of barbecue sauce +
2 tbsp tomato paste — different but works.
