Banana bread is the recipe everyone has memorised, made on autopilot
from three or four black bananas that nobody ate. Most banana bread
recipes are functionally identical: bananas, flour, sugar, egg, oil
or butter, baking soda, salt. The variables are how long you bake it,
what you put on top, and how patient you are about letting it cool
before slicing.

The air fryer changes the baking part. You still need a small loaf
tin — a 7×4-inch loaf tin (or two mini ones) fits in most baskets.
The advantage is speed: 35 minutes at 320°F vs. 55 in the oven, and
the dry circulating heat develops a crust on top while the inside
stays moist. The pecans on top toast as the loaf bakes; you don't
need a separate step.

The photo shows the cut loaf: a thick slice plus the loaf alongside,
showing a dark mahogany crust on top studded with pecan halves, the
crumb visible through the slice as moist, dark-flecked, properly
banana-bread-textured. There's a sprinkle of brown sugar on top under
the pecans — that's what makes the crust shatter slightly when you
cut into it.

## Ingredients

**For the bread:**
- 3 very ripe bananas (black-spotted), mashed (about 1½ cups)
- ½ cup (110g) light brown sugar
- ¼ cup (60g) unsalted butter, melted
- ¼ cup (60ml) plain Greek yogurt (or sour cream)
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1½ cups (190g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup (60g) pecans, chopped (optional, plus more for topping)

**For the topping:**
- 2 tbsp pecan halves
- 1 tbsp light brown sugar

## Method

### 01. Prep the tin.
Grease a 7×4-inch (18×10cm) loaf tin with butter and line with
parchment, leaving overhang on the long sides for lifting out.

### 02. Mash and mix wet.
In a large bowl, mash the bananas thoroughly with a fork. Add the
brown sugar, melted butter, yogurt, egg, and vanilla. Whisk until
smooth.

### 03. Add dry.
Sift the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt over the wet
ingredients. Fold gently with a spatula until just combined — don't
overmix, lumps are fine. Fold in the chopped pecans.

### 04. Pour and top.
Pour the batter into the prepared tin. Smooth the top with a spatula.
Scatter the pecan halves over the top and sprinkle with the topping
sugar.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 320°F (160°C). Place the tin in the basket.
Cook for 32–38 minutes, checking at 30 minutes — a skewer inserted
into the middle should come out with only a few moist crumbs (not
wet batter). If the top is browning too fast in the last 10 minutes,
loosely tent a piece of foil over the loaf.

### 06. Cool and slice.
Let the loaf cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then lift out using the
parchment overhang. Cool another 15 minutes on a rack before slicing
— this is when banana bread firms up to sliceable consistency.

## Notes

Keeps 4 days at room temp in an airtight container, 1 week
refrigerated, 3 months frozen. For chocolate chip banana bread, swap
the pecans for ⅔ cup chocolate chips (mini or regular). For walnut,
straight swap pecans for walnuts. The riper the bananas, the sweeter
the loaf — if yours aren't black yet, ripen them quickly by roasting
whole at 350°F for 15 minutes (until the skins turn black). For mini
loaves: same recipe, two mini tins, 22 minutes.
