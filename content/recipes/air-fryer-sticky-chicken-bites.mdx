The "sticky chicken" Chinese-takeaway dish — chunks of crispy fried
chicken glazed in a sweet-savoury sauce — is one of the most
universally ordered things on every British and Australian Chinese
menu. The home version is genuinely easy: chicken thigh chunks
dredged in cornflour for the crisp coating, air-fried at 200°C until
golden, then tossed in a quick honey-soy-garlic glaze.

The glaze is the soul: honey for sweetness, both soy sauces for
salt and colour, rice vinegar for acidity, garlic and ginger for
aromatic depth, and a cornflour slurry to thicken into a proper
lacquer. Made in 4 minutes in a small saucepan while the chicken
cooks.

The photo shows the chicken piled in a small parchment-lined bowl:
deeply glazed in glossy dark amber sauce, sesame seeds scattered
across, fresh green spring onion rings on top. Eat with chopsticks
directly from the bowl.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken:**
- 1½ lb (700g) boneless, skinless chicken thigh, cut into 1-inch
  chunks
- 4 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tsp Chinese five-spice
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp white pepper
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

**For the sticky glaze:**
- ¼ cup honey
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp Sriracha (optional, for heat)
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water

**To finish:**
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 3 spring onions, finely sliced
- Steamed jasmine rice (to serve)

## Method

### 01. Coat the chicken.
Mix cornflour, five-spice, salt, and white pepper in a bowl. Toss
chicken in beaten egg, then in the cornflour mix. Drizzle with the
neutral oil and toss again.

### 02. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook chicken in a single layer
11–13 minutes, shaking every 4 minutes, until deeply crisp.

### 03. Make the glaze.
In a small saucepan: whisk honey, both soy sauces, vinegar, sesame
oil, garlic, ginger, and Sriracha. Bring to a simmer for 90 seconds.
Whisk in cornflour slurry, cook 30 seconds until glossy and
thick.

### 04. Toss.
Transfer crisp chicken to a bowl. Pour over the glaze and toss
gently to coat.

### 05. Serve.
Plate over steamed rice. Scatter sesame seeds and spring onions.

## Notes

For Korean-style: swap honey for gochujang + 2 tbsp brown sugar; add
1 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes). The chicken will lose its
crispness within 10 minutes of glazing — eat immediately. Leftovers
reheat passably in the air fryer for 4 minutes at 380°F.
