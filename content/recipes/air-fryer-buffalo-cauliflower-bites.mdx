This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Cauliflower florets cooked until crisp at the edges, then tossed in buttery Buffalo sauce for a vegetarian wing-night plate.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 cup Buffalo sauce
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- Ranch or blue cheese dressing, for serving

## Method

### 01. Coat.
Whisk flour, milk, garlic powder, paprika, and a pinch of salt into a thin batter. Toss cauliflower until lightly coated.

### 02. Air fry.
Arrange in a single layer and cook at 390°F for 14–16 minutes, shaking once, until browned at the tips.

### 03. Sauce.
Toss hot cauliflower with Buffalo sauce and butter, then return to the basket for 3–4 minutes to set the glaze.

### 04. Serve.
Pile onto a plate with celery and dressing while the edges are still crisp.

## Notes

For a drier, crispier bite, sauce only half the cauliflower and serve extra Buffalo sauce at the table.
