Taquitos solve dinner by behaving like snacks. They are crisp enough for fingers, substantial enough for a plate, and friendly to every topping in the fridge. The air fryer is good at this specific magic: tight rolled tortillas that brown without turning greasy.

A smear of beans acts like glue, the beef brings the spice, and cheese makes the whole thing behave. Make a double batch if your household has teenagers or sports on television.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 cup refried beans
- 1 cup shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack
- 10 small flour or corn tortillas
- Cooking spray
- Salsa, sour cream, and shredded lettuce, for serving

## Method

### 01. Cook the beef.
Brown the beef in a skillet with chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt. Drain if needed.

### 02. Fill the tortillas.
Warm tortillas until flexible. Spread each with beans, add beef and cheese, then roll tightly seam-side down.

### 03. Crisp the taquitos.
Mist with cooking spray and air fry at 390°F for 7–9 minutes, turning once, until browned and crisp.

### 04. Serve with cold things.
Pile onto a plate with salsa, sour cream, lettuce, and whatever hot sauce is currently winning.

## Notes

Corn tortillas taste best but crack if cold. Warm them under a damp towel first. These freeze well before cooking; add 3–4 minutes from frozen.
