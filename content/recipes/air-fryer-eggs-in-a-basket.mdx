This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Toast with a soft egg set into the middle — diner breakfast, basket edition.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sturdy bread
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp softened butter
- Salt and pepper
- Chives or hot sauce, optional

## Method

### 01. Cut bread.
Cut a hole from the center of each slice. Butter both sides lightly.

### 02. Start toast.
Air fry bread and cutouts at 350°F for 3 minutes.

### 03. Add eggs.
Crack an egg into each hole, season, and cook 5–7 minutes more, until whites set.

### 04. Serve.
Lift out carefully and finish with chives or hot sauce.

## Notes

Use parchment only if your air fryer manual allows it, and never without food weighing it down.
