Chicken stuffed with creamed corn, wrapped in bacon — that's the kind
of recipe combination that sounds slightly mad on paper and turns
out, in practice, to be one of the better weeknight dinners you'll
make. The creamed corn (Australian tinned creamed corn, which most
international supermarkets carry) provides the moisture and binding
that keeps the chicken from drying out. The cheese melts down through
the corn. The bacon wraps the whole package and provides salt, fat,
and visual drama when sliced.

The technique requires butterflying the chicken (a horizontal cut
that opens the breast like a book), filling it, rolling, and wrapping
in bacon. The bacon does double duty — securing the roll and providing
its own crust. No toothpicks needed if the bacon overlap holds.

Air-fry at 400°F for 18–22 minutes, until the bacon is deeply crisp
and the chicken reads 165°F internally. Rest, slice across, plate
with relish or chutney.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken:**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6 oz / 170g each)
- 8 slices streaky bacon
- 1 cup (250g) tinned creamed corn
- ½ cup (50g) grated tasty cheese (sharp cheddar)
- ¼ cup (25g) grated parmesan
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For serving:**
- Tomato chutney or relish
- Mixed leaves dressed with lemon juice

## Method

### 01. Make the filling.
In a bowl, combine creamed corn, both cheeses, parsley, garlic, salt,
and pepper. Set aside.

### 02. Butterfly the chicken.
Place each breast on a cutting board. Holding it flat with one hand,
slice horizontally into the thickest side, stopping about ½ inch
before cutting through. Open like a book.

### 03. Stuff.
Spoon a quarter of the corn-cheese mixture onto one side of each
butterflied breast. Fold the top over to close.

### 04. Wrap with bacon.
Lay 2 bacon slices side-by-side, slightly overlapping. Place a
stuffed chicken breast on top and wrap the bacon around the chicken,
tucking the ends underneath. Repeat with the other breasts.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the wrapped chicken
seam-side-down in the basket, spacing apart. Cook 18–22 minutes
until the bacon is deeply crisp and the chicken reads 165°F (74°C)
at the thickest part.

### 06. Rest and slice.
Move to a board, rest 5 minutes. Slice each parcel into 3–4 thick
rounds to show the filling.

### 07. Serve.
Plate with a spoonful of tomato chutney and a small handful of
dressed leaves.

## Notes

For more contrast: add finely chopped chives or jalapeños to the
filling. For a smokier version: use smoked bacon and smoked cheddar.
The parcels can be assembled up to 6 hours ahead and refrigerated;
add 3 minutes to the cook time. Leftovers reheat well — slice cold
the next day for sandwiches with crusty bread and pickled red onion.
