This is the dinner I make on the nights I want something that feels
like effort but actually isn't. Cauliflower and shrimp cook at the
same rate in the air fryer (about 10 minutes), so you toss them
both with the same lemon-garlic-parsley dressing and cook them
together. The cauliflower goes nutty and charred at the edges; the
shrimp pick up the herb-flecked oil and turn pink and curled. The
whole thing tastes Mediterranean-bright and feels intentionally
composed rather than thrown together — which it absolutely was.

The cauliflower technique is worth knowing on its own. Florets
tossed in oil and air-fried at 400°F for 10–12 minutes develop the
same browned-and-tender quality as oven-roasted cauliflower, but
faster, in a single basket, with cleaner edges. The high heat pulls
moisture off the surface quickly so the florets caramelise rather
than steam.

The photo shows the assembled dish: a generous pile of cauliflower
florets on a white plate, two wooden skewers of shrimp resting on
top (about 3 shrimp per skewer), the whole thing flecked with
chopped parsley and visible red chilli flakes. A yellow-striped
napkin on the side. This is a complete dinner — protein, vegetable,
fat, acid, herb — on one plate, with nothing else strictly
required.

For a complete meal, serve over couscous or with crusty bread for
mopping. For a low-carb version, the cauliflower IS the carb.

## Ingredients

**For the cauliflower:**
- 1 medium head cauliflower (about 1½ lb / 700g), cut into 1-inch
  florets
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp black pepper

**For the shrimp:**
- 1 lb (450g) large shrimp (16/20 count), peeled and deveined,
  tail-on
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

**To finish:**
- 3 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 4 wooden skewers, soaked in water for 10 minutes

## Method

### 01. Soak the skewers.
If using wooden skewers, soak in cold water 10 minutes so they
don't scorch.

### 02. Season the cauliflower.
In a large bowl, toss the cauliflower florets with olive oil,
minced garlic, salt, red pepper flakes, and black pepper.

### 03. Season the shrimp.
In a separate bowl, toss the shrimp with olive oil, lemon zest,
lemon juice, minced garlic, and salt.

### 04. Thread the shrimp.
Thread 3–4 shrimp onto each skewer, alternating direction so
each shrimp lies flat.

### 05. Air fry together.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Add the cauliflower in a
single layer in the basket. Lay the shrimp skewers across the
top, resting on the basket walls if possible. Cook 10–12 minutes,
shaking the cauliflower at the 6-minute mark (skewers stay put),
until the cauliflower edges are deeply caramelised and the shrimp
are just opaque.

### 06. Plate.
Tip the cauliflower onto a serving platter. Place the shrimp
skewers on top. Scatter chopped parsley over everything and
arrange lemon wedges around the plate.

### 07. Serve.
Eat immediately. The shrimp lose their snap if held too long.

## Notes

For a vegetarian version, swap the shrimp for thick slices of
halloumi (½-inch slabs) — cook for the same time, no skewer needed.
For a fuller meal, serve over couscous or saffron rice. For a one-
pot variation, skip the skewers entirely and toss the shrimp loose
into the basket on top of the cauliflower for the last 6 minutes
(easier, less photogenic). The leftover cauliflower without the
shrimp is excellent cold the next day in a salad or tossed with
tahini and lemon.
