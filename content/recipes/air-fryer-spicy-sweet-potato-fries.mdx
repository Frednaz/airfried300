This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. A second sweet potato fry article, this one warmer and spicier: paprika, cayenne, and a quick cornstarch trick for better edges.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into fries
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- Lime wedges, for serving

## Method

### 01. Soak briefly.
Soak cut fries in cold water for 20 minutes if you have time, then dry extremely well.

### 02. Dust.
Toss with cornstarch first, then oil, spices, and salt.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 380°F for 16–20 minutes in batches, shaking every 6 minutes.

### 04. Serve.
Finish with lime and another pinch of salt while hot.

## Notes

Sweet potato fries will never be as rigid as russet fries; the goal is browned edges and a creamy center.
