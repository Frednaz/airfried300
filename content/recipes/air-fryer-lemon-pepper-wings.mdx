## Ingredients

- 1 lb main ingredient from the photo family
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Finishing sauce, citrus, herbs, or cheese

## Method

### 01. Prep.
Pat everything dry and cut pieces evenly so they cook at the same pace. Preheat the air fryer if your model benefits from it.

### 02. Season.
Toss with oil, salt, and seasonings until lightly coated. The food should look glossy, not wet.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook in a single layer at 375°F to 400°F, shaking or turning once, until browned at the edges and cooked through.

### 04. Finish.
Add sauces, cheese, herbs, citrus, or crunchy toppings while hot, then serve immediately.

## Notes

This is a variant recipe, so use the photo as the texture guide: browned edges, not dried-out middles. Reduce cook time for smaller pieces and add a few minutes for a crowded basket.
