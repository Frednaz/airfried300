The beef-and-broccoli stir-fry is the lunchtime takeaway you
order when you can't decide what else to have. It's reliable,
balanced, and comes in a small white box. Making it at home in
the air fryer means cleaner flavours and proper texture — the
beef gets a real sear on every face instead of steaming, the
broccoli stays toothsome instead of waterlogged, and the
vegetables come out crisp-tender at the edges.

The trick is to cook everything separately or in stages. Beef
strips first (5 minutes at 200°C) — they need direct heat to
caramelise. Then the vegetables on top of the beef for the final
3 minutes — broccoli florets, baby corn, carrot ribbons. Toss
everything hot into a pan with cooked noodles and the sauce
(soy, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, a touch of honey). Stir for 60
seconds — done.

## Ingredients

**For the stir-fry:**
- 12 oz (340g) flank or skirt steak, cut against the grain into
  ¼-inch strips
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets (about 2 cups)
- 1 large carrot, peeled into ribbons with a vegetable peeler
- 6 baby corn cobs, halved lengthwise
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For the sauce:**
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (or extra light + 1 tsp molasses)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1-inch piece ginger, finely grated
- ½ tsp white pepper
- ½ tsp red chilli flakes (optional)
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp water (to thicken)

**To serve:**
- 8 oz (225g) dry medium egg noodles
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method

### 01. Cook the noodles.
Bring a pot of water to a boil. Cook the noodles 2 minutes less
than the package says. Drain, rinse briefly under cold water to
stop cooking, toss with a teaspoon of oil to prevent sticking.

### 02. Air fry the beef.
Toss the beef strips with 1 tbsp neutral oil, salt, and pepper.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook the beef in a single
layer for 5 minutes, shaking once, until the edges are deeply
browned.

### 03. Add the vegetables.
Scatter the broccoli florets, carrot ribbons, and baby corn over
the beef. Cook 3 more minutes — vegetables should be crisp-tender,
slightly charred at the edges.

### 04. Make the sauce.
While the vegetables cook, whisk together all sauce ingredients
except the cornflour slurry.

### 05. Combine.
Heat a large skillet or wok over medium-high. Tip in the cooked
noodles and the beef-vegetable mixture. Pour over the sauce, then
add the cornflour slurry. Toss for 60–90 seconds — the sauce will
thicken and coat everything in glossy lacquer.

### 06. Serve.
Divide between two bowls. Top with spring onions and sesame
seeds. Squeeze lime over the top right before eating.

## Notes

For a vegetarian version, swap the beef for extra-firm tofu
(pressed and cubed) — air-fry first for 12 minutes at 400°F. For
chicken thigh strips, same technique, same timing. The dark soy
gives the noodles their classic brown colour; if you only have
light soy, add 1 tsp of molasses or maple syrup. Leftover stir-
fry keeps 2 days but reheats poorly; eat fresh.
