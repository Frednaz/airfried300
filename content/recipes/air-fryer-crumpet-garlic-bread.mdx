This is the kind of recipe that sounds like a TikTok prank and turns
out to be one of the better garlic-bread inventions of the decade.
Take a baguette. Split it lengthwise. Slather both cut faces with
garlic butter. Now press crumpets (yes, crumpets — the spongy British
griddle cakes with the characteristic holes on top) onto the buttered
bread, holes facing up. Brush more garlic butter into the crumpet
holes. Top with mozzarella, parmesan, and a herb-flecked cheddar.
Air-fry until the cheese melts down into every crumpet hole and the
bread underneath is properly toasted.

The result is structurally absurd and practically genius. The crumpets
act as cheese cups — the holes catch the melted cheese and create
hundreds of cheese-stretch moments per slice. The garlic butter
soaks into both the crumpet and the bread. The whole thing is more
texture and more flavour than standard garlic bread.

The photo shows the finished loaf in a foil packet on the bench: the
cheese a deep amber, garlic butter pooled at the edges, herb flecks
visible across the top, the surface slightly crispy where the cheese
caught direct heat. A pull-apart structural marvel.

## Ingredients

**For the garlic butter:**
- 8 tbsp (115g) salted butter, softened
- 6 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 3 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp flaky salt

**For the loaf:**
- 1 medium baguette (about 16 inches long)
- 4 small crumpets
- 1½ cups (170g) grated mozzarella
- 1 cup (110g) grated cheddar
- ¼ cup (25g) grated parmesan

**To finish:**
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Cracked black pepper

## Method

### 01. Mix the garlic butter.
In a bowl, beat the softened butter with garlic, parsley, olive oil,
oregano, red pepper flakes, and salt until uniform.

### 02. Split and butter the baguette.
Slice the baguette in half lengthwise. Place both halves cut-side-up
on a piece of foil that fits your air fryer. Smear each half
generously with garlic butter — use about half the total.

### 03. Add the crumpets.
Place 2 crumpets on top of each baguette half, holes facing up,
pressing them into the buttered bread. Brush more garlic butter
over the crumpets, working some into the holes.

### 04. Top with cheese.
Mix the mozzarella, cheddar, and parmesan. Pile generously over the
crumpets, pushing cheese down into the holes. Make sure to cover
all the way.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Place the foil-lined loaf in
the basket (work in batches if needed). Cook 8–10 minutes until the
cheese is deeply melted, bubbling, and golden at the edges.

### 06. Rest briefly.
Pull out and let rest 90 seconds — the cheese is molten and will
firm slightly.

### 07. Slice and serve.
Cut into thick pull-apart slices through both crumpet and baguette.
Scatter parsley and cracked pepper. Eat immediately.

## Notes

For an extra rich version, add 4 oz of crisp pancetta cubes between
the cheese and crumpets. For heat: swap the red pepper flakes for
finely chopped pickled jalapeños. The crumpets are non-substitutable
— they're what give the dish its character. Without crumpets, you've
just made standard garlic bread. The leftover slices (rare) reheat
passably for 3 minutes at 380°F.
