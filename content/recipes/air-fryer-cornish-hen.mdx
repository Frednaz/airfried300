This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. A tiny roast chicken moment: crisp skin, tender meat, and dinner for two from one basket.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 Cornish hen, about 1 1/2 lb
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 lemon wedge
- Rosemary or thyme sprig

## Method

### 01. Season.
Pat the hen dry. Rub with oil, salt, paprika, garlic powder, and pepper.

### 02. Start breast-side down.
Cook at 360°F breast-side down for 20 minutes.

### 03. Flip.
Turn breast-side up and cook 12–18 minutes more, until thigh reaches 165°F.

### 04. Rest.
Rest 10 minutes before splitting. Squeeze lemon over the top.

## Notes

Dry skin is everything. Leave the hen uncovered in the fridge for an hour if you have time.
