Taquitos are the dish to make when you have leftover cooked chicken and
no plan. Shredded rotisserie chicken, a heavy hand with cumin and
chili powder, a cup of cheese for the binder, soft flour tortillas
rolled tight enough to stay closed during cooking. The air fryer does
in eight minutes what the deep-fryer used to do in three — but without
the splatter, the oil bath, or the inevitable burning of one of them
while the others were still pale.

The photo shows the finished plate: two taquitos on shredded romaine,
chunks of fresh tomato, a heavy dollop of guacamole in the middle, a
small ramekin of more guac on the side. The taquitos themselves are
golden-edged, slightly blistered from the air, just a little oil sheen
where they brushed the basket. This is the order in which they
disappear: the ends first (the cheese gets caramelised against the
basket), the middle last. Two per person is dinner with a salad; three
is dinner without.

A note on the tortilla: this works best with FLOUR tortillas, not corn.
Corn cracks when rolled. Flour bends. If you want corn flavour, brush
the rolled flour taquitos with melted butter and dust with cornmeal
before frying — close enough.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken (rotisserie or leftover)
- 1 cup shredded Mexican blend cheese (or jack + cheddar)
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened (the glue)
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning (or 1 tsp chili powder + ½ tsp cumin + ½ tsp
  garlic powder + ½ tsp salt)
- 2 tbsp salsa
- 8 small flour tortillas (6-inch street-taco size)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for brushing)
- Guacamole, to serve
- Diced tomato, to serve
- Shredded romaine or cilantro, to serve

## Method

### 01. Make the filling.
In a bowl, mash together the shredded chicken, cream cheese, shredded
cheese, taco seasoning, and salsa until uniform. The cream cheese is
what holds it inside the rolled tortilla — don't skip it.

### 02. Warm the tortillas.
Wrap the tortillas in a damp paper towel and microwave for 30 seconds.
This makes them pliable for rolling without cracking. (If you're
patient, lay them on a dry skillet for 10 seconds each side over
medium heat — better flavour, more steps.)

### 03. Fill and roll.
Lay a warm tortilla flat. Spoon 2½ tablespoons of filling in a thin
line down the center. Roll tightly from one edge to the other, seam-
side down. Repeat with all 8.

### 04. Brush and air fry.
Brush each rolled taquito lightly with olive oil. Heat the air fryer
to 400°F (200°C). Place the taquitos seam-side down in a single layer
(work in two batches if needed). Cook 7–9 minutes, flipping halfway,
until golden and crisp on the outside.

### 05. Serve.
Plate on a bed of shredded romaine, top with diced tomato and a heavy
dollop of guacamole. Add a ramekin of more guacamole on the side.

## Notes

To freeze: roll, brush with oil, and freeze on a sheet pan before
bagging. Cook from frozen at 400°F for 10–12 minutes. The filling
adapts cheerfully: shredded beef + cotija (beef taquitos), black beans
+ corn + jack (vegetarian), shredded pork + green sauce (al pastor-
adjacent). Sour cream and salsa verde on the side are also welcome.
