The prawn taco is one of those dishes that punches several weight
classes above its preparation difficulty. Twenty minutes of work
delivers something that tastes like the kind of beach-shack lunch
you'd write home about — and the home version costs about a third
what you'd pay at the restaurant. The air fryer makes it even
faster, because cooking prawns properly is the only tricky bit and
the air fryer handles that perfectly.

Prawns (or shrimp, in American English) cook in six minutes flat in
the basket at 200°C. They go from translucent grey to opaque pink
with a slightly curled shape; pull them at exactly that point or
they go rubbery. The spice rub — paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic,
chili — develops a slight char on the outside that adds smokiness
without needing actual smoke.

The photo shows the assembly: two soft flour tortillas slightly
overlapping on a metal serving plate, piled with spiced prawns,
sliced avocado, sliced beetroot (the magenta visible against the
green and pink), torn fresh coriander, lime wedges scattered, and
a small bowl of the yogurt sauce — pale, dotted with green herbs.
The contrast is the dish — heat from the prawns, cool from the
yogurt and avocado, citrus from the lime, earthy sweetness from
the beetroot.

## Ingredients

**For the prawns:**
- 1 lb (450g) large raw prawns (shrimp), peeled, deveined, tail-off
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp chili powder (or cayenne for more heat)
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For the lime-yogurt sauce:**
- ½ cup (120ml) plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 small clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp fresh coriander, finely chopped
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

**To assemble:**
- 8 small soft flour tortillas (or corn, if you prefer)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 small cooked beetroot, sliced (or quick-pickled red onion)
- ½ cup fresh coriander leaves, torn
- 2 limes, cut into wedges
- Hot sauce, optional

## Method

### 01. Mix the spice rub.
In a bowl, combine the olive oil, paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic
powder, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Whisk into a loose paste.

### 02. Toss the prawns.
Pat the prawns very dry with paper towels. Add to the spice paste
and toss until every prawn is evenly coated.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spread the prawns in a single
layer in the basket. Cook 5–7 minutes, shaking once halfway through,
until just opaque and curled. Don't overcook — they go rubbery fast.

### 04. Make the sauce.
While the prawns cook, whisk together the Greek yogurt, lime juice,
grated garlic, chopped coriander, and salt in a small bowl.

### 05. Warm the tortillas.
Stack the tortillas, cover with a damp paper towel, microwave 30
seconds. (Or warm each briefly over a gas burner for proper char
marks.)

### 06. Assemble.
Lay 2 tortillas per plate. Pile 4–5 prawns in each, layer avocado
and beetroot slices, scatter coriander leaves, drizzle with yogurt
sauce. Serve with lime wedges and hot sauce alongside.

## Notes

For meal-prep: cook the prawns and make the sauce in advance — store
separately. Reheat the prawns briefly in the air fryer (90 seconds
at 360°F), warm the tortillas, assemble fresh. Beetroot is the
unusual choice in the photo and it's worth it for the colour and
sweetness; if you can't be bothered, quick-pickled red onion is the
standard sub (1 small red onion sliced thin, covered with white
vinegar + 1 tsp sugar + ½ tsp salt for 15 minutes). For corn-tortilla
purists: yes, they're more authentic; warm them on a dry skillet 30
seconds per side until pliable.
