This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Folded tortilla packets with seasoned beef, cheese, tostada crunch, lettuce, and tomato, crisped seam-side down.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning
- 4 large burrito tortillas
- 4 tostada shells
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- Cooking spray

## Method

### 01. Cook filling.
Brown beef with taco seasoning and a splash of water until glossy.

### 02. Layer.
Add beef and cheese to each tortilla, top with a tostada, sour cream, lettuce, and tomato.

### 03. Fold.
Fold edges toward the center, overlapping to seal. Place seam-side down.

### 04. Air fry.
Mist with spray and cook at 375°F for 6–8 minutes, turning once if needed, until crisp.

## Notes

If tortillas are not large enough, patch the center with a small extra tortilla circle before folding.
