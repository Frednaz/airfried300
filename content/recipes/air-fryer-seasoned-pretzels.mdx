This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Crunchy pretzels warmed with ranch seasoning, garlic, dill, and butter until they taste like a party bowl.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 6 cups mini pretzels
- 3 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tbsp ranch seasoning
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried dill
- Pinch cayenne, optional

## Method

### 01. Season.
Toss pretzels with melted butter, ranch seasoning, garlic powder, dill, and cayenne.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 300°F for 5–7 minutes, shaking twice, until fragrant.

### 03. Cool.
Spread on a tray to cool so they crisp fully.

### 04. Store.
Transfer to an airtight container once completely cool.

## Notes

This is a snack recipe, not a cooking challenge. The low temperature keeps the seasoning from scorching.
