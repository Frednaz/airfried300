Baked oats are the breakfast that the internet turned into a category.
Originally it was a TikTok thing — blend oats and a banana, bake into
something between a soft cookie and a slice of cake, eat warm with a
spoon — and like a lot of things the internet turned into a category,
it's actually good. The basic recipe is genuinely useful: it takes
ingredients you have, requires no special equipment besides a blender (or
a fork and some patience), and produces something more interesting than
toast for the same amount of effort.

The air fryer makes it better. Twelve minutes instead of twenty-five in
the oven, no preheating a whole oven for one ramekin, and the dry heat
gives the top a real golden crust that the oven won't quite match. The
inside stays soft, almost custardy near the bottom. It's a single
serving — one ramekin, one person — but you can bake three at a time if
your basket fits them, which is exactly what the photo shows.

The base recipe is one mashed banana, half a cup of oats, an egg, a
splash of milk, a teaspoon of baking powder, cinnamon, and a pinch of
salt. The toppings are where you decide what kind of morning you're
having. Berries if you want fruit-forward and tart. Dates and peanut
butter if you want something denser and more dessert-like. Chocolate
chips and peanut butter if you've given up pretending breakfast isn't
just a smaller version of dessert (which, honestly, is fine).

## Ingredients

**Base (per ramekin):**
- ½ cup rolled oats
- 1 ripe banana, mashed (about ½ cup)
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp milk (any)
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

**Berry topping:**
- ¼ cup mixed berries (strawberries quartered, blueberries whole)

**Date + peanut butter topping:**
- 2 Medjool dates, pitted and chopped
- 1 tbsp creamy peanut butter, warmed (to drizzle)
- 1 tbsp chopped walnuts (optional)

**Chocolate chip + peanut butter topping:**
- 2 tbsp mini chocolate chips
- 1 tbsp creamy peanut butter, warmed (to drizzle)

## Method

### 01. Blend or mash.
If you have a blender, throw everything for the base in and pulse until
mostly smooth (a few oat flecks are fine). If you don't, mash the banana
hard with a fork, then whisk in the egg, milk, syrup, vanilla, then fold
in the oats, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.

### 02. Pour and top.
Grease a small (6–8 oz) ramekin with a little butter or oil. Pour in the
batter. Press your chosen topping into the surface — for berries, push
them halfway in; for dates or chocolate chips, scatter on top.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Place the ramekin in the basket and
cook for 12–14 minutes, until the top is golden and a skewer comes out
mostly clean.

### 04. Finish.
For the peanut butter toppings: warm a tablespoon of peanut butter for
10 seconds in the microwave and drizzle over the hot oats. Eat with a
spoon, directly from the ramekin, while it's still warm.

## Notes

Scaling up: three ramekins fit in most baskets — same time, same temp.
For meal prep, bake three at once on Sunday and reheat in the air fryer
for 3 minutes at 300°F. The texture isn't as good cold but it's
acceptable if you're in a hurry. Use frozen berries if it's not summer —
no need to thaw, just add an extra 2 minutes to the cooking time.
