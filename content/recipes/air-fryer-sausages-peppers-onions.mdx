The sausage-and-peppers sub is the Italian-American answer to the
cheesesteak: a long roll, something cooked in a pan, melted cheese,
nothing on the table that doesn't fit between two halves of bread. In
its native habitat — a bocce court fundraiser, a feast-day street fair,
a New Jersey diner at 1am — it's made in a giant flat-top skillet that's
been crusted with thirty years of sausage drippings. The air fryer is a
poor cosmetic approximation but a good functional one: the sausages
crisp, the peppers char at the edges, the onions caramelise enough,
the rolls toast in their own dedicated minute.

The photo is the assembled version: sub roll split open, sausage links
visible through the pile of red-and-green pepper strips, provolone
melting down the side, onions tucked into the crevices. You eat this
holding it with both hands, leaning forward over a plate that's going
to catch the drips. Two per person if it's a real lunch; one is dinner.

A note on the sausage: sweet Italian works, hot Italian works (and is
what you actually want if you like it hot), chicken sausage works for
the lighter version. Don't pre-cook them on the stove; they finish
perfectly in the air fryer in the same window as the vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 4 fresh Italian sausage links (sweet or hot)
- 1 red bell pepper, cored and cut into ½-inch strips
- 1 green bell pepper, cored and cut into ½-inch strips
- 1 large yellow onion, cut into ½-inch slices
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 4 sub rolls (6-inch hoagie rolls, soft)
- 4 slices provolone cheese
- 1 tbsp fresh basil, torn (optional)

## Method

### 01. Toss the vegetables.
In a bowl, toss the pepper strips, onion slices, olive oil, Italian
seasoning, salt, and pepper.

### 02. Load the basket.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the sausages in the basket,
then scatter the peppers and onions around and on top of them. It's
fine if they overlap; they'll redistribute.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook 12–14 minutes, shaking the basket twice — once at 5 minutes, once
at 9 minutes. The sausages should reach 160°F (71°C) internally, with
deep-browned exteriors. The peppers should be soft with charred edges.

### 04. Toast the rolls.
Slice the sausages on the bias into ½-inch pieces. Split the rolls
open (but not all the way through). Lay a slice of provolone in each
roll. Stack the sausage and peppers on top.

### 05. Finish in the basket.
Place the assembled subs back in the air fryer for 2 minutes at 380°F,
just until the cheese melts and the rolls toast slightly. Scatter
basil over the top if using and serve immediately.

## Notes

For a sheet-pan version (no air fryer): same components, 425°F oven for
22 minutes, finish the cheese under the broiler for 60 seconds. Pickled
banana peppers on top are not traditional but very welcome. The peppers
keep 3 days in the fridge and reheat in the air fryer for 3 minutes —
they're also good cold over salad the next day.
