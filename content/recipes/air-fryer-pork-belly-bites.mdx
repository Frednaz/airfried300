Pork belly is the cut that rewards heat. The fat renders and crisps
the skin (or in this case the cubed edges); the meat stays tender
and rich. Cubed and air-fried, pork belly becomes a perfect bite-
sized appetiser — every cube has crisp surfaces and a juicy interior.

The technique: cube the pork belly into ¾-inch pieces, toss with
five-spice and salt, air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes. The fat renders
during cooking and self-bastes the cubes. Pull them out, toss in a
quick sticky glaze (honey, soy, rice vinegar, garlic), return for 2
minutes to caramelise. The glaze becomes lacquer.

The photo shows the cubes piled on a metal tray: deeply browned with
visible glossy glaze, scattered sesame seeds across the top, a small
bowl of extra glaze on the side. Pub-food aesthetic but at home.

## Ingredients

**For the pork belly:**
- 1½ lb (700g) skinless pork belly, cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 1 tsp Chinese five-spice
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp white pepper

**For the sticky glaze:**
- ¼ cup honey
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (for colour)
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes

**To finish:**
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method

### 01. Season.
In a bowl, toss the pork-belly cubes with five-spice, salt, and white
pepper.

### 02. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the cubes in a single
layer in the basket. Cook 18 minutes, shaking every 6 minutes — the
fat will render and self-baste the meat.

### 03. Make the glaze.
While the pork cooks, whisk honey, both soy sauces, rice vinegar,
garlic, ginger, sesame oil, and red pepper flakes in a small bowl.

### 04. Toss and finish.
After 18 minutes, transfer the pork to a bowl. Pour over the glaze
and toss to coat. Return to the air fryer for 2 more minutes — the
glaze will caramelise.

### 05. Plate.
Tip onto a serving plate. Scatter sesame seeds and spring onions.
Serve with lime wedges for squeezing.

## Notes

For party-portion-sized bites: cut the cubes smaller (½-inch). Cook
14 minutes instead of 18. For a five-spice-only version (no sticky
glaze): season as in step 1 and serve plain with the lime wedges —
the rendered fat and crisp edges are excellent on their own. The
pork-belly cubes keep 3 days; reheat in the air fryer for 4 minutes
at 400°F. For a meal: serve over jasmine rice with steamed bok choy.
