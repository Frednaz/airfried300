The stuffed chicken breast is the dish you make when you want to feel
like you cooked something. It's the dish whose photo on a recipe site
makes you think "okay, that's actually a meal." It's also, contrary to
its plated impressiveness, not that hard — there are exactly three
techniques: butterfly the breast, stuff it, cook it. The whole operation
is twenty minutes hands-on, and the air fryer handles the unattended
part better than the oven because it browns the outside while the
filling stays creamy.

The filling here is sun-dried tomato, spinach, garlic, and cream cheese
— Italian-leaning, savoury without being heavy. You can see it in the
cross-section in the photo: the dark red flecks of sun-dried tomato,
the green from the spinach, the pale cream-cheese binder holding it
together. Basil scattered on top for the green flecks and the smell.

A note on the chicken: you want thick breasts (8 oz each, ideally) so
they don't dry out during the longer cook needed to heat the filling
through. Butterflying — slicing horizontally most of the way through —
creates a pocket without breaking the breast in half. If you're nervous
about cutting evenly, ask the butcher to butterfly them for you; they
will, cheerfully, in ten seconds.

## Ingredients

**For the filling:**
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed), drained and chopped
- 1 cup fresh spinach, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ cup grated parmesan
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped (plus more to garnish)

**For the chicken:**
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 8 oz each)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method

### 01. Make the filling.
In a bowl, stir together the cream cheese, sun-dried tomatoes, spinach,
garlic, parmesan, salt, red pepper flakes, and basil until uniform.

### 02. Butterfly the chicken.
Place each breast on a cutting board. Holding it flat with one hand,
slice horizontally into the thickest side, stopping about ½ inch
before cutting all the way through. Open like a book.

### 03. Stuff.
Spoon half the filling onto one side of each butterflied breast. Fold
the top half over to close. Secure with toothpicks at the edges if
needed — three per breast usually does it.

### 04. Season the outside.
Rub each stuffed breast with olive oil, then sprinkle with Italian
seasoning, salt, and pepper.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Place the breasts in the basket
with space between them — they shouldn't touch. Cook 22–25 minutes,
flipping once at the 12-minute mark, until the chicken reads 165°F
(74°C) at the thickest point and the filling is hot.

### 06. Rest and serve.
Move to a plate and rest 5 minutes — the filling firms slightly and
the juices redistribute. Remove the toothpicks. Slice across the grain
into 1-inch medallions to expose the filling. Scatter fresh basil over
the top.

## Notes

The filling can be made up to 2 days ahead and refrigerated; bring to
room temperature before stuffing. For a lower-fat version, swap half
the cream cheese for ricotta. Other filling combos that work the same
way: feta + olives + oregano (Greek), goat cheese + roasted peppers +
thyme (Provençal), pesto + mozzarella (caprese-adjacent). Serve over
orzo (as shown) or any pasta, with a side of roasted zucchini.
