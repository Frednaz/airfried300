The chicken thigh is the chicken breast's better-cooking sibling. More
fat, more flavour, more forgiveness — if you overshoot the cooking
time by three minutes, the thigh is still tender, whereas the breast
would be jerky. Bone-in, skin-on is the gold-standard cut: the bone
slows the cooking enough that the inside stays moist while the outside
crisps, and the skin renders into something approaching the crackling
on a Sunday roast.

This is the thigh recipe that works as a weeknight default. The herb-
and-paprika rub is straightforward — nothing exotic, just the spices
you have in the cupboard. The vegetables go in the basket alongside,
catching the rendered chicken fat as it drips. The whole operation is
twenty-five minutes unattended.

the vegetables — they're glossy and slightly burnt at the tips,
which is exactly the point.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken:**
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1½ lb / 700g total)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme (or 2 tsp fresh)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp onion powder

**For the vegetables:**
- 1 lb (450g) baby potatoes, halved
- 2 carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 parsnip, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 small shallot, peeled and quartered
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp dried thyme

## Method

### 01. Dry the chicken.
Pat the chicken thighs very dry with paper towels — wet skin steams
instead of crisping. Place skin-side-up on a board.

### 02. Season.
Combine the paprika, garlic powder, thyme, salt, pepper, and onion
powder in a small bowl. Rub the chicken thighs all over with olive oil,
then sprinkle the spice rub generously on all sides, pressing it into
the skin.

### 03. Toss the vegetables.
In a bowl, toss the potatoes, carrots, parsnip, and shallot with the
olive oil, salt, and thyme.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the chicken thighs
skin-side-up in the basket. Tuck the vegetables around them in a
single layer (it's fine if there's some overlap — they'll redistribute
as they cook).

### 05. Cook.
Cook for 25–28 minutes, shaking the vegetables (but not the chicken)
at the 12-minute mark and again at 20. The chicken is done when the
skin is deeply crisp and the internal temperature reads 175°F (80°C)
at the thickest part of the thigh (thighs are forgiving — even at
185°F they stay juicy).

### 06. Rest and serve.
Move the chicken to plates, rest 5 minutes. Spoon the vegetables and
any pan juices around them. Eat hot.

## Notes

For larger thighs (over 8 oz each), extend the cooking time to 30
minutes. For boneless skinless thighs, reduce to 18–20 minutes.
The leftover rendered fat at the bottom of the basket is liquid gold —
spoon it over the vegetables before serving. For a lemon-herb version,
add the zest of one lemon to the rub and a few sprigs of rosemary
under the chicken before cooking.
