The potato pancake — latke in Yiddish, rösti in Swiss German, draniki
in Russian, deruny in Ukrainian — exists across half of Europe under
different names with the same fundamental technique: grated raw
potato, salted, squeezed dry, bound with egg, fried until crisp.
Traditionally cooked in a hot pan with a generous slick of oil. The
air fryer version uses about a quarter of that oil and produces
something with most of the same qualities.

The trick is moisture removal. Grated potato leaks water aggressively
— salt it for ten minutes, then squeeze hard in a tea towel. The
drier the surface, the crisper the result. Some recipes call for
adding flour or matzo meal to bind; this version uses just an egg
and a small amount of cornflour for the cleaner version.

The photo shows them stacked between sheets of parchment: small
golden pancakes with visibly lacy crisp edges, a bowl of sour cream
on the side, scattered cracked pepper. The kind of side dish that's
also a meal if you make enough.

## Ingredients

**For the pancakes:**
- 1½ lb (700g) Maris Piper or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and
  coarsely grated
- 1 tsp salt (for sweating)
- 1 small yellow onion, finely grated
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 2 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, finely sliced
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- Olive oil spray

**To serve:**
- ½ cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp chives, snipped
- Apple sauce (the other traditional accompaniment)
- Smoked salmon and dill (for the upscale version)

## Method

### 01. Sweat the potato.
Grate the potatoes. Toss with 1 tsp salt and let sit 10 minutes.

### 02. Squeeze dry.
Tip into a clean tea towel and squeeze hard over a bowl — you'll
get about ½ cup of liquid. Let the starch settle (3 minutes), then
carefully pour off the liquid, scraping the white starch back into
the squeezed potato.

### 03. Mix.
Combine squeezed potato with grated onion, egg, cornflour, chives,
pepper, and nutmeg. Mix gently until uniform.

### 04. Shape.
Scoop ¼ cup portions onto a parchment square that fits your air
fryer. Press each into a flat disc about ½-inch thick. Don't pack
too firmly.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray pancakes generously with
olive oil. Cook in a single layer for 14–16 minutes, flipping at
the 8-minute mark, until deeply golden on both faces.

### 06. Serve.
Top with a dollop of sour cream and a scatter of chives. Apple sauce
on the side for the traditional pairing.

## Notes

For sweet potato variation: substitute half the potatoes with grated
sweet potato. For a Korean-style pancake: omit nutmeg, add 2 tbsp
sliced kimchi to the mix. The pancakes reheat passably in the air
fryer for 3 minutes at 380°F. Best within an hour of cooking — they
soften as they sit. The starch step matters more than people think
— it's what binds the pancakes from the inside out without making
them dense.
