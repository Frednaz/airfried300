Standard potato salad — boiled potatoes, mayo, hard-boiled eggs, eaten
cold — has been the picnic side for decades and is, frankly, a bit
tired. This version reinvents the dish: baby potatoes are air-fried
until the cut faces caramelise, then tossed warm with crispy bacon
and a drizzle of ranch dressing. The cold-and-soggy texture is
replaced with hot-and-crisp.

The technique: halve the potatoes, toss with oil and salt, air-fry
at 200°C for 18 minutes until the cut sides are deeply golden. Cook
bacon in the same basket (8 minutes), crumble. Make a quick ranch
dressing (mayo + buttermilk + dill + chives + garlic + lemon). Toss
hot potatoes with bacon and ranch, scatter chives.

## Ingredients

**For the potatoes:**
- 1½ lb (700g) baby potatoes (any colour), halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder

**For the bacon:**
- 4 oz (110g) streaky bacon

**For the ranch dressing:**
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup buttermilk (or yogurt + 1 tsp lemon juice)
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**To finish:**
- 3 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- Cracked black pepper

## Method

### 01. Roast the potatoes.
Toss halved potatoes with oil, salt, paprika, and garlic powder.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook 16–20 minutes, shaking
once, until cut sides are deep golden.

### 02. Crisp the bacon.
In the last 8 minutes of cooking, add bacon strips to the basket
alongside the potatoes (or do separately). Crumble once cooked.

### 03. Make ranch dressing.
Whisk mayo, buttermilk, dill, chives, garlic, lemon, onion powder,
salt, and pepper until smooth.

### 04. Toss warm.
Move hot potatoes to a serving bowl. Add bacon crumbles. Drizzle
generously with ranch dressing and toss.

### 05. Finish.
Scatter remaining chives and cracked pepper. Serve immediately.

## Notes

For a healthier version: swap mayo for Greek yogurt in the ranch.
For a herbier version: add 2 tbsp chopped parsley + 1 tbsp tarragon
to the ranch. The dish can be served slightly cooled (warm rather
than hot) and works as a brought-to-a-BBQ side. Don't make ahead
fully — the potatoes lose their crisp once mixed with dressing.
Make the components, toss just before serving.
