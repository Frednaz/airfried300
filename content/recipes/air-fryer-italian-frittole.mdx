Frittole are small fried savoury bites that appear under different
names across Italy — frittelle, sfingi, polpette di zucchine — all
sharing the same basic structure: a vegetable, often zucchini,
mixed with eggs, breadcrumbs, herbs, and grated cheese, shaped into
small balls or patties, fried until golden.

This version uses grated zucchini (squeezed dry, of course), grated
parmesan, breadcrumbs, basil, mint, and a beaten egg to bind. Shape
small patties about the diameter of a 50p coin, air-fry at 200°C for
12 minutes. They come out crisp on the outside, soft and herbaceous
inside.

Serve with tomato chutney for the Sicilian-pub aesthetic, or with
a yogurt-mint dip for a Greek-leaning twist. They're equally good
warm or at room temperature, which makes them excellent picnic and
party food.

## Ingredients

**For the frittole:**
- 2 medium zucchini (about 1 lb / 450g), coarsely grated
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup (60g) panko breadcrumbs
- ⅔ cup (60g) grated parmesan
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Olive oil spray

**To serve:**
- ½ cup tomato chutney (jarred or homemade)
- Extra fresh basil

## Method

### 01. Squeeze the zucchini.
Toss grated zucchini with 1 tsp salt. Let sit 10 minutes. Squeeze
hard in a tea towel — should remove ½ cup of liquid.

### 02. Mix.
In a bowl, combine squeezed zucchini, breadcrumbs, parmesan, eggs,
garlic, basil, mint, oregano, red pepper flakes, and pepper.

### 03. Shape.
Form into 16 small patties, about 1½ inches diameter, ½-inch thick.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray with olive oil. Arrange
in a single layer (work in 2 batches if needed). Cook 12–14 minutes,
flipping halfway, until deeply golden.

### 05. Serve.
Pile on a plate, scatter fresh basil. Serve with tomato chutney.

## Notes

For meal prep: cook, cool, refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat in the
air fryer for 3 minutes at 380°F. For a sausage-leaning version:
fold in 4 oz crumbled cooked Italian sausage. For larger party
fritters: form 8 larger patties, cook 15 minutes. Best warm but
genuinely excellent room-temperature.
