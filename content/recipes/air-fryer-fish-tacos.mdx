This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Crisp fish pieces tucked into tortillas with cabbage, lime crema, and enough crunch to count as dinner.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb white fish, cut into strips
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup panko
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 8 tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 lime
- Salsa, for serving

## Method

### 01. Bread fish.
Coat fish in flour, egg, then panko mixed with spices and salt.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 400°F for 8–10 minutes, turning once, until crisp and flaky.

### 03. Make crema.
Stir sour cream with lime juice, zest, and a pinch of salt.

### 04. Build tacos.
Layer cabbage, fish, crema, and salsa into warm tortillas.

## Notes

Small fish pieces are easier to taco and crisp better than whole fillets.
