This is a deliberate variant article: same photographic world, different cooking promise. Salmon fillets glazed with soy, honey, ginger, and garlic until the top turns lacquered.

The air fryer is good at these repeatable little shifts — a different seasoning, a different sauce, a different finish — because the core technique stays stable while the eating experience changes.

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- Sesame seeds
- Green onion

## Method

### 01. Glaze.
Stir soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic, and vinegar.

### 02. Season.
Pat salmon dry and brush lightly with glaze.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 7–9 minutes, brushing once more halfway.

### 04. Finish.
Top with sesame and green onion.

## Notes

Use parchment if your air fryer allows it; sticky glaze likes to cling to the basket.
