The filled doughnut is the European cousin of the American
yeasted ring. Where the American doughnut shows off its glaze,
the European version hides its filling inside — a Polish pączki,
a German berliner, an Israeli sufganiyot, an Italian bombolone.
The format is the same: round ball, sugar-dusted exterior,
cream or jam interior, eaten with messy fingers.

This version uses whipped cottage cheese as the cream filling.
This sounds wrong but is correct. Cottage cheese blended smooth
with cream, sweetened with maple, becomes a thick mousse with
twice the protein of typical pastry cream. The texture is rich
without being heavy; the flavour is mild dairy with a slight
tang. Pair with a swirl of fresh strawberry compote and it's
genuinely delightful.

The dough is yeasted, soft, slightly enriched. Roll into balls
the size of tennis balls (about 60g each), proof briefly, air-fry
at 350°F for 8 minutes until golden and pillowy. Roll in caster
sugar while still warm. Cool completely, then fill — piped through
a small hole in the side.

## Ingredients

**For the doughnut dough:**
- 2 cups (250g) plain flour
- 2¼ tsp (1 sachet) instant yeast
- 3 tbsp (45g) caster sugar
- ½ tsp fine salt
- ½ cup (120ml) whole milk, warmed
- 3 tbsp (45g) melted unsalted butter
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**For the strawberry compote:**
- 1½ cups (200g) fresh or frozen strawberries, halved
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp vanilla extract

**For the whipped cottage cheese:**
- 1 cup (240g) full-fat cottage cheese
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ¼ cup (60ml) double cream
- Pinch of salt

**To finish:**
- ½ cup (100g) caster sugar (for rolling)
- Olive oil spray
- Fresh strawberries, halved (to garnish)

## Method

### 01. Mix the dough.
In a stand mixer with a dough hook, combine flour, yeast, sugar,
and salt. Add warm milk, melted butter, egg, vanilla. Knead 6
minutes until smooth and slightly tacky.

### 02. Proof.
Place in an oiled bowl, cover, leave in a warm spot 30 minutes
until almost doubled.

### 03. Shape.
Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces (about 60g each). Roll
each into a tight ball. Place on parchment, cover loosely,
leave 15 minutes to puff slightly.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Spray the doughnuts with
olive oil. Cook in batches in a single layer for 8 minutes,
flipping at the 4-minute mark, until deep golden.

### 05. Sugar coat.
While warm but not piping hot, roll each doughnut in caster
sugar until fully coated.

### 06. Make the strawberry compote.
In a small saucepan, combine strawberries, sugar, and lemon
juice. Simmer over medium-low heat for 8 minutes, mashing
gently with a spoon, until thickened. Stir in vanilla. Cool
completely.

### 07. Whip the cottage cheese.
In a food processor (or with an immersion blender), blend
cottage cheese with maple syrup and vanilla until silky-smooth
— about 90 seconds. Transfer to a bowl. Whip the double cream
to soft peaks separately, then fold into the cottage cheese.

### 08. Fill the doughnuts.
Once doughnuts are fully cool, poke a hole in the side of each
with the handle of a wooden spoon. Pipe in 1–2 tbsp of cottage-
cheese cream through a piping bag with a small round tip.
Layer in 1 tsp of strawberry compote (or pipe in alternating
swirls).

### 09. Serve.
Plate with fresh halved strawberries. Eat the same day.

## Notes

For a simpler version, fill with just strawberry jam (skip the
cream entirely). For a Polish pączki version, fill with prune
jam. The cottage cheese cream is excellent on its own — use
leftovers as a topping for granola or pancakes. The doughnuts
are best within 4 hours of filling; the cream softens the dough
over time. For a higher-protein version (we've already started),
swap the milk in the dough for skim milk and add 1 scoop of
vanilla protein powder to the cottage cheese cream.
