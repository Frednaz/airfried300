Fried ice cream is the dessert trick that seems impossible — how
do you fry ice cream without it melting? — and the answer is:
very, very quickly, with the ice cream frozen so cold it could
break a tooth. The coating crisps in 90 seconds in the air
fryer; the centre stays solid; the contrast between hot crisp
shell and cold creamy interior is the entire reason this dish
exists.

The technique requires advance planning. Scoop the ice cream
into balls, freeze on a tray for at least 2 hours (3 is better)
until they're rock-hard. Roll in beaten egg, then in crushed
cornflakes mixed with cinnamon sugar. Return to the freezer
another 30 minutes. Only then does the air-frying step happen.

The cornflake coating is the cheat. Crushed cornflakes have all
the qualities of a deep-fry coating — they go gold, they crackle
when heated, they hold their structure — without needing actual
oil. A light spray of butter at the end gives the coating its
final colour.

The chocolate sauce gets poured over right before serving so it
hardens slightly when it hits the cold ice cream — a small
chocolate-shell effect. Whipped cream and a maraschino cherry
are optional but encouraged.

## Ingredients

**For the ice cream balls:**
- 1 pint (480ml) good vanilla ice cream
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 3 cups (90g) cornflakes, crushed to small pieces (not powder)
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 tbsp butter, melted (for the final spray)

**For the chocolate sauce:**
- 4 oz (110g) dark chocolate (70%), chopped
- ¼ cup (60ml) double cream (heavy cream)
- 1 tbsp golden syrup or maple syrup
- Pinch of sea salt

**To serve:**
- Whipped cream
- Maraschino cherries (optional)

## Method

### 01. Scoop and pre-freeze.
Line a baking sheet with parchment. Using a large ice-cream
scoop, form 4 large balls of vanilla ice cream. Place on the
sheet and freeze for at least 2 hours, preferably 3 — they
need to be rock-hard.

### 02. Make the coating.
In a shallow bowl, combine the crushed cornflakes, sugar, and
cinnamon. In a separate bowl, beat the egg.

### 03. Coat the balls.
Working with one ball at a time (keep the rest in the freezer),
quickly roll in beaten egg, then press into the cornflake
mixture, turning to coat thickly. Return to the tray. Repeat.

### 04. Re-freeze.
Return the coated balls to the freezer for at least 30 minutes
— they need to be cold-as-iron when they hit the hot air.

### 05. Make the chocolate sauce.
Heat the cream and syrup in a saucepan until just bubbling
around the edges. Off heat, add the chopped chocolate and
salt, whisk until smooth and glossy. Keep warm.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray or brush the balls
with melted butter. Place in the basket with space between.
Cook for 90 seconds only — long enough for the coating to
crisp, fast enough that the centre stays solid.

### 07. Plate and serve.
Transfer immediately to plates. Pour warm chocolate sauce
over the top — it'll harden slightly on contact with the cold
ice cream. Add whipped cream and a cherry. Eat immediately.

## Notes

Vanilla is the classic but any flavour works — coffee is
excellent, butter pecan, chocolate. Don't use soft-serve or
lower-fat ice creams; they melt too fast. For a fancier
coating, mix the crushed cornflakes 50/50 with crushed shortbread
biscuits. Make ahead through step 4 — keep frozen up to 1 week.
The chocolate sauce can be made ahead and gently reheated. If
your ice cream feels too soft after the air-fryer step, you've
either cooked too long or didn't pre-freeze enough — return to
the freezer to firm up before serving.
