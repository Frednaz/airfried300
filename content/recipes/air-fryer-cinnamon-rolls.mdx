This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. A can-of-dough cinnamon roll method for mornings when the oven feels like too much commitment.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 can refrigerated cinnamon rolls with icing
- Cooking spray
- 1 tbsp melted butter, optional
- Pinch cinnamon, optional

## Method

### 01. Arrange.
Lightly spray the basket and place cinnamon rolls with space between them.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 330°F for 8–10 minutes, until puffed and golden on top.

### 03. Check centers.
If the centers still look doughy, cook 1–2 minutes more at 320°F.

### 04. Ice.
Brush with butter if using, then drizzle with icing while warm.

## Notes

Lower heat is better here. Too hot browns the outside before the center cooks through.
