This is a deliberate variant article: same photographic world, different cooking promise. Simple chicken breast with lemon, thyme, garlic, and olive oil — the clean meal-prep version that does not taste like punishment.

The air fryer is good at these repeatable little shifts — a different seasoning, a different sauce, a different finish — because the core technique stays stable while the eating experience changes.

## Ingredients

- 4 small chicken breasts
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- Black pepper

## Method

### 01. Season.
Pat the chicken dry and toss with oil, salt, and the spice blend.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F, turning once, until browned and the thickest piece reaches 165°F.

### 03. Sauce.
Toss or brush with the finishing sauce during the last 2 minutes so it sets without burning.

### 04. Rest.
Rest 3 minutes before serving so the juices settle.

## Notes

Pull the chicken at 160°F and rest it to 165°F for juicier slices.
