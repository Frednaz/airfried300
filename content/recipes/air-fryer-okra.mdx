This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Cornmeal-crusted okra with crisp ridges and tender centers, built for hot sauce.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb okra, sliced
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp buttermilk or milk
- 3/4 cup cornmeal
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Cooking spray

## Method

### 01. Coat okra.
Dip okra in egg mixed with milk, then toss in cornmeal, flour, seasoning, and salt.

### 02. Rest.
Let coated okra sit 5 minutes so the crust hydrates.

### 03. Air fry.
Spray generously and cook at 390°F for 10–12 minutes, shaking twice.

### 04. Serve.
Salt immediately and serve with hot sauce.

## Notes

Fresh okra works best. Frozen sliced okra can work if thawed and dried thoroughly.
