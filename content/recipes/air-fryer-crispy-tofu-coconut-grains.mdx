Crispy air-fryer tofu is the dish that finally makes a case for
tofu as a default rather than a substitute. The trick is two-fold:
press out as much moisture as possible (30 minutes wrapped in
kitchen towels under a weight), then dredge in cornflour before
cooking. The cornflour creates a thin shell that goes audibly
crisp at 200°C, with the tofu staying tender inside. The contrast
is the whole point.

The marinade is doing colour and flavour duty: soy sauce for salt
and umami, sambal oelek or sriracha for heat, a touch of maple
for sweetness, a teaspoon of toasted sesame oil for depth. Toss
the cubed tofu in this for 15 minutes — that's all you need.
Cornflour goes on after the marinade has soaked in.

The bulgur (or quinoa, or rice — all work) gets cooked with
coconut milk and a pinch of salt — picks up the coconut's
sweetness without going cloying. Edamame stirred in for protein
and chew. Coriander and lime to finish.

## Ingredients

**For the tofu:**
- 1 block (400g) extra-firm tofu, drained
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
- 1 tbsp sambal oelek (or sriracha)
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 3 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

**For the coconut grains:**
- ¾ cup (140g) bulgur wheat (or quinoa, or jasmine rice)
- 1 cup (240ml) coconut milk (light or full-fat)
- 1 cup (240ml) water or vegetable stock
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 1 cup frozen edamame, defrosted
- ½ cup fresh coriander, chopped (plus more to garnish)

**To finish:**
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce (optional, for dipping)
- ½ tsp red chilli flakes

## Method

### 01. Press the tofu.
Wrap the block in a clean kitchen towel. Place on a plate, set
a heavy book or skillet on top. Press 30 minutes — moisture is
the enemy of crispy tofu.

### 02. Marinate.
Cut the pressed tofu into ¾-inch cubes. In a bowl, whisk soy
sauce, sambal, maple, sesame oil, ginger, garlic. Add the
tofu cubes, toss gently, marinate 15 minutes.

### 03. Cook the grains.
While the tofu marinates, combine bulgur, coconut milk, water,
and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low,
cover, cook 12–15 minutes until tender and liquid absorbed.
Off heat, fluff with a fork. Stir in defrosted edamame and
chopped coriander.

### 04. Dredge the tofu.
Drain the tofu from the marinade (save the marinade). Toss the
cubes with cornflour until each is fully coated. Drizzle with
the neutral oil, toss again.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the tofu cubes in
a single layer in the basket. Cook 14–16 minutes, shaking
every 5 minutes, until deeply golden and audibly crisp.

### 06. Glaze.
Bring the reserved marinade to a simmer in a small saucepan for
2 minutes — it'll thicken slightly. Drizzle over the hot
crispy tofu.

### 07. Plate.
Divide the coconut grains between two bowls. Top with crispy
tofu, scatter coriander and spring onions, sprinkle chilli
flakes. Serve lime wedges and sweet chilli on the side.

## Notes

For a heartier dinner: double the tofu, halve the grains, serve
over a bed of greens (spinach or pak choi). For a different
grain feel, swap bulgur for quinoa (same liquid ratio, 18
minutes) or jasmine rice (1:1.25 ratio, 18 minutes). The coconut
grains are excellent on their own — make extra and use as a base
for other bowls all week. Don't skip the 30-minute press — it's
the difference between crispy and damp.
