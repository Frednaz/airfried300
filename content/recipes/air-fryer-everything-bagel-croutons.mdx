## Ingredients

- 4 cups cubed bread
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp everything-bagel seasoning
- Pinch garlic powder

## Method

### 01. Prep.
Dry the main ingredients and cut everything evenly.

### 02. Season.
Toss with oil, salt, and seasonings, or bread firmly if using a crumb coating.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 375°F to 400°F, shaking or turning once, until browned and cooked through.

### 04. Finish.
Add the sauce, glaze, herbs, cheese, or citrus while hot and serve immediately.

## Notes

Everything seasoning is salty; taste before adding extra salt.
