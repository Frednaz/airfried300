This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Zucchini sticks with a gluten-free crumb crust, crisp outside and tender within, built for marinara dipping.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium zucchini, cut into sticks
- 1/3 cup gluten-free flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup gluten-free panko or crushed rice cereal
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Cooking spray
- Marinara, for serving

## Method

### 01. Dry zucchini.
Pat zucchini sticks dry, then season lightly with salt.

### 02. Bread.
Dip in flour, egg, then gluten-free crumbs mixed with seasoning and garlic.

### 03. Air fry.
Mist generously and cook at 390°F for 8–10 minutes, turning once.

### 04. Dip.
Serve hot with marinara.

## Notes

Rice cereal gives the crispiest gluten-free crust; pulse it briefly if the pieces are large.
