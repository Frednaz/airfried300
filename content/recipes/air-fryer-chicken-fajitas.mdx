This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Chicken strips, peppers, and onions cooked hot and fast for tortillas, bowls, or the clean-out-the-fridge dinner.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 8 warm tortillas
- Lime, salsa, sour cream, for serving

## Method

### 01. Season.
Toss chicken, peppers, and onion with oil, spices, and salt.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 12–15 minutes, shaking twice, until chicken reaches 165°F.

### 03. Warm tortillas.
Wrap tortillas in foil and warm separately or tuck them in the basket for the final minute.

### 04. Serve.
Pile into tortillas with lime and salsa.

## Notes

Slice the chicken thinly so it finishes at the same time as the peppers.
