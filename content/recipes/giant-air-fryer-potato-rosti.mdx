The rösti is the Swiss potato cake — grated raw or par-cooked potato
pressed flat and pan-fried in butter until golden on both sides.
It's the national breakfast dish, the side at every alpine restaurant,
and the structural cousin to the latke, the hash brown, and the
draniki. The "giant" version skips making individual rounds and goes
for one big disc — easier to make, more impressive to plate, sliced
into wedges at the table.

The technique requires the same moisture-squeeze step as latkes —
grate the potato, salt, wait, squeeze hard. The difference here is
the pressing: the grated potato gets packed firmly into a parchment-
lined basket, weighed down lightly for the first 10 minutes (a
heat-safe plate on top), then released and finished for another 8
minutes. The flatten-then-cook compresses the grated potato into a
solid disc that holds together when sliced.

The photo shows the finished rösti from above on a marble counter:
golden-brown disc with visible char marks on the surface, a slight
puff at the edges, scattered chives and lemon halves on the side.
The kind of simple dish that becomes the centrepiece because of
its size.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb (900g) Maris Piper or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and
  coarsely grated
- 1½ tsp salt (for sweating)
- 2 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp grated nutmeg (optional)

**To serve (any or all):**
- 4 oz smoked salmon
- ½ cup crème fraîche
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Cracked black pepper

## Method

### 01. Sweat the potato.
Toss grated potato with 1½ tsp salt. Let sit 10 minutes.

### 02. Squeeze.
Tip into a clean tea towel and squeeze hard. Should yield about 1
cup of liquid.

### 03. Mix.
Combine the squeezed potato with melted butter, olive oil, pepper,
and nutmeg.

### 04. Press into the basket.
Cut a parchment square to fit your air-fryer basket. Press the
potato mixture into a flat round on the parchment — about ½-inch
thick.

### 05. Air fry — first stage.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook 12 minutes — the top will
brown.

### 06. Flip.
Carefully invert the rösti onto another parchment square (place a
plate on top, flip, slide back into basket). Cook 8 more minutes
until the other side is also deeply golden.

### 07. Serve.
Slide onto a board, cut into 4 wedges. Top with smoked salmon and
crème fraîche, scatter chives, serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes

For a herbed rösti: fold in 2 tbsp chopped chives + 1 tbsp dill
before pressing. For breakfast: top with a poached egg and bacon
instead of salmon. For a cheese version: scatter ½ cup grated Gruyère
across the surface after the flip. The rösti is best within 30
minutes of cooking; the surface softens as it cools. Don't try to
make this thicker than ½-inch — the inside won't cook through.
