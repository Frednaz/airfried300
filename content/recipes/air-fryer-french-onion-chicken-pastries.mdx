The chicken-and-French-onion bakery pastry is one of those Australian
takeaway items — alongside meat pies and sausage rolls — that
appears in every petrol-station bakery. Filo or puff pastry wrapped
around a thick creamy chicken filling, sometimes with bacon, always
with onion.

This homemade version uses pre-cooked chicken (rotisserie is fastest)
mixed with a thick French-onion-soup-mix-based cream sauce, wrapped
in store-bought puff pastry. The trick is making the filling thick
enough that it doesn't leak — flour-roux base with cream and stock,
cooked until coating-the-back-of-a-spoon thickness.

The photo shows three pastries: golden-brown plaited tops, one cut
open showing the creamy chicken filling spilling out, visible bacon
flecks. The kind of weeknight dinner that looks like effort.

## Ingredients

**For the filling:**
- 1 lb (450g) cooked chicken, finely shredded
- 4 oz (110g) bacon, finely diced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 packet (35g) French onion soup mix
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- ¾ cup (180ml) chicken stock
- ½ cup (120ml) double cream
- ½ cup (55g) grated cheddar
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

**For wrapping:**
- 2 sheets ready-rolled puff pastry, thawed
- 1 large egg, beaten (for egg wash)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)

## Method

### 01. Brown the bacon.
Cook diced bacon in a skillet over medium until crisp (5 minutes).
Set aside, leaving the fat in the pan.

### 02. Sauce.
Add butter to the bacon fat. Cook chopped onion 4 minutes until
softened. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds. Stir in flour and soup mix,
cook 1 minute. Pour in stock and cream, whisking. Simmer 4 minutes
until very thick.

### 03. Combine.
Off heat, fold in shredded chicken, crisped bacon, cheddar, Dijon,
and parsley. Cool 10 minutes.

### 04. Wrap.
Cut each pastry sheet into 3 rectangles (6 total). Spoon ⅓ cup of
filling onto the centre of each. Fold pastry over the filling and
seal the edges by pressing with a fork. Use kitchen scissors to
snip decorative slashes on top, or score with a knife.

### 05. Egg wash.
Brush each pastry with beaten egg. Scatter sesame seeds if using.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Place pastries on parchment
in a single layer (work in batches). Cook 15–17 minutes, until
deeply golden and puffed.

### 07. Rest briefly.
Let cool 5 minutes before serving — the filling is molten.

## Notes

**Freezer-friendly**: assemble through step 5, freeze on a tray, then
bag. Cook from frozen at 380°F for 22 minutes. Without French onion
soup mix: use 2 tsp onion powder + 1 tsp dried thyme + 1 tsp beef
stock powder + ½ tsp salt. Filling can be made 2 days ahead.
