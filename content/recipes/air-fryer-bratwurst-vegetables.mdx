Brats are already built for the air fryer: fatty enough to brown, sturdy enough not to dry out, and happy beside vegetables. This version cooks the vegetables first so they get a head start, then the sausages go on top and baste everything below.

It is a sheet-pan dinner in basket form, just faster and with better edges. Mustard belongs on the table; sauerkraut would not be unwelcome.

## Ingredients

- 4 bratwurst links
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 12 oz baby potatoes, halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp caraway or fennel seeds, optional
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Mustard, buns, or sauerkraut, for serving

## Method

### 01. Start the vegetables.
Toss potatoes, peppers, and onion with oil, salt, and caraway. Cook at 380°F for 10 minutes.

### 02. Add brats.
Nestle bratwurst on top of the vegetables. Cook 10–12 minutes more, turning the sausages once.

### 03. Check doneness.
Brats should be browned and read 160°F inside. Give the vegetables another shake if needed.

### 04. Serve.
Pile into bowls or buns with mustard and any pan juices from the basket drawer.

## Notes

Raw bratwurst works here. If using fully cooked brats, add them after the vegetables have cooked 14 minutes and warm just until browned.
