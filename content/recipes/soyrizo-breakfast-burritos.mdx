Soyrizo is one of the rare vegetarian meat-imitators that works on its
own merits. Crumbled into a hot pan with the same fat content as
chorizo, it browns at the edges and adds the same paprika-and-vinegar
funk to whatever it touches. In a breakfast burrito it's a perfect
swap — you don't miss the meat because there's no gap to miss it from,
just the warm spice and the orange-red colour bleeding into the
scrambled eggs.

The structure is the standard breakfast burrito: a base of crispy
roasted potatoes (do these in the air fryer for 12 minutes if you
have one going, or the skillet if not), the soyrizo crumbled and
browned, soft scrambled eggs, melted cheese, all wrapped in a warm
flour tortilla. Then — and this is the part that elevates it — pan-
toast the rolled burrito briefly to crisp the outside. The photo shows
exactly what that gets you: golden-brown wrap with grill lines, the
inside cross-section showing the layered filling.

For meal-prep: make six on Sunday, eat one, freeze five. Each frozen
burrito reheats in about 5 minutes in the air fryer (or 90 seconds
microwave + 3 minutes pan), and they're indistinguishable from fresh.

## Ingredients

**For the filling:**
- 12 oz soyrizo (1 package)
- 6 large eggs
- 2 tbsp whole milk
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 2 medium russet potatoes, diced into ½-inch pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- 1½ cups shredded Mexican blend cheese

**For assembling:**
- 6 large flour tortillas (10-inch)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for crisping)
- Salsa verde, to serve
- Hot sauce, to serve

## Method

### 01. Crisp the potatoes.
Toss the diced potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, and ¾
tsp salt. Either: air fry at 400°F for 14–16 minutes, shaking every 5
minutes, OR cook in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high for 18 minutes,
turning every 5 minutes. Set aside.

### 02. Brown the soyrizo.
In the same skillet over medium heat, crumble the soyrizo and cook,
breaking it up with a spoon, until it's deeply browned and slightly
crisp at the edges, about 8 minutes. Move to a bowl.

### 03. Scramble the eggs.
Whisk the eggs with the milk, ½ tsp salt, and pepper. Melt the butter
in the same skillet over medium-low. Add the eggs and cook slowly,
stirring often with a rubber spatula, until just set and still glossy
— 3 to 4 minutes. Pull off the heat slightly underdone.

### 04. Warm the tortillas.
Stack the tortillas on a plate, cover with a damp paper towel, and
microwave for 30 seconds. (Or warm each briefly over a gas burner.)

### 05. Assemble.
Lay a tortilla flat. Down the middle, layer: ⅓ cup eggs, ⅓ cup soyrizo,
½ cup crispy potatoes, ¼ cup cheese. Fold in both sides, then roll
tightly from the bottom up. Repeat with all 6.

### 06. Pan-toast.
Wipe the skillet, return to medium heat with a little olive oil. Place
each burrito seam-side-down and cook 90 seconds per side, until the
outside is golden-brown with toast marks.

### 07. Serve or freeze.
Cut in half on the bias. Serve with salsa verde and hot sauce. For
freezing, skip the pan-toast on the ones you're freezing; do it when
reheating instead.

## Notes

**To freeze**: cool the rolled burritos to room temperature, wrap each
individually in foil, freeze in a freezer bag up to 1 month. To
reheat from frozen: unwrap, microwave 60 seconds, then pan-toast or
air-fry at 380°F for 5 minutes. For meat-eaters, swap soyrizo for the
same amount of fresh Mexican chorizo (cooks the same way). For a
heartier version, add black beans (½ cup, drained) to the filling.
