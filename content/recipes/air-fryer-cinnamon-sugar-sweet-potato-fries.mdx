## Ingredients

- 2 sweet potatoes, cut into fries
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Method

### 01. Prep.
Pat ingredients dry, cut everything to an even size, and heat the air fryer.

### 02. Season.
Toss with oil, salt, and the seasoning blend until lightly coated, not wet.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook in a single layer, shaking or turning once, until browned and cooked through.

### 04. Finish.
Add sauce, herbs, cheese, citrus, or glaze after cooking so the crisp edges stay crisp.

## Notes

Sweet potato fries stay softer than russet fries; browned edges are the win.
