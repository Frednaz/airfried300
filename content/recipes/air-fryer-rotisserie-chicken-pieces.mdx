The supermarket rotisserie chicken is one of the great convenience
foods of the late twentieth century — fully cooked, properly seasoned,
under £8, sitting in a warming case ready to go. It's also, almost
always, slightly dry, slightly bland-smelling, and from a chicken
that's been spinning under a heat lamp for who knows how long. The
home rotisserie alternative used to require a counter-eating
appliance with a spit. The air fryer makes that obsolete.

This recipe approximates the rotisserie flavour through a spice rub
— the standard rotisserie blend is smoked paprika, garlic powder,
onion powder, salt, dried herbs (thyme, oregano), and a touch of
sugar to encourage browning. The chicken pieces (a mix of bone-in
thighs and drumsticks, which is what most rotisserie chickens give
you) get rubbed all over and air-fried at moderate heat — 380°F
rather than 400°F — so the meat cooks through evenly without
scorching the spice rub.

The photo shows the result: a glass casserole dish with four pieces
of golden-brown bone-in chicken (drumsticks and thighs), the skin
deeply burnished and crackling, the spice rub visible as a dark
pebbled crust. The slightly oily sheen is the rendered fat — that's
the dripping you want to spoon over rice or potatoes later.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken:**
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken drumsticks (or 2 more thighs if you
  prefer)
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**For the rotisserie rub:**
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1½ tsp garlic powder
- 1½ tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp light brown sugar
- ¼ tsp ground white pepper (optional)
- ¼ tsp cayenne (optional, for a little heat)

## Method

### 01. Dry the chicken.
Pat all the chicken pieces very dry with paper towels. Surface
moisture prevents the skin from crisping properly.

### 02. Mix the rub.
In a small bowl, combine the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder,
thyme, oregano, salt, pepper, brown sugar, white pepper (if using),
and cayenne (if using).

### 03. Oil and season.
Rub each piece of chicken with a thin coat of olive oil, then
sprinkle the rotisserie rub all over, pressing it into the skin.
Don't be shy — there should be visible coverage everywhere.

### 04. Air fry skin-side-down (to start).
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Place the chicken
skin-side-DOWN in the basket (counterintuitively — this helps render
the fat and crisp the skin in the second half). Cook 12 minutes.

### 05. Flip and finish.
Flip each piece skin-side-up. Cook 16–18 minutes more, until the
skin is deeply burnished and the internal temperature reads 175°F
(80°C) at the thickest part of the thigh.

### 06. Rest.
Move to a plate and rest 5 minutes — the juices redistribute.
Serve whole or shred for sandwiches, bowls, or tacos.

## Notes

For pulled rotisserie-style chicken: rest the cooked chicken 10
minutes, then shred with two forks. Reserve the rendered fat from
the basket to drizzle over the shredded meat — keeps it moist and
flavourful. The rub keeps in a sealed jar for 2 months; make a
triple batch. For a whole chicken version, see the air-fryer whole
roast chicken recipe — same rub works. To meal-prep: shred the
cooked chicken, divide into 4 containers, use across the week in
chicken Caesar wraps, tacos, soups, and salads.
