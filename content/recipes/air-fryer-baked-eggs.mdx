Baked eggs in tomato sauce is the dish that goes by many names —
shakshuka in Israel, huevos rancheros in Mexico, uova in
purgatorio in Italy. They all share the structural idea: a
flavourful tomato base, eggs cracked in and gently cooked until
the whites set and the yolks stay runny. Served with bread for
mopping. The differences are in the spice profile.

This version leans Mediterranean: paprika, cumin, garlic, red
pepper, fresh parsley. The air fryer does the egg-cooking step
perfectly — direct heat from above sets the whites quickly while
the yolks stay liquid (about 6 minutes at 350°F for an egg in
hot sauce).

The technique is in stages. First, make the sauce on the stovetop
— soften onion and pepper, bloom paprika and cumin, add tomatoes,
simmer 8 minutes until thickened. Then divide between two small
oven-safe dishes (or one larger). Make wells in the sauce, crack
in the eggs. Air-fry until the whites are just set.

## Ingredients

**For the sauce:**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 (14 oz / 400g) can chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar

**For the eggs:**
- 4 large eggs
- 2 oz (60g) feta, crumbled (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped (optional)

**To serve:**
- 4 slices crusty sourdough or country bread
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, peeled

## Method

### 01. Make the sauce.
Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add onion and red
pepper, cook 6 minutes until softened. Add garlic, paprika,
cumin, and red pepper flakes; cook 1 minute. Stir in chopped
tomatoes, tomato paste, salt, pepper, sugar, and vinegar.
Simmer 8 minutes until thickened.

### 02. Divide.
Spoon the sauce evenly into two oven-safe dishes (8-oz ramekins
or a 6-inch round baking dish each).

### 03. Add the eggs.
With the back of a spoon, make 2 wells in the sauce of each dish.
Crack an egg into each well. If using feta, scatter crumbles
around the eggs.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Cook the dishes for 6–8
minutes — whites should be just set, yolks still runny. Cook
2–3 minutes more if you want firmer yolks.

### 05. Toast the bread.
While the eggs finish, toast the sourdough until deeply golden
(toaster, grill, or back in the air fryer at 380°F for 2 minutes
per side). Rub one side with the cut garlic clove. Drizzle with
olive oil.

### 06. Serve.
Scatter parsley and coriander over the eggs. Serve with the
garlicky toast for dipping. Eat directly from the dish, with
the bread.

## Notes

For a North African shakshuka, swap the paprika and cumin for
2 tsp each of cumin and coriander + ½ tsp turmeric. Add a
pinch of harissa to the sauce. For a hangover-friendly version,
double the chilli flakes. For a meat version, brown 4 oz of
chorizo with the onions. The sauce can be made ahead and
refrigerated 3 days; just reheat in a small saucepan, transfer
to ramekins, and proceed from step 3.
