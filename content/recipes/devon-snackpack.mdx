The snackpack — also called HSP (halal snackpack) in some Sydney
contexts, but the proper devon version is a different beast entirely
— is one of the great Australian late-night convenience-store
inventions. Chips on the bottom, processed meat (devon, fritz, or
luncheon — the pink rectangle of cured pork loaf that Australians
either love or pretend they don't) cubed and warmed on top, melted
cheese covering it all, then a chaotic drizzle of BBQ sauce and
garlic mayo in zigzag patterns.

This is not refined food. It's the dish you eat at 1am after a night
out, served in a foil container, eaten with a fork. Making it at
home in the air fryer is a small act of nostalgia — and it's
surprisingly good given the inputs.

The technique: cook chips first (any frozen chip works — 18 minutes
at 200°C), then top with diced devon and grated cheese, return to
the air fryer for 4 more minutes to melt the cheese. Drizzle sauces
right before serving.

The photo shows the result in a foil container: golden chips visible
underneath, melted cheese pooled with pink devon cubes showing
through, BBQ sauce in dark stripes, garlic mayo in white zigzag.
Looks like a late-night order. Tastes the same.

## Ingredients

**For the snackpack:**
- 1 lb (450g) frozen chips (American-style fries or chunky chips —
  either works)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp salt
- 6 slices devon / fritz / luncheon meat, diced into ½-inch cubes
- 1½ cups (170g) grated cheddar (or mozzarella for stretch)

**For the sauces:**
- ¼ cup BBQ sauce (your favourite brand)
- ¼ cup garlic aioli (or mayo mixed with 2 cloves grated garlic)

**To serve:**
- 2 tbsp finely sliced spring onions
- Pickled jalapeños (optional)
- Sriracha (optional, for an extra hot drizzle)

## Method

### 01. Cook the chips.
Toss frozen chips with olive oil and salt. Heat the air fryer to
400°F (200°C). Cook 16–18 minutes, shaking once, until deep golden.

### 02. Add devon and cheese.
Move chips to a foil container or oven-safe dish that fits the air
fryer. Scatter devon cubes evenly across the top. Pile grated cheese
over everything.

### 03. Melt.
Return to the air fryer at 380°F (193°C) for 4–5 minutes, until the
cheese is fully melted and bubbling.

### 04. Sauce.
Pull out. Drizzle BBQ sauce in zigzag stripes. Then drizzle garlic
aioli in a contrasting pattern.

### 05. Finish.
Scatter spring onions, jalapeños if using, and a final hit of
sriracha if you want it hotter.

### 06. Eat.
Eat directly from the container with a fork.

## Notes

For an HSP version: use shredded kebab/lamb meat instead of devon
+ chilli sauce + garlic sauce. For a less-processed version: replace
devon with chopped leftover ham or cooked chorizo. The sauces are
non-negotiable — without both, it's just chips and cheese. For
vegetarians: skip the devon, add halloumi cubes instead. Best within
five minutes of cooking — the chips lose their crispness fast.
