The pan-fried meatball is great but it's a project: oil splatters, you
need to turn each one three or four times, the kitchen smells like
beef for the next day. The oven meatball is fine but they don't brown
as well — they bake into pale spheres unless you crank the heat to
broiler-only-level. The air fryer is the in-between that gets it
exactly right: high heat from above and below, no oil management, deep
browning on every surface, twelve minutes start to finish.

The mix here is the classic 50/50 beef-and-pork — beef for the flavour,
pork for the fat (without which meatballs go dry). Parmesan in the
mix, breadcrumbs to lighten, an egg to bind, garlic and herbs to
season. Roll them small (golf-ball or smaller), give them space in the
basket, and they come out browned on every face with that perfect
crusty-outside, tender-inside duality.

The photo is the natural habitat: spaghetti with tomato sauce, three or
four meatballs per bowl, parmesan grated over the top, a scatter of
parsley. Two bowls because this is a dinner-for-two photograph; the
recipe makes enough for four (or a meal-prep batch of three lunches
and a dinner).

## Ingredients

**For the meatballs:**
- 1 lb (450g) ground beef (80/20)
- ½ lb (225g) ground pork
- ½ cup (50g) panko breadcrumbs
- ⅓ cup (30g) grated parmesan
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp whole milk

**To serve:**
- 12 oz dry spaghetti
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce (or homemade)
- ¼ cup grated parmesan, to finish
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped, to finish

## Method

### 01. Mix the meatball base.
In a large bowl, combine the beef, pork, breadcrumbs, parmesan, egg,
garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and milk. Mix with
your hands until just combined — don't overwork it, or the meatballs
go tough.

### 02. Roll.
Scoop the mixture by the heaping tablespoon and roll between your
palms into balls about 1¼ inches across. You should get 24 meatballs.
Arrange on a tray as you go.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the meatballs in a single
layer in the basket, leaving space between them — work in two batches
if your basket is small. Cook 10–12 minutes, shaking the basket at the
6-minute mark, until deeply browned and the internal temperature
reaches 160°F (71°C).

### 04. Cook the pasta and warm the sauce.
While the meatballs cook, boil the spaghetti in salted water according
to the package. Warm the marinara sauce in a saucepan over medium-low.

### 05. Combine and serve.
Drain the pasta. Toss the cooked meatballs gently into the warm sauce.
Plate the spaghetti, spoon meatballs and sauce on top, finish with
parmesan and parsley.

## Notes

**To freeze**: arrange cooked, cooled meatballs on a sheet pan, freeze
solid, then bag. Reheat from frozen at 380°F for 8 minutes. The
uncooked meatballs also freeze well — same arrangement, freeze, bag.
Cook from frozen at 400°F for 14 minutes. For an all-beef version,
use 1½ lb beef and increase the breadcrumbs to ⅔ cup. For a sub
sandwich, stuff 4 meatballs and sauce into a sub roll with provolone
and broil 90 seconds.
