The vegetable taco is a category that, when done badly, feels like
something you're settling for. Done well, it's not a compromise at
all — it's a different dish from the meat-taco, with its own logic
and pleasures. Butternut squash makes this case better than most
vegetables. Its natural sweetness caramelises hard in the air
fryer; tossed with smoky chipotle and cumin, it picks up enough
savoury depth to feel like dinner rather than a side dish; and the
soft-but-structural texture works well wrapped in a tortilla.

The technique: dice the squash small (½-inch cubes), toss with
oil and a chipotle-cumin-paprika-brown-sugar rub, air-fry at
400°F until the edges go dark amber. The brown sugar isn't there
to make the squash sweeter — there's plenty of natural sugar
already — it's there to help the surface caramelise. Without it,
the squash browns; with it, the squash glazes.

The taco assembly is unfussy: warm corn tortillas (the right
choice for this dish — flour tortillas are too soft for the
squash), the squash filling, sliced avocado, finely diced red
onion, crumbled queso fresco, fresh cilantro, lime juice. That's
it. No salsa needed (the squash is the flavour); no sour cream
(the avocado does that job).

## Ingredients

**For the squash:**
- 1 small butternut squash (about 2 lb / 900g), peeled, seeded,
  cut into ½-inch cubes (about 4 cups)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground chipotle powder (or 2 tsp chipotle in adobo,
  finely minced)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp lime juice (to finish)

**For assembling:**
- 8 small corn tortillas (5-inch)
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- ¼ red onion, finely diced
- ⅓ cup queso fresco, crumbled (or feta as a sub)
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes, cut into wedges
- Hot sauce or salsa, to serve

## Method

### 01. Prep the squash.
Peel the butternut (a sharp Y-peeler is the right tool). Halve
lengthwise, scoop out the seeds. Cut the flesh into ½-inch cubes.

### 02. Season.
In a bowl, toss the squash cubes with olive oil, chipotle,
smoked paprika, cumin, brown sugar, salt, garlic powder, and
cinnamon.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the squash in a single
layer in the basket. Cook 18–22 minutes, shaking every 6 minutes,
until the cubes are tender inside (fork-pierceable) and deeply
caramelised at the edges.

### 04. Finish with lime.
Tip the cooked squash into a bowl and squeeze 1 tablespoon of
lime juice over it. Toss gently.

### 05. Warm the tortillas.
Stack the tortillas, wrap in foil or a damp paper towel,
microwave 30 seconds. Or warm individually over a gas burner for
char marks.

### 06. Assemble.
Lay a warm tortilla flat. Spoon about ⅓ cup of squash into the
centre. Top with 2 avocado slices, a sprinkle of red onion,
queso fresco, and cilantro. Squeeze a lime wedge over the top.

### 07. Serve.
Serve immediately. 2 tacos per person is the right amount; 3 is
a hungry person's amount.

## Notes

For a heartier filling, add a can of black beans (drained, rinsed)
mixed into the squash for the last 5 minutes of cooking. For a
vegan version, swap queso fresco for a sprinkle of toasted
pepitas (pumpkin seeds — gives crunch). The squash filling
keeps 3 days refrigerated and reheats well in the air fryer.
Don't substitute pre-cubed butternut from the grocery store —
their cubes are inconsistent in size and they're often slightly
dried out. Cube your own.
