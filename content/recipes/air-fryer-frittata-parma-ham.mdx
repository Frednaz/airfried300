The frittata is the Italian relative of the Spanish tortilla and
the French omelette — eggs cooked with vegetables and cheese,
substantial enough to be lunch but elegant enough for brunch.
Traditionally finished under a grill; the air fryer does the same
job better, because the heat circulates and you get even
browning on the surface without the risk of one side burning
while the centre stays raw.

The eight-egg ratio gives you a frittata that serves four
comfortably — generous wedges, with leftovers for one person's
lunch. The technique is essentially "make a custard with
vegetables in it." Whisk eggs with cream and grated parmesan,
add seasonings, fold in halved cherry tomatoes and sliced spring
onions, pour into an oiled tin. Air-fry at 350°F for 18 minutes.

The Parma ham gets its own basket trip — separate sheets of ham
laid flat, air-fried at 380°F for 4 minutes until crisp and
brittle. They break like glass when you bite them. Lay them on
the side of the plate as you'd lay bacon, or crumble over the
frittata wedges.

## Ingredients

**For the frittata:**
- 8 large eggs
- ⅓ cup (80ml) double cream (heavy cream)
- ⅓ cup (30g) grated parmesan
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1½ cups (220g) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 4 spring onions, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for greasing the tin)

**For the Parma ham crisps:**
- 8 slices Parma ham (or prosciutto)

**To serve:**
- 2 cups baby spinach or rocket
- Squeeze of lemon
- Extra olive oil
- Cracked black pepper

## Method

### 01. Crisp the Parma ham first.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Lay the Parma ham slices
in a single layer on a piece of parchment in the basket. Cook
4–5 minutes until shatter-crisp and deeply burnished. Move
carefully to a plate (they break easily). Set aside.

### 02. Mix the custard.
In a bowl, whisk eggs with cream, parmesan, Dijon, salt, and
pepper until smooth.

### 03. Prepare the tin.
Brush a 7-inch (or smaller) round cake tin (or a small loaf
tin) generously with olive oil. Scatter the cherry tomato
halves, spring onions, and most of the chives across the
bottom.

### 04. Pour and air fry.
Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables. The cherry tomatoes
will float to the top. Heat the air fryer to 350°F (175°C).
Cook 16–20 minutes, until the top is deeply golden and the
centre is just set with no liquid wobble.

### 05. Rest.
Let the frittata rest 5 minutes in the tin — it firms up as it
cools and slices more cleanly.

### 06. Plate.
Run a knife around the edge, invert onto a plate, then flip
back so the browned side faces up. Cut into 4 wedges.

### 07. Serve.
Plate each wedge with 2 Parma ham crisps on the side and a
small handful of rocket dressed with lemon and olive oil.
Scatter the remaining chives.

## Notes

For a vegetarian version, skip the ham (the frittata stands on
its own) or replace with crisp-fried sage leaves or sun-dried
tomato strips. For more substance, fold in 1 cup of cooked
diced potatoes or leftover roasted vegetables before pouring.
Goat's cheese crumbles excellent in place of parmesan. The
frittata is excellent cold the next day — perfect lunchbox
material. The Parma ham crisps don't keep well — eat the day
they're made.
