This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Chicken pieces glazed with pineapple, soy, brown sugar, and ginger until sticky at the edges.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 cup pineapple chunks
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- Green onions, for serving

## Method

### 01. Marinate.
Toss chicken with soy sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, ginger, garlic, and cornstarch.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 12 minutes, shaking once.

### 03. Add pineapple.
Add pineapple and cook 4–6 minutes more, until chicken is 165°F and glossy.

### 04. Serve.
Scatter green onions and serve over rice.

## Notes

Canned pineapple works; drain it well so the glaze does not turn soupy.
