The meal-prep plate is the lunch container that has solved the problem
of "what's for lunch" for an entire generation of office workers. Two
or three compartments, a protein on one side, a fruit and a snack on
the other, and you don't have to think before you eat. This is that,
buffalo-chicken-flavoured, which is the most universally inoffensive
flavour profile that still has personality.

Buffalo chicken salad is the trick. Shredded chicken plus buffalo
sauce plus a cooling element (ranch, blue cheese, or in the photo's
case, a creamy dressing with pickle relish) plus crunch (celery,
finely diced). The chicken absorbs the flavour overnight and gets
BETTER on day two — this is one of the rare meal-prep dishes that
doesn't degrade as the week goes on.

The photo shows the four containers: chicken salad in the largest
compartment with green parsley flecks, blueberries in the small
compartment for sweet-and-fresh, in-shell pistachios for the salt and
texture and the satisfaction of having something to crack open. Each
container is one complete lunch, eaten cold, no reheating, no fuss.

## Ingredients

**For the buffalo chicken salad:**
- 3 cups cooked, shredded chicken (rotisserie or boiled)
- ⅓ cup mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt for lighter)
- 3 tbsp buffalo sauce (Frank's RedHot or similar)
- 2 tbsp ranch dressing (or blue cheese crumbles)
- 2 stalks celery, finely diced
- 2 tbsp red onion, finely minced
- 2 tbsp dill pickle, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For each meal-prep box (4 boxes):**
- ½ cup blueberries (or strawberries, sliced)
- ¼ cup pistachios, in the shell

## Method

### 01. Mix the salad.
In a bowl, combine the shredded chicken, mayonnaise, buffalo sauce,
ranch, celery, red onion, pickle, parsley, salt, and pepper. Stir
until the chicken is evenly coated and the colour is uniformly
buffalo-orange.

### 02. Taste and adjust.
Buffalo sauces vary in heat — taste, add another tablespoon if you
want it spicier, or more ranch to mellow it. The salad should sting
slightly at the tongue, then mellow on the swallow.

### 03. Portion.
Divide the chicken salad evenly into the four meal-prep containers.
Fill the second compartment with blueberries (or strawberries). Fill
the third with pistachios.

### 04. Cover and refrigerate.
Seal the containers and refrigerate. They keep up to 5 days.

### 05. Eat cold.
No reheating required. Add a fork.

## Notes

For a heartier version, line the chicken-salad compartment with crisp
romaine leaves — eat the salad scooped onto the lettuce as a wrap.
The blueberries can be swapped for any fruit that holds up cold:
strawberries, grapes, mandarin segments, apple slices (tossed in
lemon to prevent browning). The pistachios can be swapped for any
snack that has structural integrity: almonds, cashews, pretzels,
mini cheese rounds. Goal is variety in texture, not a specific
food group.
