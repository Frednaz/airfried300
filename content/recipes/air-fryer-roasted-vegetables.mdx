The roasted-vegetable platter is the side dish that quietly takes
over the meal. You start with the idea of "we'll have some vegetables
alongside the chicken." You end with everyone going back for seconds
of the vegetables and ignoring the chicken. The trick is the
caramelisation: vegetables roasted at high heat develop nutty,
slightly burnt edges that taste fundamentally different from boiled
or steamed ones.

The air fryer beats the oven for this specific job. Same heat (400°F),
half the time (22 minutes vs. 40), and the circulating air browns
every face of every cube — including the surfaces touching the
basket, which on a sheet pan would be the bottoms that don't crisp.
The downside is volume: you can do 1–1.5 lb of vegetables at a time;
much more and you have to do two batches.

The photo shows the platter: chunks of new potato (gold-skinned),
butternut squash (deep orange with charred tips), red bell pepper
(charred to brown at the edges), red onion wedges (the layers
separated and crisped at the points), fresh thyme sprigs tucked in
for the green flecks and the smell. Plated on a long oval white
dish — the kind of platter you take to the table and let everyone
serve themselves.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz (340g) new potatoes (or fingerlings), halved
- 1 small butternut squash (about 12 oz / 340g), peeled and cubed
  (¾-inch / 2cm)
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 4 cloves garlic, peeled but whole
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Fresh thyme sprigs (to garnish)
- Flaky sea salt (to finish)

## Method

### 01. Cut to similar sizes.
Cut the vegetables roughly the same size — about ¾-inch — so they
cook at the same rate. Small potatoes can be halved, larger ones
quartered. Squash cubes should be uniform.

### 02. Toss.
In a large bowl, combine all the vegetables and garlic cloves with
the olive oil, thyme, salt, paprika, and pepper. Toss until every
piece has a sheen of oil.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spread the vegetables in the
basket — slightly mounded is fine, they'll redistribute as they
cook. Cook 22–25 minutes, shaking the basket at the 10-minute mark
and again at 18, until the potatoes are tender when pierced and
the squash is caramelised at the edges.

### 04. Plate.
Tip onto a serving platter. Scatter fresh thyme sprigs and a pinch
of flaky salt over the top. Serve hot.

## Notes

**Vegetable swaps**: any combination that cooks in roughly the same
time works — root vegetables (carrots, parsnips), brassicas (broccoli,
cauliflower — add later, they only need 12 minutes), zucchini (add
later, 10 minutes). Avoid mixing very-fast-cooking ones (zucchini)
with very-slow ones (whole carrots) unless you stagger them. For a
sweeter version, add 1 tbsp maple syrup in the last 5 minutes — it
caramelises into a glaze. Leftovers are excellent cold over greens
or stirred into pasta with feta.
