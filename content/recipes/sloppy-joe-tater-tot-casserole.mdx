This is the casserole that says "I have other things going on tonight."
You brown a pound of ground meat, add tomato sauce and a few spices to
fake a from-scratch sloppy joe filling, top the whole thing with frozen
tater tots, slide the skillet into the oven for 25 minutes, and walk
away. It comes out in one pan, golden on top, with the tots arranged
like little crisp pebbles over a stew of sloppy joe filling that's
been thickening underneath.

It's not air-fried (the only one in this set that isn't) — the tots
brown best in the oven where the heat is more even and the filling
underneath doesn't dry out. But it's the spiritual cousin of the air-
fried potato recipes, in that the tots come out shatteringly crisp from
having sat over the bubbling-meat moisture for the whole bake.

The photo shows the finished skillet: tater tots arranged in a tight
circle, deeply browned at the tips, scattered with sliced scallions for
green flecks. A bottle of hot sauce (Cholula, in this case) sits next
to the pan because that's how this gets eaten — you spoon out a portion
into a bowl, hit it with hot sauce, eat it standing up. It's not
refined. That's not what this is for.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (or beef)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 lb frozen tater tots
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar (optional, but recommended)
- 2 scallions, finely sliced (to garnish)
- Hot sauce, to serve

## Method

### 01. Heat the oven.
Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Place a 10-inch cast-iron skillet
on the stove over medium-high heat.

### 02. Brown the meat.
Add the olive oil to the skillet, then the ground turkey. Cook,
breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until the meat is browned, about
6 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.

### 03. Aromatics.
Add the chopped onion and cook 4 minutes, until softened. Add the
garlic and cook 30 seconds more.

### 04. Build the sauce.
Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, Worcestershire, brown sugar,
mustard, paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Simmer 5 minutes,
until thickened to a stew-like consistency.

### 05. Top with tots.
Pull the skillet off the heat. Arrange the frozen tater tots over the
top in a single layer, packed tightly together so they form a complete
crust over the filling.

### 06. Bake.
Slide the skillet into the oven and bake 22–25 minutes, until the tots
are deeply golden and crisp. If using cheese, sprinkle it on in the
last 3 minutes and return to the oven to melt.

### 07. Serve.
Scatter the scallions over the top. Spoon into bowls, hit with hot
sauce, serve.

## Notes

For a vegetarian version, swap the meat for 2 cups of cooked lentils
(plus an extra splash of soy sauce for umami). Casserole keeps 4 days
in the fridge and reheats well — the tots stay surprisingly crisp.
Cornbread on the side is the unofficial pairing. For a less-sweet
sauce, halve the brown sugar; for a smokier version, add a teaspoon of
chipotle in adobo.
