The Caesar chicken wrap is the lunch you order at the airport and then
spend the next two months wondering why you can't recreate it. The
answer is anchovies — not in the wrap itself, but mashed into the
dressing, where they dissolve into salty depth that mayonnaise alone
can't reach. Two tiny fillets per cup of dressing; nobody can tell
they're there, but you'd notice if they weren't.

The chicken is whatever you've got: rotisserie chicken shredded with
two forks, leftover roast chicken, even canned chicken in a pinch. The
romaine is the structural element — you want enough that it crunches
audibly when you bite the wrap. Parmesan finely shredded so it
distributes; not the powdery kind from the green can, but actual
shredded parmesan (or, even better, microplaned wedges).

The photo shows the cross-section — the wrap split in half, you can see
the layers: creamy chicken salad with green flecks of romaine, white
parmesan rubble at the surface, the soft flour tortilla holding it all
together. A sprinkle of parmesan on the cutting board to suggest "more
where that came from."

## Ingredients

**For the Caesar dressing:**
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp lemon juice (about ½ a lemon)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 anchovy fillets, mashed to a paste (or 1 tsp anchovy paste)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or pressed
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ¼ tsp black pepper, freshly cracked

**For the wraps:**
- 3 cups cooked, shredded chicken (rotisserie)
- 4 cups romaine lettuce, finely shredded
- ½ cup grated parmesan (plus more to garnish)
- 4 large flour tortillas (10-inch)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (optional, for crisping)

## Method

### 01. Make the dressing.
In a bowl, whisk together the mayo, lemon juice, Dijon, mashed
anchovies, garlic, parmesan, Worcestershire, and pepper until smooth.
Taste — it should be punchy and just-savoury. Add more lemon if it
feels heavy.

### 02. Combine.
Add the shredded chicken and toss until every shred is coated. Add the
romaine and ½ cup of parmesan, and toss gently — just enough to mix.

### 03. Warm the tortillas.
Stack the tortillas on a plate, cover with a damp paper towel, and
microwave for 20 seconds. This makes them pliable for wrapping
without cracking.

### 04. Assemble.
Lay a tortilla flat. Spoon ¼ of the chicken salad in a line down the
middle (leave 2 inches at the sides). Fold the sides in, then roll
tightly from the bottom up. Repeat with all four.

### 05. Optional: crisp them.
For a slightly toasted exterior, place the wraps seam-side-down in a
hot dry skillet for 90 seconds per side, or in the air fryer at 380°F
for 4 minutes total. They'll have those golden grill-marked stripes.

### 06. Slice and serve.
Cut each wrap on the diagonal. Garnish with extra parmesan on the
cutting board for the photo, eat for lunch.

## Notes

The dressed chicken-romaine mix can sit in the fridge for 24 hours
without going limp — the dressing protects the lettuce. For meal-prep,
keep the filling and tortillas separate, wrap each morning. Add
chopped pickled banana peppers for heat. Swap anchovies for ½ tsp soy
sauce + 1 tsp miso paste if you don't have them (closer-than-you'd-
think result).
