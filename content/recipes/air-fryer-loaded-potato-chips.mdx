This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Homemade potato chips finished with cheddar, bacon, scallions, and sour cream — nachos, but potato.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 2 russet potatoes, sliced thin
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar
- 3 slices cooked bacon, crumbled
- 2 scallions, sliced
- Sour cream, for serving

## Method

### 01. Soak slices.
Soak potato slices in cold water 20 minutes, then dry very well.

### 02. Air fry chips.
Toss with oil and salt. Cook at 360°F for 14–18 minutes in batches, shaking often.

### 03. Load.
Pile chips in an oven-safe dish, top with cheese and bacon, and air fry 2 minutes to melt.

### 04. Finish.
Add scallions and sour cream.

## Notes

A mandoline makes even chips; uneven slices cook unevenly. Pull browned chips early as needed.
