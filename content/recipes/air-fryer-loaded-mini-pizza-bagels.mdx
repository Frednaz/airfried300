This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. The second pizza-bagel lane: more toppings, crispier edges, and a deliberately snacky after-school energy.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 8 mini bagels, split
- 1/2 cup pizza sauce
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 1/3 cup mini pepperoni
- 2 tbsp sliced olives
- 2 tbsp diced bell pepper
- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning

## Method

### 01. Build.
Spread each bagel half with sauce, then add cheese, pepperoni, olives, peppers, and seasoning.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 375°F for 5–7 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and the bagel edges crisp.

### 03. Rest briefly.
Let stand 2 minutes so the cheese does not take the roof of your mouth with it.

### 04. Repeat.
Cook in batches and keep finished bagels on a rack, not a plate.

## Notes

Use a light hand with sauce; too much makes the bagel soggy before the cheese melts.
