Shawarma is technically a slow-cooked rotating-spit-of-meat dish.
This isn't that. But it borrows shawarma's spice profile —
cumin, coriander, paprika, garlic, lemon — and applies it to a
quick traybake of squash and vegetarian sausages. The result
tastes Middle-Eastern in the way that matters (warm, garlicky,
slightly smoky from the paprika) without committing to the
all-day project of actual shawarma.

The spice rub is the heart of the dish: 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp
ground coriander, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp
garlic powder, a pinch of cinnamon, salt and pepper. Mix in a
bowl, toss with cubed squash and a few tablespoons of olive oil.
The sausages get rubbed in the same spice. Everything goes in the
air-fryer basket together for 20 minutes.

The yogurt sauce balances the warm spice: Greek yogurt, lemon
juice, grated cucumber (squeezed of moisture), garlic, mint, salt.
Stir together — it gets better as it sits. The lettuce wraps add
crunch and freshness.

## Ingredients

**For the spiced squash:**
- 1 medium butternut squash (about 2 lb / 900g), peeled and
  cubed (¾-inch)
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 vegetarian sausages

**For the yogurt sauce:**
- 1 cup (250g) Greek yogurt
- ½ cucumber, coarsely grated and squeezed dry
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp fresh mint, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp kosher salt

**To serve:**
- 4 flatbreads (pita, naan, or wraps)
- 4 leaves butterhead lettuce, washed
- Fresh thyme sprigs (optional)
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds (optional, for colour)

## Method

### 01. Mix the spice rub.
In a small bowl, combine cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric,
garlic powder, salt, cinnamon, and pepper.

### 02. Season the squash and sausages.
In a large bowl, toss the cubed squash and red onion wedges with
2 tbsp olive oil and most of the spice rub (save 1 tsp for the
sausages). Rub the sausages with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil
and the reserved spice mix.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Add the squash and onion
to the basket in a roughly even layer. Lay the sausages on top.
Cook 20–25 minutes, shaking the squash at the 12-minute mark.
The squash should be tender with caramelised edges; the sausages
deeply browned.

### 04. Make the yogurt sauce.
While the bake cooks, mix the yogurt with squeezed cucumber,
garlic, mint, lemon juice, and salt.

### 05. Warm the flatbreads.
Briefly warm the flatbreads in the air fryer for 2 minutes at
380°F at the end, or in a dry skillet for 30 seconds per side.

### 06. Assemble.
Place a flatbread on each plate. Top with squash and a sausage.
Add a lettuce leaf folded to one side, a generous spoonful of
yogurt sauce on the other. Scatter pomegranate seeds and thyme
if using.

### 07. Serve.
Squeeze a lemon wedge over the top and eat — folded like a
flatbread sandwich, or knife-and-fork.

## Notes

For a meat version, swap the veggie sausages for fresh lamb merguez
(same cooking time). For a fully vegan version, use coconut yogurt
in the sauce. The spice mix keeps in a jar for 2 months — make a
triple batch. The squash makes excellent lunchbox material the
next day, mixed with the yogurt sauce and a handful of grains.
