This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. The easiest frozen-corn-dog upgrade: crisp outside, hot middle, no oven preheat.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 4 frozen corn dogs
- Mustard and ketchup, for serving

## Method

### 01. Preheat.
Heat the air fryer to 370°F for 2 minutes.

### 02. Cook.
Place frozen corn dogs in the basket and cook 8–10 minutes, turning once.

### 03. Rest.
Let stand 1 minute; the filling gets extremely hot.

### 04. Serve.
Serve with mustard, ketchup, and probably napkins.

## Notes

Mini corn dogs cook faster, usually 5–6 minutes. No oil needed.
