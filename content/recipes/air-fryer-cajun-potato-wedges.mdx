This is a deliberate variant article: same photographic world, different cooking promise. A spicier potato wedge with paprika, cayenne, garlic, and a lemony finish for burgers, fish, or just dipping sauce.

The air fryer is good at these repeatable little shifts — a different seasoning, a different sauce, a different finish — because the core technique stays stable while the eating experience changes.

## Ingredients

- 2 large russet potatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Lemon wedges

## Method

### 01. Prep.
Cut the potatoes into even wedges and soak in cold water for 20 minutes if you have time. Dry very well.

### 02. Season.
Toss with oil and the seasonings until every cut side is lightly coated.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 400°F for 18–22 minutes, shaking every 7 minutes, until browned and crisp at the edges.

### 04. Finish.
Season while hot and serve immediately.

## Notes

If your Cajun seasoning is already salty, reduce the extra salt by half.
