The custard scroll is the bakery item Australians grew up on — soft
yeasted dough rolled with thick vanilla custard, sometimes finished
with icing or fruit. The passionfruit variant uses fresh passionfruit
pulp folded into the custard before rolling, giving the scrolls a
sharp tropical edge against the sweet custard base, and the seeds
caramelise on top as they bake.

The dough is enriched and lightly sweetened — close to brioche but
quicker. The custard is the standard pastry-cream technique: milk
and cream warmed, egg yolks and sugar whisked, combined and thickened
with cornflour. Cool fully before spreading or it makes the dough
soggy.

Roll, slice into 8 scrolls, proof briefly, air-fry at 180°C for
12 minutes. The bottoms go deep golden, the top edges blister, and
the passionfruit seeds on top caramelise into tiny pops of dark
sweetness.

the soft custardy spiral inside.

## Ingredients

**For the dough:**
- 2½ cups (375g) plain flour
- 2¼ tsp instant yeast
- 3 tbsp caster sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ cup (125ml) warm milk
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp melted butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**For the custard:**
- 1 cup (250ml) whole milk
- ¼ cup (60ml) double cream
- 3 egg yolks
- ⅓ cup (75g) caster sugar
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**For the passionfruit:**
- Pulp of 3 fresh passionfruit

**For finishing:**
- 1 tbsp melted butter (for brushing)
- 2 tbsp powdered sugar (icing sugar)

## Method

### 01. Mix the dough.
Combine flour, yeast, sugar, salt. Add warm milk, egg, melted butter,
vanilla. Knead 6 minutes until smooth. Proof in an oiled bowl 45
minutes.

### 02. Make the custard.
Warm milk and cream until just steaming. In a separate bowl, whisk
yolks, sugar, and cornflour. Pour warm milk over yolks while whisking.
Return to saucepan, cook on medium, stirring constantly, until thick
(3 minutes). Stir in vanilla. Cool completely.

### 03. Mix the passionfruit.
Stir the passionfruit pulp (seeds and all) into the cooled custard.
Save 2 tablespoons of seeded pulp for the top.

### 04. Roll out the dough.
Punch down the dough. Roll into a 12-inch x 9-inch rectangle.

### 05. Spread and roll.
Spread the passionfruit custard evenly across the dough, leaving a
½-inch border. Starting from the long edge, roll tightly into a log.

### 06. Slice.
Cut the log into 8 equal scrolls. Place cut-side-up on parchment.

### 07. Second proof.
Cover loosely, leave 20 minutes.

### 08. Air fry.
Brush tops with melted butter, spoon the reserved passionfruit pulp
over the top. Air fry at 360°F (180°C) for 12–14 minutes until
golden.

### 09. Finish.
Cool 10 minutes, dust with powdered sugar. Serve warm.

## Notes

Without fresh passionfruit: use 2 tbsp passionfruit pulp from a tin
or jar. For a glazed version: mix 1 cup powdered sugar with 2 tbsp
passionfruit pulp and drizzle over the cooled scrolls. The scrolls
keep at room temperature 24 hours. Refresh in the air fryer for 3
minutes at 350°F.
