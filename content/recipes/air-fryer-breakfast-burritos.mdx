The breakfast burrito is the great equalizer of American breakfast — a
gas-station meal and a brunch-spot meal at the same time, depending on
the wrapper. The version that matters is the one you make six of on a
Sunday morning, wrap individually in foil, and freeze. For the next two
weeks, every weekday morning, you take one out, run it under hot water
for ten seconds, drop it in the air fryer for five minutes, and you
have a breakfast that does not taste like it came out of a freezer.

The four-ingredient principle: scrambled eggs (soft, butter-cooked,
slightly under), breakfast sausage (or chorizo if you want it spicier),
crispy diced potatoes (the air fryer handles these — same temperature as
the burritos eventually go, just a different batch), and shredded
cheddar (or pepper jack). Everything else is optional. Salsa verde
folded inside, hot sauce drizzled on the eggs, chopped scallions, black
beans — go ahead. But the base four are the load-bearing wall.

The thing that separates this from a microwaved burrito at the office is
the air-frying step at the end. You don't have to do it — you can eat
the burrito as soon as it's rolled, warm and soft. But four minutes at
380°F gives the outside of the tortilla a crackling, slightly blistered
crust that is, structurally, what makes the burrito feel like a thing
and not just a wrap. The photo shows what's inside one. Hold it together
with both hands.

## Ingredients

**Crispy potatoes:**
- 2 medium russet potatoes, diced into ½-inch pieces (about 3 cups)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**Filling:**
- 8 oz breakfast sausage (or fresh chorizo)
- 6 large eggs
- 2 tbsp whole milk
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1½ cups shredded sharp cheddar

**To assemble:**
- 6 large flour tortillas (10-inch, soft taco size)
- Salsa verde or hot sauce, to serve
- Chopped scallions, to serve (optional)

## Method

### 01. Crisp the potatoes.
Toss the diced potatoes with the oil, salt, paprika, garlic powder, and
pepper. Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C) and cook for 14–16 minutes,
shaking the basket every 5 minutes, until deep golden and crisp at the
edges. Set aside.

### 02. Cook the sausage.
While the potatoes cook, brown the sausage in a skillet over medium
heat, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Drain off most of the fat
and set aside.

### 03. Scramble the eggs.
Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt, and pepper. Melt the butter in
the same skillet over medium-low heat. Add the eggs and cook slowly,
stirring often, until just set and still glossy — 3 to 4 minutes. Pull
them off the heat slightly underdone; they'll keep cooking.

### 04. Assemble.
Warm the tortillas (10 seconds in the microwave, or briefly over a gas
burner). Working with one at a time, layer down the center: ⅓ cup eggs,
2 tbsp sausage, ⅓ cup potatoes, ¼ cup cheese. Fold in both sides, then
roll tightly from the bottom, tucking as you go.

### 05. Air fry.
Wipe out the basket. Place burritos seam-side down, 2–3 at a time
depending on basket size. Spray or brush the tops lightly with olive
oil. Air fry at 380°F (193°C) for 4–5 minutes, flipping once, until
the outside is golden and crackling.

### 06. Serve.
Cut in half on a bias to show the layers. Eat warm, with salsa verde
or hot sauce on the side.

## Notes

**To meal-prep**: wrap unfried burritos individually in foil after
step 4 and freeze for up to a month. To reheat: unwrap, microwave for
60 seconds, then air fry at 380°F for 5 minutes (no thawing needed).
For chorizo, use 8 oz fresh Mexican chorizo and skip the seasoning on
the potatoes — the chorizo will do the work.
