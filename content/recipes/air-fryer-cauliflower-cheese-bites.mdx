These are the cauliflower equivalent of popcorn chicken — bite-sized
nuggets, breaded and crisp, with a soft cheesy interior. The
cauliflower is parboiled briefly so the inside is tender by the
time the breading crisps, then double-dredged in a cheese-rich
batter and panko for proper crunch.

The technique is to brine-blanch the cauliflower (2 minutes in
salted boiling water), pat very dry, then dip in a batter of beaten
egg with grated parmesan and cheddar mixed through, then press into
panko. Air-fry at 200°C for 10 minutes.

The photo shows the bites piled on a parchment-lined plate: small
golden nuggets with visible panko texture, a creamy garlic aioli
dipping sauce in the side bowl with a swirl of melted cheese on
top. The dish that wins over vegetable sceptics.

## Ingredients

**For the cauliflower:**
- 1 medium head cauliflower (1½ lb / 700g), cut into 1-inch florets
- 1 tbsp salt (for blanching)

**For the cheese batter:**
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- ½ cup (50g) grated parmesan
- ½ cup (55g) grated cheddar
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**For breading:**
- 1½ cups (130g) panko breadcrumbs
- Olive oil spray

**For the garlic aioli:**
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp finely sliced chives
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### 01. Blanch.
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add cauliflower florets,
cook 2 minutes — they should still be firm. Drain. Spread on a tea
towel to dry completely.

### 02. Mix the batter.
In a bowl, whisk beaten eggs with parmesan, cheddar, garlic powder,
paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper.

### 03. Dredge.
Dip each cauliflower floret in the batter, letting excess drip off,
then press firmly into panko.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray florets with oil. Cook
in a single layer 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until deeply
golden.

### 05. Make the aioli.
Whisk mayo with garlic, lemon, chives, and salt.

### 06. Serve.
Pile florets on a plate, scatter cracked pepper, serve with the
aioli alongside.

## Notes

Don't skip the blanching step — raw cauliflower stays crunchy and
the breading goes too brown by the time the inside cooks. For a
heat version: add ½ tsp cayenne to the breading. The aioli keeps
3 days. Best within 20 minutes of cooking.
