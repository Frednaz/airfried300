Tandoori chicken comes from a clay oven (the tandoor) where charcoal
heat reaches 480°C and chicken cooks in 8–10 minutes with proper
char. The home cook doesn't have a tandoor. The air fryer at 200°C
isn't a tandoor either, but it does produce the high circulating
heat needed for proper edge-charring on chicken — closer to the
real thing than an oven.

The technique is mostly about the marinade. Yogurt is the
non-negotiable base — its acid tenderises while its protein clings
to the chicken and chars beautifully. Add ginger, garlic, garam
masala, a tandoori masala blend (most supermarkets carry one), and
lemon juice. Overnight is best — 24 hours is the magic mark — but
4 hours minimum.

The colour of the finished chicken is the tandoori signature. Real
tandoori uses Kashmiri chilli powder or food colour for the deep
orange-red. We use a combination of smoked paprika + Kashmiri chilli
(or regular chilli) — natural colour without the additive.

## Ingredients

**For the tandoori marinade:**
- 8 chicken drumsticks (skin removed)
- 1 cup (250g) thick Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp tandoori masala (supermarket blend)
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 4 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 thumb fresh ginger, finely grated
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kashmiri chilli (or regular chilli powder)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp kosher salt

**To finish:**
- Olive oil spray
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Fresh coriander, chopped
- ¼ red onion, finely sliced

## Method

### 01. Make the marinade.
In a bowl, whisk yogurt with all spices, lemon, garlic, ginger, oil,
and salt.

### 02. Marinate.
Score the drumsticks with shallow cuts. Add to the marinade and
toss to coat thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours,
ideally overnight (24 hours).

### 03. Bring to room temperature.
Take the drumsticks out 30 minutes before cooking.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place drumsticks in a single
layer in the basket. Spray with oil. Cook 20–22 minutes, turning
once at the 12-minute mark, until the surfaces are charred and the
internal temperature reads 165°F (74°C).

### 05. Plate.
Move to a serving platter. Scatter coriander and red onion. Serve
with lemon wedges and any cooling accompaniments.

## Notes

For tandoori chicken breasts: pound to ½-inch thick, marinate
same way, cook 12 minutes at 400°F. For boneless thighs: 18 minutes.
For a yogurt-mint sauce on the side: ½ cup yogurt + ¼ cup chopped
mint + 1 tsp lemon juice + ½ tsp salt. Don't skip the marinade time
— the spices need time to penetrate. Serve with basmati rice, naan,
and cucumber raita.
