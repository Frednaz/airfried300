This is the kind of recipe that proves pantry cooking can be properly
satisfying. A tin of tuna, a tin of sweetcorn, a few mashed potatoes,
an egg, panko — all common store-cupboard items — combined into
small nuggets that taste much better than the sum of their parts.

The technique is straightforward. Mash the potatoes (use leftover
mash if you have it), fold in drained tuna and sweetcorn, add herbs,
season, bind with an egg. Shape into small oval nuggets, dredge in
panko, air-fry at 200°C until crisp.

These are excellent kid food but adults eat them too — they're
basically a portion-controlled fishcake. Serve with sweet chilli
sauce + mayo (the Australian sweet chilli-and-mayo combo) or
tartare sauce for adults.

The photo shows the nuggets piled on a white oval plate: golden-
brown oblong bites with one cut open showing the white tuna mixture
and yellow corn kernels inside, a ramekin of sweet chilli and mayo
swirled together on the side. The kind of weeknight dinner you can
make from what's already in the kitchen.

## Ingredients

**For the nuggets:**
- 1 lb (450g) mashed potatoes (about 2 medium potatoes, boiled and
  mashed)
- 2 (5 oz / 142g) cans tuna in spring water, drained
- 1 cup (160g) tinned sweetcorn, drained
- 1 large egg, beaten
- ½ cup (50g) grated cheddar
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**For breading:**
- ½ cup plain flour
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1¼ cups (130g) panko breadcrumbs
- Olive oil spray

**To serve:**
- 3 tbsp sweet chilli sauce mixed with 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- Lemon wedges

## Method

### 01. Mix.
In a bowl, combine mashed potatoes, drained tuna, sweetcorn, egg,
cheese, spring onions, parsley, Dijon, salt, and pepper.

### 02. Shape.
Form into 16 small oval nuggets, about 2 inches long.

### 03. Chill briefly.
Refrigerate 15 minutes — they firm up and won't fall apart during
breading.

### 04. Bread.
Set up flour, beaten egg, and panko in three bowls. Dredge each
nugget in flour, then egg, then panko.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray nuggets with olive oil.
Cook in a single layer 10–12 minutes, flipping at the 6-minute
mark, until deeply golden.

### 06. Serve.
Plate with sweet chilli-mayo dipping sauce and lemon wedges.

## Notes

For salmon nuggets: swap tuna for tinned pink salmon (drained).
**Freezer-friendly**: freeze breaded nuggets on a tray, then bag.
Cook from frozen at 400°F for 14 minutes. For a gluten-free
version: swap panko for crushed cornflakes or gluten-free panko.
The nuggets keep 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the air fryer for
3 minutes at 380°F.
