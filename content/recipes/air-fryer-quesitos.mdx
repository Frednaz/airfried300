Quesitos — literally "little cheeses" — are the Puerto Rican
bakery-counter pastry: rectangular strips of puff pastry rolled
around a sweet cream cheese filling, baked or fried until the
exterior crisps and the inside stays creamy. Served at every San
Juan bakery alongside strong coffee.

The technique is straightforward. Mix softened cream cheese with
sugar, vanilla, and a touch of egg yolk for richness. Cut puff
pastry into rectangles, spoon filling along one edge, roll up,
seal with beaten egg. Air-fry at 380°F for 10 minutes. Drizzle
warm honey across the top, dust with cinnamon sugar.

The photo shows the quesitos on a marble surface: small golden-brown
rolled pastries with visible cream cheese peeking out the ends,
honey drizzled in glossy lines across the tops, powdered sugar
dusted. The kind of breakfast pastry that justifies the early
alarm.

## Ingredients

**For the filling:**
- 8 oz (225g) cream cheese, softened
- ⅓ cup (75g) caster sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ tsp lemon zest
- Pinch of salt

**For the pastries:**
- 2 sheets ready-rolled puff pastry, thawed
- 1 large egg, beaten (for sealing and brushing)

**For finishing:**
- 3 tbsp honey, warmed
- 2 tbsp caster sugar mixed with ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp powdered sugar, for dusting

## Method

### 01. Make the filling.
Beat softened cream cheese with sugar until smooth. Add egg yolk,
vanilla, lemon zest, and salt. Beat until fluffy.

### 02. Cut the pastry.
Cut each puff pastry sheet into 4 equal rectangles (8 total).

### 03. Fill and roll.
Spoon 1½ tablespoons of filling along one short edge of each
rectangle, leaving a small border. Brush the opposite edge with
beaten egg. Roll up from the filled edge, sealing on the egg-washed
side.

### 04. Egg wash.
Place rolled pastries seam-side-down on parchment. Brush tops with
egg.

### 05. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Cook on parchment in a single
layer 10–12 minutes, until deeply golden and puffed.

### 06. Finish.
While still warm, drizzle each pastry with warm honey, then dust
with cinnamon sugar. Finish with powdered sugar.

### 07. Serve.
Eat warm with strong coffee.

## Notes

For a guava version (also classic): pipe a small line of guava paste
alongside the cream cheese before rolling. Best within 4 hours of
baking. The cream cheese filling should be very smooth — lumpy
filling distributes unevenly. Don't overstuff — too much filling
leaks out at the ends during cooking.
