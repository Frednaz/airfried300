## Ingredients

- 4 ears corn
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 garlic clove
- Parsley
- Salt

## Method

### 01. Prep.
Pat the main ingredients dry and cut them evenly. Preheat the air fryer if your model runs better hot.

### 02. Season.
Toss with oil, salt, and the seasonings until lightly coated. For breaded recipes, press the coating on firmly and mist with cooking spray.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook in a single layer at 390°F, shaking or turning once, until browned at the edges and cooked through.

### 04. Finish.
Add sticky sauces, cheese, fresh herbs, citrus, or crunchy toppings after cooking while everything is still hot. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Cut ears in half if needed to fit the basket.
