This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. A plain, useful asparagus recipe: blistered tips, tender stalks, lemon at the end, and no sheet pan required.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 lemon wedge
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan, optional

## Method

### 01. Trim.
Snap or cut away the woody ends and pat the stalks dry.

### 02. Season.
Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange mostly in one layer.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 6–8 minutes, shaking once, until the tips darken and the stalks bend easily.

### 04. Finish.
Squeeze lemon over the top and add parmesan if you want the dinner-party version.

## Notes

Thin asparagus may finish in 5 minutes; thick stalks can take 9. Start checking early.
