The honest case for the air fryer is in the dishes where the alternative
would be a hot pan, a splatter screen, and a stove top that needs wiping
down afterward. Shrimp is exactly that dish. Sautéed shrimp is great,
but it's a five-minute operation that demands your full attention and
produces a kitchen that smells like seafood for the rest of the evening.
This is the opposite. You toss everything in a bowl, dump it in the
basket, set a timer, and walk away to wash the rice cooker.

The vegetables here are the structural part. Red bell pepper holds its
shape and adds sweetness; yellow onion melts down to something
caramelized at the edges. Together they're the cushion that catches all
the spice — chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic, a tap of cayenne if
you want a little burn. The shrimp pick up that spice and don't
overcook because the air fryer is fast enough to pull them out before
they go from snappy to rubbery.

Lime at the end is non-negotiable. The whole thing is built around that
final squeeze. Cilantro if you have it, parsley if you don't. Brown rice
underneath because the oils need somewhere to go and a tortilla isn't
big enough.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb large shrimp (16/20 count), peeled, deveined, tail-off
- 1 red bell pepper, cored and cut into 1-inch pieces
- ½ medium yellow onion, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 cups cooked brown rice, to serve

## Method

### 01. Dry the shrimp.
Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels — if they go in wet, they steam
instead of getting that slightly caramelized crust.

### 02. Toss.
In a medium bowl, combine the shrimp, pepper, and onion. Drizzle with
the olive oil, sprinkle on the chili powder, paprika, garlic powder,
cayenne (if using), salt, and pepper. Toss until everything is evenly
coated.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Tip everything into the basket
in a single layer — some overlap is fine, it'll settle. Cook 6–7
minutes, shaking the basket halfway through. The shrimp should be
opaque and just curled.

### 04. Plate.
Spoon the shrimp and peppers over warm brown rice. Squeeze a lime wedge
over the top, scatter the cilantro, and serve immediately with the
remaining lime wedges on the side.

## Notes

Frozen shrimp works — thaw them quickly under cold running water for 5
minutes, then pat very dry. If you can't find peeled shrimp, you can
peel them yourself in about 4 minutes (save the shells in the freezer
for stock). For a lower-carb version, serve over cauliflower rice or
shredded romaine instead of brown rice — it doesn't hold the spice as
well but it'll do.
