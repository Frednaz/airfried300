This is the side dish I make when something else is taking all my
attention. Boil pasta? Air-fry asparagus while it cooks. Roast a
chicken? Asparagus in the basket for the last 7 minutes of the
chicken's rest. Steak in a pan? Asparagus in the air fryer parallel
to the pan. The whole recipe is twelve minutes — most of them
unattended — and produces something that looks like restaurant
plating with about a 90-second active commitment.

The technique is the simplest in this entire collection. Trim the
woody ends. Toss with olive oil, lemon zest, minced garlic, salt,
and grated parmesan. Air fry at 400°F for 6 to 8 minutes,
depending on thickness. That's the whole recipe. The parmesan
melts onto the surface of the spears and bakes into a thin golden
crust at the tips; the lemon zest oils up and perfumes everything;
the garlic mellows.

A note on the parmesan: real Parmigiano-Reggiano makes a meaningful
difference here. The pre-grated stuff sold in a plastic shaker
won't melt the same way — it's processed with anti-caking agents.
Buy a wedge, grate it yourself, use what you need, wrap and
refrigerate the rest. It keeps for months.

For thin asparagus (pencil-thickness), reduce the time to 5
minutes. For thick asparagus (the size of your thumb), 9 minutes
and check for tenderness with a knife tip.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450g) fresh asparagus, woody ends snapped off
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp lemon zest (from 1 lemon)
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper, freshly cracked
- ⅓ cup (35g) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, divided
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (to finish)
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (to finish)
- Lemon wedges, to serve

## Method

### 01. Trim the asparagus.
Hold each spear with both hands and snap the bottom — it'll break
where the woody part ends. Discard the woody ends.

### 02. Season.
In a wide bowl, toss the asparagus with olive oil, lemon zest,
garlic, salt, and pepper. Add half the parmesan (about ¼ cup) and
toss again until each spear is lightly coated.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the spears in a
single layer in the basket — they can overlap slightly. Cook 6–8
minutes, shaking the basket once halfway, until the tips are
just-charred and the thickest part is tender when pierced with a
knife tip.

### 04. Finish.
Tip onto a serving platter. While hot, sprinkle the remaining
parmesan over the top — it melts immediately. Drizzle with lemon
juice, scatter parsley.

### 05. Serve.
Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side. Best within 10
minutes of cooking.

## Notes

For a richer version, finish with a tablespoon of melted butter
poured over just before the second parmesan dose. For an asparagus-
and-prosciutto pairing, lay 4 slices of prosciutto on top of the
finished asparagus — the residual heat warms the prosciutto just
enough. Don't try to make this with frozen asparagus — the texture
goes mushy. Fresh, in season (spring), is the only way this dish
makes sense.
