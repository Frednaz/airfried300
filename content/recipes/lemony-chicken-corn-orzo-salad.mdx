Orzo is the cheat-code pasta. Looks like rice, behaves like pasta,
cooks in nine minutes, soaks up flavour like couscous, holds at room
temperature without going gummy. For salads, it's structurally superior
to any other shape: small enough that every forkful has a balanced ratio
of grain to extras, but big enough to retain bite when chilled.

This is high-summer cooking. The corn is fresh, kernels cut off raw
cobs and tossed straight in (or charred briefly in a dry skillet —
your call). The chicken is leftover or grilled. The feta is crumbled.
The basil is torn (not chopped — basil bruises). The lemon is heavy.
Olive oil generous. Salt judicious. This is the dish you make when the
farmers' market is overwhelming you and you've come home with too much
of everything.

The photo shows the bowl, top-down: orzo evenly distributed with
flecks of green (basil, parsley) and yellow (corn kernels), white
crumbles of feta, golden chicken pieces folded through. Lemon halves
on the side for the squeeze that happens immediately before serving.
This is the dish that goes from the kitchen to a deck table to a
plate to a stomach in about fifteen minutes flat.

## Ingredients

**For the salad:**
- 1½ cups dry orzo
- 2 ears fresh corn (or 1½ cups thawed frozen corn)
- 2 cups cooked, diced chicken (grilled or rotisserie)
- 4 oz feta, crumbled (about 1 cup)
- ½ cup fresh basil, torn
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- ¼ cup red onion, finely minced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**For the dressing:**
- ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- ½ tsp Dijon mustard
- ½ tsp kosher salt

## Method

### 01. Cook the orzo.
Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the orzo for 8 minutes
(2 minutes less than the package says — it firms up as it cools).
Drain and rinse briefly under cold water to stop the cooking.

### 02. Cut the corn.
If using fresh corn, slice the kernels off the cob with a sharp knife
held vertically (stand the cob on its flat end). For an extra layer:
char the kernels in a dry skillet over high heat for 3 minutes,
shaking the pan, until some are blackened.

### 03. Whisk the dressing.
In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice and zest,
vinegar, honey, Dijon, and salt.

### 04. Combine.
In a large bowl, combine the drained orzo, corn, chicken, red onion,
parsley, basil, salt, and pepper. Pour the dressing over and toss.
Add the feta last, fold gently so it doesn't disintegrate.

### 05. Rest and serve.
Let sit 10 minutes for flavours to meld. Serve at room temperature or
slightly chilled. Right before serving, taste — add more lemon or
salt if it's flat.

## Notes

Keeps 4 days refrigerated. For a heartier version, add halved cherry
tomatoes, sliced cucumber, kalamata olives. For a vegetarian version,
skip the chicken and add chickpeas (drained, rinsed). The dressing
works on grain bowls too — make a double batch. If serving for a
crowd, hold the feta and basil until just before serving so they
stay vibrant.
