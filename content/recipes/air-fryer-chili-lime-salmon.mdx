This is a deliberate variant article: same photographic world, different cooking promise. A bright salmon variant with chili powder, lime, and a little honey for heat and shine.

The air fryer is good at these repeatable little shifts — a different seasoning, a different sauce, a different finish — because the core technique stays stable while the eating experience changes.

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp honey
- Cilantro

## Method

### 01. Season.
Rub salmon with oil, chili powder, cumin, salt, lime zest, and honey.

### 02. Air fry.
Cook at 390°F for 7–9 minutes, until just flaky.

### 03. Lime.
Squeeze lime over the hot fish.

### 04. Serve.
Scatter cilantro and serve with rice or tortillas.

## Notes

Salmon is best slightly under rather than dry; start checking at 7 minutes.
