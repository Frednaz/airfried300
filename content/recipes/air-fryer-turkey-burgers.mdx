This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Juicy turkey burgers with garlic, Dijon, and a little mayo in the mix so they do not dry out.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 lb ground turkey
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 4 buns
- Lettuce, tomato, pickles

## Method

### 01. Mix.
Combine turkey, mayo, Dijon, garlic powder, salt, and pepper gently.

### 02. Shape.
Form into 4 patties and press a shallow dimple in the center.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 375°F for 10–12 minutes, flipping once, until 165°F inside.

### 04. Build.
Rest 3 minutes, then serve on buns with toppings.

## Notes

Ground turkey needs seasoning and fat. The spoonful of mayo disappears into the burger but keeps it tender.
