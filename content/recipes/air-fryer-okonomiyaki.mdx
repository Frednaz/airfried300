Okonomiyaki is Japan's "as you like it" pancake — Osaka street food
made from a flour-and-egg batter studded with shredded cabbage and
whatever extras the cook fancies (pork belly, prawns, octopus,
cheese, kimchi). It's pan-fried on a flat-top griddle, finished
with a sweet-savoury okonomiyaki sauce (similar to Worcestershire),
zigzag of Kewpie mayonnaise, bonito flakes that dance in the heat,
and pickled ginger.

This air-fryer version makes small round individual bites instead
of one large pancake — easier to plate, faster to cook, perfect for
sharing. The batter is the standard cabbage-heavy ratio (about
60% cabbage by weight). Spoon into small rounds on parchment,
air-fry at 200°C for 12 minutes.

The photo shows the assembled bites on a grey ceramic plate:
golden-brown patties with dark sauce zigzags, white Kewpie mayo
zigzag crossing it, pink pickled ginger curls and bright green
spring onion sliced on top, sesame seeds scattered.

## Ingredients

**For the batter:**
- ¾ cup (110g) plain flour
- ¼ cup (35g) cornflour
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp dashi powder (optional but authentic)
- ⅔ cup (160ml) cold water
- 3 large eggs

**For the okonomiyaki:**
- 3 cups (250g) finely shredded green cabbage
- 4 spring onions, finely sliced (whites in batter, greens to garnish)
- ¼ cup pickled ginger, finely chopped (for batter)
- Olive oil spray

**For the sauce:**
- 3 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or extra Worcestershire for vegetarian)
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp soy sauce

**To finish:**
- Kewpie mayonnaise (in a squeeze bottle)
- Bonito flakes (katsuobushi) — optional, skip for vegetarian
- Pickled ginger
- Sesame seeds

## Method

### 01. Mix the batter.
Whisk flour, cornflour, baking powder, salt, dashi (if using),
water, and eggs until smooth.

### 02. Fold in vegetables.
Add shredded cabbage, white parts of spring onions, and pickled
ginger. Mix until cabbage is coated.

### 03. Shape.
Cut a square of parchment to fit the basket. Scoop ¼ cup mounds
onto parchment, spread to ½-inch thick discs.

### 04. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray with oil. Cook 12–14
minutes, flipping at the 7-minute mark, until deeply golden.

### 05. Make the sauce.
Whisk all sauce ingredients in a small bowl.

### 06. Assemble.
Plate the pancakes. Drizzle okonomiyaki sauce in zigzags, then
Kewpie mayo crossways. Top with bonito flakes (they'll wave in the
steam), pickled ginger, sesame seeds, and spring onion greens.

### 07. Serve.
Eat immediately.

## Notes

Real okonomiyaki uses yamaimo (Japanese mountain yam) in the batter
for fluffiness — most Western kitchens don't have it. The cornflour
substitute approximates the texture. For a pork version: scatter
4 strips of streaky bacon over the top of each disc before air-frying.
For prawn: stir 8 oz peeled raw prawns into the batter.
