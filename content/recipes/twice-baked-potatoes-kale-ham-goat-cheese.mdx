The twice-baked potato is a small architectural masterpiece. A
baking potato becomes a vessel for its own contents — scooped,
mashed, mixed with anything in the fridge, repacked, baked again
until the top blisters. It's substantial enough to be dinner,
elegant enough to be served at a table without apology, and uses
ingredients that are usually already in the kitchen.

The filling here is greens-and-cheese-leaning: sautéed kale (the
slight bitterness cuts through the cheese), smoked ham (small
dice, for salt and chew), goat's cheese (the tangy crumbly kind,
not the soft spreadable kind), spring onions, chives. Plus the
mashed potato itself, with butter and a splash of double cream.

Air-frying the initial bake takes about 35 minutes for a medium
russet at 400°F (vs 60+ in the oven) — a real time savings. The
second bake (the "twice" part) is 8 minutes to crisp the tops.

## Ingredients

**For the potatoes:**
- 4 large baking potatoes (about 12 oz / 340g each)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt

**For the filling:**
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 cups (about 80g) curly kale or cavolo nero, stems removed,
  chopped
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- ⅓ cup (80ml) double cream (heavy cream)
- 4 oz (110g) cooked smoked ham, small-diced
- 4 oz (110g) crumbly goat's cheese, divided
- 3 spring onions, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh chives, snipped
- ¼ tsp grated nutmeg
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**To serve:**
- 8 oz (225g) tenderstem broccoli or purple sprouting kale,
  steamed
- Additional fresh chives
- Extra goat's cheese to crumble on top

## Method

### 01. First bake.
Scrub the potatoes, prick each several times with a fork, rub
with olive oil and salt. Place in the air fryer at 400°F
(200°C) and cook 35–45 minutes, until a knife slides easily
through the centre.

### 02. Sauté the kale.
While the potatoes bake, melt 1 tbsp butter in a skillet over
medium heat. Add the chopped kale and garlic. Cook 3 minutes,
stirring, until wilted and tender. Tip onto a plate to cool.

### 03. Scoop and mash.
When the potatoes are done, halve each lengthwise. Scoop out
the flesh into a large bowl, leaving a ¼-inch wall of potato
inside the skin to maintain structure. Mash the flesh smooth
with the remaining 2 tbsp butter and the double cream.

### 04. Mix the filling.
Fold the sautéed kale, diced ham, half the goat's cheese, spring
onions, chives, nutmeg, salt, and pepper into the mashed
potato. Taste and adjust seasoning.

### 05. Restuff.
Pile the filling back into the potato skins, mounding it
slightly. Top with the remaining goat's cheese, crumbled.

### 06. Second bake.
Return the stuffed potatoes to the air fryer at 380°F (193°C)
for 8–10 minutes, until the tops are deeply browned and the
cheese has gone melty-crisp at the edges.

### 07. Plate.
Serve 2 potato halves per person with a pile of steamed
tenderstem broccoli. Scatter fresh chives.

## Notes

**To make ahead**: through step 5, refrigerate up to 24 hours.
Add 4 minutes to the second bake to compensate for cold start.
For a vegetarian version, swap the ham for chopped sun-dried
tomatoes or a handful of caramelised onions. For a more
indulgent version, fold in 4 oz of grated cheddar with the goat's
cheese. The leftover filling (if any) is excellent as the next
day's lunch, fried in a buttered skillet into a hash. Don't skip
the first-bake rest — hot scooped potato breaks the filling into
mush; let it cool to warm before mashing.
