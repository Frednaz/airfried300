This is the chicken-thigh recipe I keep going back to. Bone-in
thighs are forgiving (you can't really overcook them), the
seasoning is a one-bowl job (Italian seasoning, garlic, salt,
pepper, olive oil), and the whole thing takes under thirty minutes
including a quick caesar salad on the side. The air fryer does the
work; you don't have to think about it.

What makes this dish punch above its weight is the contrast. The
chicken comes out with a dark, almost-burnt-looking crust on the
skin side — the Italian herbs caramelise in the dry heat, the garlic
powder takes on a slight nutty colour, the natural sugars in the
chicken skin go properly brown. Underneath, the meat is meltingly
tender. The caesar salad — crisp romaine, parmesan, a sharp dressing —
cuts through the richness and turns "chicken on a plate" into actual
dinner.

The photo shows the assembled plate: a pile of two bone-in thighs
with the skin gone deep mahogany, herb specks visible across the
surface, sitting on a pink ceramic plate next to a generous handful

needed. No starchy side. This is what dinner looks like on a

also want to be eating in twenty minutes.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken:**
- 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 12 oz / 340g total)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp lemon zest (optional)

**For the quick caesar:**
- 4 cups (4 oz / 110g) romaine lettuce, chopped
- 3 tbsp caesar dressing (store-bought is fine)
- ¼ cup (25g) shaved parmesan
- 2 tbsp croutons (optional)
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method

### 01. Dry the thighs.
Pat the chicken thighs very dry with paper towels. Wet skin
doesn't crisp.

### 02. Season.
In a small bowl, mix the Italian seasoning, garlic powder, paprika,
salt, pepper, and lemon zest (if using). Rub each thigh with olive
oil, then sprinkle the seasoning all over, pressing it into the
skin and underneath.

### 03. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Place the thighs skin-side-up
in the basket with space between them. Cook 22–25 minutes,
without turning, until the internal temperature reaches 175°F
(80°C) at the thickest part and the skin is deeply burnished.

### 04. Make the caesar.
While the chicken cooks, toss the chopped romaine with caesar
dressing in a bowl. Add the parmesan and croutons (if using).
Toss again. Cracked pepper on top.

### 05. Rest and plate.
Move the chicken to a plate and rest 5 minutes. Add a pile of
caesar salad to one side. Eat with a knife and fork (or hands —
no judgement).

## Notes

For a salad-as-the-meal version, shred the cooked chicken and toss
it into the caesar — chicken caesar bowl. For boneless thighs,
reduce the cook time to 16 minutes (still 175°F internal). The
Italian herb rub also works on chicken breasts (15 minutes at
400°F), chicken wings (18 minutes), and pork chops (8 minutes).
For a heartier caesar, add chopped hard-boiled egg, anchovies, and
quartered cherry tomatoes.
