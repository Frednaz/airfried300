This is the steak dinner you'd order at a gastropub on a Friday
night — a thick steak sliced into medallions, served on creamy mash
with tenderstem broccoli and a red-wine-and-shallot reduction. The
plating is the whole game. The cooking itself is straightforward.

The air-fryer steak technique: rest the steak at room temperature
30 minutes, season heavily with salt and pepper, air-fry at 200°C
for 8–10 minutes for a 1¼-inch thick steak (medium-rare). Rest 5
minutes before slicing. Slice across the grain into ¾-inch
medallions for the plate-appeal arrangement.

The jus is made in 5 minutes in a saucepan while the steak rests:
finely chopped shallot softened in butter, ½ cup red wine, ½ cup
beef stock, simmered to a glossy reduction.

The photo shows the assembled plate: sliced steak medallions fanned
across mash, glossy dark jus poured down the slices and pooling on
the mash, tenderstem broccoli arranged to one side, the kind of
plating that says "I'm taking this seriously."

## Ingredients

**For the steak:**
- 2 thick steaks (sirloin, rump, or ribeye, 1¼-inch thick, 8 oz
  each)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp coarse black pepper

**For the red-wine jus:**
- 2 tbsp butter, divided
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 sprig fresh thyme
- ½ cup (120ml) red wine (any dry red)
- ½ cup (120ml) beef stock
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp redcurrant jelly or honey
- Salt and pepper

**For the mash:**
- 1½ lb (700g) Maris Piper or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 3 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup (60ml) double cream
- Salt and white pepper

**To serve:**
- 8 oz tenderstem broccoli, steamed
- Flaky sea salt
- Cracked black pepper

## Method

### 01. Rest the steaks.
Take steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Pat very
dry. Rub with olive oil, salt, and pepper.

### 02. Boil the potatoes.
Cover cubed potatoes with salted water, boil 15 minutes until very
tender. Drain.

### 03. Make the mash.
Mash potatoes smooth with butter, cream, salt, and pepper. Keep
warm.

### 04. Air fry the steaks.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Cook steaks 6–8 minutes for
medium-rare (130°F / 54°C internal). Rest on a board 5 minutes.

### 05. Make the jus.
While steaks rest, melt 1 tbsp butter in a small saucepan. Add
shallot, cook 3 minutes. Add garlic and thyme, cook 30 seconds.
Pour in wine, simmer hard 2 minutes to reduce by half. Add stock,
mustard, and jelly. Simmer 4 minutes until glossy and slightly
syrupy. Off heat, swirl in remaining 1 tbsp butter. Strain if you
want it smooth.

### 06. Slice the steaks.
Cut across the grain into ¾-inch medallions.

### 07. Plate.
Spoon a generous mound of mash onto each plate. Arrange steak
medallions across the mash. Pour the jus generously over the
slices. Add tenderstem broccoli to the side. Finish with flaky
salt and pepper.

## Notes

For a smaller jus reduction: use 2 tbsp red wine vinegar + ½ cup
stock if you don't want to open a bottle. Without redcurrant jelly:
use 1 tsp honey or maple syrup. The mash should be smoother than
typical chunky mash for this dish — use a potato ricer for the
silkiest result. Don't skip the steak rest — the slices won't lose
juice if rested.
