The burrito is one of those dishes where the platonic ideal lives
in your memory of one specific late-night order at one specific
takeaway, against which all subsequent burritos are unfairly
judged. The qualities to chase: dense filling that doesn't fall
out, tortilla pan-toasted to crisp on the outside while staying
pliable on the inside, balance between protein, beans, vegetable,
fat, acid.

This version uses shredded chicken in a chipotle-honey glaze —
slightly sweet, mostly smoky, deeply colour-saturated. Pre-cooked
chicken works (a rotisserie chicken from the supermarket is the
fastest path), or poach two breasts in salted water for 15
minutes and shred. The glaze takes 5 minutes to make and acts as
both sauce and binder.

The red cabbage slaw is the textural counterpoint — crunchy,
acidic, slightly bitter — that prevents the burrito from going
one-note rich. Quick-pickled in lime juice with a pinch of salt;
the colour goes from dark purple to vivid magenta within 10
minutes.

The pan-toasting at the end is non-negotiable. Folded burrito,
seam-side-down in a hot dry skillet for 90 seconds, flip, 90
seconds more. The outside turns golden-spotted, the inside heats
through, the cheese melts. This is the difference between a
burrito and a wrap.

## Ingredients

**For the chipotle chicken:**
- 1½ lb (700g) cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie or poached)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, finely chopped (plus 1 tbsp
  adobo sauce)
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce (or 8 oz tomato passata)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 lime, juiced

**For the red cabbage slaw:**
- 3 cups (250g) red cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp honey
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

**For the lime crema:**
- 1 cup (240ml) soured cream (or Greek yogurt)
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- ½ tsp kosher salt

**To assemble (per burrito):**
- 1 large flour tortilla (10-inch)
- ⅓ cup refried black beans (or any refried beans), warmed
- ¼ cup shredded cheese (Monterey Jack or cheddar)
- ¼ avocado, sliced
- 2 tbsp crispy fried shallots (optional)

**To pan-toast:**
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

## Method

### 01. Make the slaw.
Toss the shredded cabbage with lime juice, salt, and honey.
Set aside for at least 10 minutes — it'll soften and turn bright
magenta. Stir in coriander before serving.

### 02. Make the lime crema.
Whisk soured cream with lime zest, lime juice, garlic, coriander,
and salt. Refrigerate until serving.

### 03. Cook the chipotle chicken.
Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add chopped onion,
cook 5 minutes. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds. Stir in chipotles,
adobo sauce, honey, tomato sauce, cumin, paprika, and salt.
Simmer 3 minutes. Add the shredded chicken, toss to coat, cook
3 more minutes until heated through and well-glazed. Finish
with lime juice.

### 04. Warm the tortillas.
Briefly warm the tortillas (10 seconds in the microwave under a
damp paper towel, or 30 seconds in a dry skillet) — they should
be pliable enough to roll without cracking.

### 05. Assemble.
Lay a tortilla flat. Down the centre: 2 tbsp refried beans,
spread into a line. Top with ¾ cup chipotle chicken, ¼ cup
cheese, slaw (a generous handful), avocado slices, crispy
shallots if using. Fold in both sides of the tortilla, then
roll tightly from the bottom up.

### 06. Pan-toast.
Heat a large dry skillet over medium-high. Add a film of oil
or butter. Place burritos seam-side-down, 2 at a time. Cook
90 seconds per side, until the tortilla is golden-spotted and
the burrito is heated through.

### 07. Serve.
Cut each burrito in half on a sharp bias to show the cross-
section. Plate with a small dish of lime crema for dipping.

## Notes

The chipotle chicken keeps 4 days refrigerated, freezes 3
months — make a double batch for meal prep. For a vegan
version, swap the chicken for jackfruit (drained canned, shredded)
or black beans + corn. For more heat, add an extra chipotle.
Without chipotle in adobo, use 2 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp
chilli powder + 1 tsp brown sugar + 1 tbsp tomato paste — close
enough. To make ahead: assemble through step 5, refrigerate up
to 24 hours, pan-toast just before serving (add 60 seconds to
the toast time to compensate for cold start).
