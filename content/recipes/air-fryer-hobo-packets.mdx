This is a volume-friendly air-fryer article in the best sense: familiar food, a real photo, and a slightly different lane from the version already in the archive. Foil packets of potatoes, sausage, peppers, and onions cooked in the air fryer until steamy and browned at the edges.

The basket does the useful work here — concentrated heat, browned edges, and less oil than the stovetop version. Keep the pieces even, shake once or twice, and let the last two minutes decide the colour.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz smoked sausage, sliced
- 1 lb baby potatoes, thinly sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp seasoned salt
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp butter

## Method

### 01. Build packets.
Toss sausage, potatoes, pepper, onion, oil, and seasonings. Divide among foil sheets and top each with butter.

### 02. Seal.
Fold into tight packets, leaving a little room for steam.

### 03. Air fry.
Cook at 380°F for 22–25 minutes, turning once, until potatoes are tender.

### 04. Brown optional.
Open packets and cook 3 minutes more if you want browned edges.

## Notes

Use heavy-duty foil and check your air fryer manual for foil guidance. Keep foil away from the heating element.
