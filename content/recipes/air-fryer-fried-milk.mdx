Leche frita — "fried milk" — is one of Spain's quiet desserts. Milk
thickened with cornflour and sugar into a pourable custard, chilled
overnight into a sliceable slab, breaded, fried, dusted in cinnamon
sugar. The exterior is crisp; the interior is a molten panna-cotta-
adjacent texture that pours out the moment you cut through.

The technique requires chilling time (overnight is best, 3 hours
minimum) but the active work is twenty minutes. Cornflour and milk
cook into a very thick custard; pour into a square tin, chill to
set, slice into squares, dredge in flour-egg-panko, air-fry at 200°C
for 6 minutes per side until golden.

The photo shows the assembled cubes on a grey ceramic plate: pale
golden squares with caramel-brown tops, one bitten through showing
the white custard oozing out, dusted with cinnamon sugar. The dessert
that makes guests ask "how?"

## Ingredients

**For the milk slab:**
- 2½ cups (600ml) whole milk
- 1 cinnamon stick
- Peel of 1 lemon (no pith)
- ⅔ cup (150g) caster sugar
- ½ cup (60g) cornflour (cornstarch)
- 3 egg yolks
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**For breading and frying:**
- ½ cup plain flour
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1¼ cups (130g) panko breadcrumbs
- Olive oil spray

**For dusting:**
- ⅓ cup (75g) caster sugar
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon

## Method

### 01. Infuse the milk.
Warm milk with cinnamon stick and lemon peel in a saucepan over
medium-low for 5 minutes. Remove from heat, fish out cinnamon and
peel.

### 02. Thicken.
Whisk sugar, cornflour, and egg yolks together. Pour warm milk over
while whisking. Return to the pan, cook on medium, whisking constantly,
until very thick (4–5 minutes). Stir in butter and vanilla.

### 03. Chill.
Pour into a parchment-lined 8-inch square tin. Smooth the top.
Refrigerate at least 3 hours (preferably overnight).

### 04. Slice.
Turn out onto a board. Cut into 24 small squares (about 1½-inch).

### 05. Bread.
Set up flour, beaten egg, and panko. Coat each square in flour, egg,
then panko, pressing firmly.

### 06. Air fry.
Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Spray squares with oil. Cook in
a single layer 6–7 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden.

### 07. Dust and serve.
Mix sugar and cinnamon. Roll each warm square in the mix. Serve
immediately.

## Notes

The cornflour-to-milk ratio is critical — too little and the custard
won't slice; too much and it's rubbery. This ratio works. Don't slice
cold custard with a warm knife. Best within 30 minutes of frying.
